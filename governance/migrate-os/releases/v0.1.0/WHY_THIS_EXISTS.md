# Why MIGRATE-OS Exists

IKR-POS made projects governable and portable at the artifact and authority level, but it intentionally did not perform application rewrites, schema translation, data transformation, security reconstruction, feature parity or cutover.

MIGRATE-OS fills that technical execution gap while preserving IKR-POS governance. It is designed to prevent repeated ad hoc Base44 exits and to make future application, database, authentication, storage and hosting moves repeatable, testable and rollback-safe.
