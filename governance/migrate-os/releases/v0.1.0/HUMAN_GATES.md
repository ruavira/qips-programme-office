# MIGRATE-OS Human Gates

The release may prepare evidence and execute reversible technical steps, but it must not autonomously claim or perform these decisions without explicit authorization:

1. Target architecture and vendor commitment
2. Privacy, residency, retention and regulatory approval
3. Production organizational and financial configuration
4. Real-data migration authorization
5. User-account migration and external invitations
6. Human browser and operational acceptance
7. Domain/DNS or traffic cutover
8. Payment, accounting, email or external-authority production activation
9. Source write-lock, archival or deletion
10. Final decommission

A run that reaches one of these gates must report the evidence, unresolved decision and safest reversible next action.
