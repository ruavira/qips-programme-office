## Item 4 — W07-01, Admissions, Recruitment and Selection

**Verdict: APPROVED WITH FOUR AMENDMENTS.**

Option B adopted: eligibility screening, two independent evidence scores per eligible application, and
moderation of material differences — with institutional nomination allowed as one application source held
to the same standard.

Not approved: score threshold, cohort cap, country allocation, conversion forecast. Q006 remains open.

**Promotes to canon: 3 facts.** That cohort-one admissions separates eligibility, evidence sufficiency,
competitive selection and financial support; that eligible applications receive two independent evidence
scores with moderation, institutional nomination being one source under the same standard; and that title,
institutional prestige, sponsorship, ability to pay and reasonable-adjustment need do not increase or
reduce the merit score.

**Amendment 1 — section 3 canon references corrected.** As drafted on 28 July the dossier stated that
cohort one recruits in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan, and that F024 and F025 were PROPOSED. All were
overtaken by the PARAMETER reclassification of 1 August. The decision does not depend on the error, but
condition 2 would have inherited it.

**Amendment 2 — new condition 6, the institutional seat.** An institutional seat is sold as transferable
within the institution, not guaranteed to a named nominee, and this must be stated in writing before money
changes hands. Reason: the third promoted fact holds that sponsorship and ability to pay do not affect the
merit score — which is right, and which means a paying institution can have its nominee rejected. A buyer
who believes it purchased a place rather than an application will not buy again.

**Amendment 3 — new condition 7, the reviewer pool.** The reviewer pool is constituted separately from the
teaching faculty; overlap is permitted where an individual consents to both roles, but is not assumed.
Reason: double scoring plus moderation is a substantial October–December workload, and the faculty
economics research found time to be the largest single driver of teaching-faculty attrition, with
first-year faculty the most fragile cohort.

**Amendment 4 — new condition 8, the support-needs field.** The application captures what support an
applicant will need in a field that does not score, excluded from the rubric and from moderation. Reason:
completion evidence from a comparable blended programme showed doctors succeeding at 53–67% where nurses
succeeded at 9% on the same course. A correctly profession-blind process will admit a cohort that
completes asymmetrically; the remedy is to know in December what must be resourced in February.

**Reopening clause:** the moderation trigger returns to the committee after observed data from the first
live cycle, as condition 4 already provides. Conditions 6, 7 and 8 return if Q006 shows eligible demand
below capacity, which would reopen the Contrarian's case for Option A.

## Item 5 — IKR-D001, ratification of the governance operating system

**Verdict: RATIFIED, without conditions.**

QIPS-IKR-POS v1.0.0 is ratified as the programme's repository and project-governance operating system.
The GitHub-centred system-of-record map becomes formal programme policy; the required registers, agent
contract, change control and health reviews become mandatory. Existing canon and CCC authority are
unchanged. No programme fact is approved, no publication authorised, no migration executed, and no change
made to external sharing.

**A correction is recorded here deliberately.** During the sitting the committee was advised that
`AGENTS.md` and `README.md` instructed agents to run scripts in a `bin/` directory that does not exist,
and ratification was initially proposed as conditional on correcting that. **The advice was wrong.** It
was drawn from a stale 28 July scaffold held outside the repository — not a git repository at all — rather
than from the repository itself. The actual `AGENTS.md` correctly instructs `engine/agenda.py`,
`engine/controlroom.py` and `engine/validate_ikr_pos.py`, which is exactly what continuous integration
runs. The correction was put to the committee before the verdict was taken, and the verdict is
accordingly unconditional.

It is minuted rather than quietly dropped because a decision register that hides a corrected error
teaches the next reader nothing, and because the same failure had already occurred once in the same
session.

**A real defect was found in its place, and fixed in the same change.** `engine/validate_ikr_pos.py`
pinned IKR-D001 to `PENDING` unconditionally, so the validator would have blocked the ratification it
exists to protect. The rule its own failure message promised — that a decision may leave PENDING only via
a minuted CCC verdict — is now the rule enforced: a decision may move to APPROVED, REJECTED, DEFERRED or
SUPERSEDED only when it records a `decided_on` date and cites a `minute_reference` that exists on disk.

Change record IKR-CR-001 moves to `IMPLEMENTED_AND_RATIFIED`.

**Reopening clause:** the profile returns to the committee at the first scheduled repository health

## Item 6 — ratification of the six PARAMETER facts

**Verdict: ALL SIX RATIFIED, with three amendments.**

F007 observership geography, F017 brand identity, F018 admissions breadth, F020 cohort 1 country set,
F024 tuition and F025 cohort 1 size are ratified in PARAMETER status with their declared ranges. The
committee ratified **the status and the range, not the value** in every case.

What ratification establishes: that "deliberately open, with a declared range" is a legitimate state for
canon to hold, and that the programme design is genuinely valid across each declared range. This is what
allows nine workstreams to proceed that would otherwise be blocked by six unmade decisions.

**Amendment 1 — F007 rationale.** The parameter now records that observership geography is not an
administrative detail. The in-person component accounted for 67–87% of total implementation cost in the
closest African blended-training costing study, with per diems, accommodation and travel at 30–40% of
implementer direct cost. It is the largest single determinant of whether the unit economics work, and
whoever decides it should know that.

**Amendment 2 — F020 decide-by, correcting a drift.** At Item 2 the committee agreed to move F020's
decide-by earlier. That agreement had been written into W03-01 as condition 6 but **not into F020
itself**, so it existed as an instruction to a workstream rather than as a property of the fact — exactly
the drift the register exists to prevent. F020's `decide_by` now reads: the earlier of the date W03
records as the latest start for lodging CPD regulator applications, to be established by 31 August 2026,
or before applications open.

**Amendment 3 — F024 narrowed on evidence.** From USD 400–900 to **USD 450–600, working figure USD 500**,
for the LMIC individual rung. USD 500 is the only point inside the committed self-pay bands of all three
candidate markets and under all three ceilings — Nigeria USD 294–661 with a ceiling near USD 600 at West
African College of Physicians fellowship-exam parity, Ghana USD 530–970, Pakistan USD 270–540. The former
USD 700–900 band sits above Pakistan's ceiling and at or above Nigeria's, and moves to the institutional
rung. The recommended published ladder, on World Bank income bands after the ISQua precedent, is recorded
in the parameter.

**Amendment 4 — F025 given targets.** The range 25–64 is retained, with a **target of 40, a floor of 24
and a ceiling of 64**. Coaching runs in groups of eight and each group is a stepped cost rather than a
smooth one, so the economically clean sizes are 24, 32, 40, 48, 56 and 64. A cohort off a multiple of
eight pays for a coaching group it does not fill.

**Reopening clause:** each parameter returns to the committee at its own declared `decide_by`, or earlier
if evidence moves its range.

**Reopening clause:** returns if the cohort start date moves.

### F026 — indemnity trigger

**Verdict: APPROVED WITH AMENDMENT.**

Restated as: *"If verified per-learner indemnity exceeds USD 75, the observership defers to cohort 2 and
the LMIC individual tuition rung drops to the floor of its declared F024 range."*

The committee endorsed the shape of the rule — it pre-commits a response to a number nobody yet has,
which is the right way to handle an open question — and amended it twice.

**Amendment 1.** The original hard-coded an LMIC price of USD 475, derived from the 28 July council
recommendation rather than from the pricing evidence base. Restated as a rule relative to F024 so that it
cannot silently contradict F024 once tuition settles.

**Amendment 2.** The USD 75 threshold is recorded as a **decision threshold chosen by the committee, not
an evidenced benchmark**. Published per-learner indemnity figures were NOT FOUND for Nigeria, Ghana or
Pakistan. This is minuted so that nobody later mistakes the figure for a market rate. Q001 remains the
open question that makes the rule testable.

**Reopening clause:** fires automatically when Q001 resolves.

### F027 — go/no-go kill date

**Verdict: DEFERRED, returned to W17.** The reason is written into `workstreams/W17/brief.md` as a
standing constraint, per the charter rule that a rejection carries a reason.

Two conflicts with decisions approved earlier in this same sitting.

**Conflict 1 — date collision.** F012 is approved canon: applications close 31 December 2026. F027
requires 40 paid deposits by 20 November 2026, forty-one days before the window closes. W07-01, approved
at Item 4, adopted eligibility screening then two independent scores then moderation then offers, over a
closed field. That process cannot yield 40 paid deposits six weeks before its own field closes. Only
rolling first-qualified offers could, and that is the Option A the council rejected.

**Conflict 2 — faculty backup.** F027 asks for 12 consented faculty. W05-01 condition 3, approved at Item
3, requires every required slot to hold a primary and a backup or an approved risk treatment. Twelve
consented faculty is twelve primaries and no backups — it would pass a gate that the faculty operating
model treats as failing.

**Not in dispute, and to be retained:** the idea of a dated kill gate, the 20 November date itself, and
the conjunctive "all three at once" structure. The committee rejected the numbers, not the mechanism.

**Reopening clause:** returns to the committee when W17 submits a version resolving both constraints.
