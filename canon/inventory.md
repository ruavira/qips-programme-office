## What this tells the committee

Four workstreams already hold substantial current work: **W02** has the curriculum spine,
**W04** has the Host Site Standard, **W14** has a complete marketing pack, and **W16** has an
evidence base, a live model and a council verdict awaiting decision.

Thirteen hold nothing yet. Of those, five are blocking cohort 1 and have never been worked:

- **W03** Assessment and Certification — there is no certificate. The credential is the product.
- **W05** Teaching Faculty — no named faculty, no consent, twelve Saturdays unassigned.
- **W15** Partnerships — no signed agreement between SQHN, RCI, QAI and TAC, so nobody has
  authority to sign a host-site MoU, so W04's standard cannot be applied to a real site.
- **W17** Governance and Risk — the unquoted indemnity sits here and gates both the price and
  the observership.
- **W07** Admissions — no eligibility criteria, no selection rubric, and no recruitment
  denominator.

That is the honest read: the programme has a good spine and a good shopfront, and almost
nothing in between them that makes it lawful, credentialled, staffed or sellable.
