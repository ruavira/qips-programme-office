# QIPS calibration pack

## Credential, faculty and admissions operating instruments

**Status:** Working prototypes; not approved public wording or canon
**Date:** 28 July 2026
**Workstreams:** W03, W05 and W07

## Purpose

This calibration round tests whether the working direction established in the
blocker-batch research can operate. It covers credential verification, faculty
nomination and clearance, and the behaviour of the admissions rubric on
deliberately difficult fictional cases.

Nothing in this pack appoints a person, admits an applicant, grants CPD
recognition or authorises a credential claim.

## Cross-stream findings

1. A completion award, professional certification, post-nominal and CPD
   recognition are different governed products.
2. Faculty publicity must follow verified appointment evidence and granular
   consent. An affiliation does not establish organisational endorsement.
3. Prestige, sponsorship and ability to pay must not substitute for evidence
   of readiness to complete applied improvement work.
4. GitHub contains blank instruments, decision logic and aggregates. Personal
   evidence remains in a restricted records system.

## W03 — credential architecture

### Working recommendation

Use a controlled programme-completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD
applications for cohort one. Do not use a QIPS post-nominal.

### Benchmark signal

| Comparator | Governed product | QIPS consequence |
|---|---|---|
| CPHQ | Professional certification with examination, verification and recertification | Do not call programme completion a certification |
| ISQua Fellowship | Maintained fellowship status and FISQua post-nominal | Do not invent a post-nominal |
| ACHS QIL | Programme plus work-based project linked to a named post-nominal | Keep the capstone; defer the designation |
| CPSP FCPS | Registered specialist training and examinations | Do not imply regulated fellowship equivalence |
| WACP Fellowship | Training, membership, logbook, activities, dissertation or cases, and examinations | Use QIPS evidence without borrowing college status |

### Options

- **A — completion certificate only.** Feasible and low-risk, but professional
  recognition is limited to the approved completion evidence and verification
  record.
- **B — completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications.**
  Adds recognition only where a named body grants it. Administratively
  fragmented, but recommended for cohort one.
- **C — governed professional certification.** Potentially the strongest later
  signal, but requires independent governance, standard setting, security,
  appeals, revocation and maintenance.

### Migration-safe verification

- Use identifiers unrelated to name, email, country or date of birth.
- Require two-person approval for issue, correction, replacement and
  revocation.
- Expose only approved public name, award, issuer, cohort or date, standard
  version and credential status.
- Link QR codes to a canonical verification domain, not directly to Base44.
- Keep a platform-neutral export so issued identifiers survive migration.

## W05 — faculty operating system

### Working recommendation

Use accountable core faculty assigned to twelve coverage slots, with bounded
guests and explicit primary and backup responsibility.

### Options

- **A — distributed partner and volunteer faculty.** Broad network and
  flexibility, but fragile coverage, continuity, rights and conflict control.
- **B — accountable core faculty plus bounded guests.** Verified primary and
  backup for each slot, with appointments, development and consent. Recommended
  for cohort one.
- **C — permanent academic unit.** Strongest continuity and scholarship, but
  disproportionate before recurring demand and revenue are established.

### Nomination-to-publication workflow

1. Nominate against a defined coverage slot using a restricted candidate ID.
2. Verify subject credibility, teaching capability, context competence and
   reliability or integrity separately.
3. Disclose and independently resolve conflicts before appointment.
4. Set outcomes, dates, honorarium, cancellation, intellectual-property,
   recording, confidentiality, accessibility and backup terms.
5. Orient, rehearse, observe and improve delivery.
6. Release only the name, title, organisation, biography, image, topic and
   promotional uses explicitly consented to.

Candidate names, contacts, CVs, references, licences, payment or tax
information, signatures and private conflicts remain in a restricted Drive or
successor records system. The committee-facing control room shows aggregate
slot status until publication clearance.

## W07 — admissions calibration

### Working recommendation

Use eligibility screening, two independent evidence scores and moderated
selection. Institutional nomination is one channel under the same standard.

### Corrected formula

Criterion points equal the rating from zero to four, divided by four, multiplied
by the criterion weight. This produces a 100-point total.

### Six-case diagnostic

| Case | Stress test | Reviewer A | Reviewer B | Diagnostic result |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| A | Strong applied senior nurse profile | 92.50 | 87.50 | Strong under both; initiative anchor needs precision |
| B | Early-career analyst; equivalent support | 77.50 | 70.00 | Route works; protected-time evidence differs |
| C | Prestigious title; weak readiness | 45.00 | 40.00 | Prestige does not compensate |
| D | Independent consultant; access conditional | 80.00 | 73.75 | Assessable; verify lawful project access |
| E | Pharmacist with adjusted completion plan | 87.50 | 83.75 | Adjustment does not reduce score |
| F | Sponsored executive; generic evidence | 62.50 | 56.25 | Payment and sponsorship do not buy merit |

The mean absolute reviewer difference was 5.63 points and the largest was 7.50.
Six fictional cases cannot establish reliability or an entry threshold. They
identify where the next calibration set must concentrate.

### Required rubric revisions

- Separate the current improvement challenge from evidence of past initiative
  and follow-through.
- Define protected time as a calendar, live-session plan, observership route and
  responsible support or equivalent contact.
- Record project access as verified, conditional or not established outside the
  numerical score.
- Keep evidence sufficiency as a gate.
- Run at least twelve more fictional borderline cases, varying one evidence
  element at a time.
- Test a moderation trigger of ten total points or a two-level criterion
  difference before approval.

### Application-to-offer workflow

1. Collect context, a bounded improvement challenge, follow-through evidence,
   completion plan, reflection and declarations without patient-identifiable
   data.
2. Record eligibility as met, conditional, not met or not applicable without
   ranking at this stage.
3. Record two independent zero-to-four ratings, evidence locations and
   rationales; calculate totals automatically.
4. Reconcile material differences against anchors, remove conflicts and apply
   only pre-approved tie-break or equity rules.
5. Issue conditional offers with explicit document, access, sponsorship or
   payment, and observership conditions and deadlines.
6. Timestamp funnel stages and publish observed conversion rates only after the
   denominator closes.
