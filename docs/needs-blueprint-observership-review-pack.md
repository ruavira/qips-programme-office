# QIPS needs, blueprint and observership review pack

Status: committee working pack; not a CCC decision pack; not approved for publication

Date: 29 July 2026

## Purpose

This pack connects who the programme is for, what evidence participants must build and what they may
safely observe. W03, W05 and W07 already occupy the current three formal Council slots. W01, W02
and W04 therefore remain verified working drafts for a later agenda.

The audit also corrected two inventory claims: the named programme architecture and Host Site
Standard source files were not found in GitHub or connected Drive. The v0.9 instruments are explicit
reconstructions from canon, brochure v1.2, official guidance and existing W17 controls.

## W01 — needs and personas

### Review proposition

Use task-and-context segmentation rather than job title or profession alone. Approve the research
protocol for fielding, not the persona hypotheses or any demand conclusion.

| Hypothesis | Core context | What must be tested |
|---|---|---|
| Frontline improvement lead | Quality work is part of a clinical/allied role | Protected time, authority, data and workplace problem access |
| Dedicated quality/governance practitioner | Owns audit, incidents, indicators, standards or accreditation | Actual proficiency and need beyond compliance |
| Facility/network decision-maker | Governs quality across teams or providers | Participant fit versus sponsor/institutional route |
| Oversight/implementation professional | Regulator, payer, ministry, NGO or programme role | Consistent judgement, conflict and observership feasibility |
| Career transitioner | Entering quality without a formal portfolio | Real project/data access and responsible workplace contact |

The proposed coverage target is at least eight practitioner and four employer/sponsor conversations
per country, deliberately covering multiple settings and titled/informal quality work. It is a
diversity target, not a statistically representative sample.

### Feedback requested

1. Which verified organisations and networks should recruit each role in each country?
2. Who can approve privacy, outreach and a restricted Drive location for contacts/transcripts?
3. Which public, private, faith-based, payer/regulator and primary/community settings are missing?
4. Who will field and code interviews without turning recruitment convenience into validation?

## W02 — 12-month programme blueprint

### Review proposition

Prototype backward from the twelve artefacts. Every month teaches the capabilities needed for one
recoverable capstone component; the observership portfolio remains separately assessable.

| Months | Progression | Evidence produced |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Problem, systems, safety, human factors, governance and measurement | Problem brief, process map, measurement plan |
| 4–6 | Observation bridge, charter, aim, change, cause and risk | Sponsor-signed charter, SMART aim, cause/driver analysis |
| 7–9 | Safety implementation, data quality, analysis and variation | Three governed PDSA-cycle records |
| 10–12 | Leadership, standards, sustainability, spread and presentation | Run chart, sustainability plan, spread/final portfolio |

The provisional workload ledger reconciles to the approved 228 hours: 96 independent/eLearning,
24 live, 24 coaching preparation/participation, 44 artefact/capstone and 40 observership hours. The
allocation must be replaced by timed prototypes before publication.

### Feedback requested

1. Does the month sequence match real task dependencies and the available workplace data cycle?
2. Which capabilities are missing or set at the wrong level for the five W01 hypotheses?
3. Who owns months 1–2 content and rubric prototypes, source review and accessibility testing?
4. What reasonable-adjustment and low-bandwidth needs must be tested before production lock?

## W04 — host-site and observership controls

### Review proposition

Calibrate the reconstructed Host Site Standard v0.9 and allow an approved block or split schedule
totalling 40 hours. Do not qualify or name sites yet.

Every site must pass legal identity/authority, insurance and local-law match, named preceptor and
alternate, no-hands-on boundaries, induction/confidentiality, patient choice where applicable,
observable quality systems, a dated 40-hour schedule, safeguarding/accessibility and continuity.
The scored domains cover governance, safety/legal, observable systems, learning/preceptor,
privacy/consent, capacity/continuity and access/welfare.

Capacity is calculated inside the actual placement window:

`raw participant capacity = floor(approved observer-hours / 40)`

Permitted allocation is the lowest of that result, preceptor, access/room, insurance, activity and
qualification-condition limits. Annual arithmetic cannot substitute for dated capacity.

### Feedback requested

1. Which candidate sites may be used only for synthetic calibration before formal outreach?
2. Who has authority to sign, and who will obtain country legal/insurance review?
3. What host activities can be observed without patient or confidential record exposure?
4. What contingency capacity and participant accessibility evidence must exist before offers?

## Non-publication and data rules

Do not publish personas, eligibility, market size, capacity, named hosts, insurance, programme
blueprint, award implications or site tiers as approved. Contacts, transcripts, insurance terms,
legal advice and restricted site evidence remain in controlled Drive locations. GitHub stores blank
instruments and de-identified synthesis; Base44 shows status and approved links only.
