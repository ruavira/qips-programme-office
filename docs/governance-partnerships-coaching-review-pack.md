# QIPS Governance, Partnerships and Coaching Review Pack

Prepared: 29 July 2026

Status: working review pack; not a CCC decision and not approved for publication

## What this pack does

This pack advances three workstreams that control whether the programme can make commitments and
deliver its monthly coaching promise safely:

- W06 — Coaching Faculty and Coach Development
- W15 — Partnerships, Affiliations and Accreditation
- W17 — Governance, Legal, Risk and Data Protection

The pack does not add anything to canon, close an open question or change the current formal CCC
agenda. It presents operating systems and the evidence that humans, partners and professional
advisers must now supply.

## Executive position

The recommended architecture is deliberately evidence-gated:

1. Coaching is an accountable internal service with observed readiness, primary and alternate
   coverage, reflective supervision and a ratio chosen only after a timed capacity rehearsal.
2. Partnership is controlled through four separate proofs: exact identity, authority, contribution
   and public claim permission. A logo or historic relationship is not a substitute.
3. Launch is controlled through one evidence-linked assurance case. Missing safety, legal, data,
   insurance or truthfulness gates cannot be averaged into an overall green state.

All three designs are portable: GitHub holds the governed source, Drive holds the human review copy,
and Base44 presents workflow state without becoming the only record.

## W06 — coaching operating system

### Working recommendation

Prepare the accountable internal coaching model required by F009. Clear each coach through observed
practice, role terms and continuing supervision. Give every group primary and alternate coverage.
Do not approve or publish a coach-to-participant ratio until a representative monthly sandbox proves
the workload.

### What is now designed

- six-domain coach competency framework;
- selection and readiness gate;
- internal development and supported-launch sequence;
- standard pre-call, in-call and post-call protocol;
- capacity test covering artefact review, calls, documentation, supervision and absence cover;
- quality, boundary, escalation, continuity and exit controls.

### Evidence still required

- approved W02 monthly artefact specifications and workload;
- approved W05 appointment system and verified actual coverage;
- W17 clearance of terms, confidentiality, safeguarding and data routes;
- actual coach candidate evidence and signed terms; and
- a timed full-month capacity and low-bandwidth rehearsal.

### Review questions

1. Is the boundary between coaching, teaching, assessment, therapy and clinical advice clear enough?
2. Should any other evidence be mandatory before the observed simulation?
3. Who should own coaching supervision and suspension authority?
4. Which realistic artefact set should be used in the capacity rehearsal?

## W15 — partner operating and claims standard

### Working recommendation

Use an SQHN-led master framework with role-specific schedules as the negotiating baseline, subject
to counsel and the actual parties' response. Require separate evidence for identity, authority,
contribution and claims. Withhold Shifa and ISQua institutional wording and marks until exact written
permission exists.

### What is now designed

- one controlled partner evidence bundle;
- provisional decision-rights schedule;
- definitive-agreement clause architecture;
- public claims matrix with evidence and takedown rules;
- accreditation-route test that separates investigation from status; and
- exit, teach-out and critical-contribution continuity gates.

### Evidence still required

- registry and signatory evidence for every party;
- exact QAI legal identity and the current governing instrument;
- definitive revenue, IP, data, liability, exit and signing-authority terms;
- separate authorised Shifa and ISQua wording/mark responses; and
- counsel-reviewed signed instruments.

### Review questions

1. Which contributions does each partner believe it has already committed?
2. Who may sign participant, vendor, faculty, coach and host-site instruments?
3. Which IP should be licensed rather than jointly owned?
4. If Shifa or ISQua supplies no authorised wording, should the public claim be removed immediately?

## W17 — governance, risk and launch standard

### Working recommendation

Build one launch assurance case with purpose-specific responsibility and country evidence. Treat
mandatory legal, safety, authority, data, insurance and public-truth gates as non-averagable. Issue
the existing broker and counsel instructions through regulator-verifiable routes.

### What is now designed

- decision layers and minimum decision record;
- eleven-part launch assurance case;
- programme risk register with triggers, controls, contingencies and owners;
- purpose-specific data-accountability and system-boundary gate;
- comparable insurance evidence and selection gate;
- single incident/crisis front door; and
- stop/go rule that preserves the PROPOSED status of F025–F027.

### Evidence still required

- comparable written insurance quotations for Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan;
- country counsel, including current Pakistan personal-data-law confirmation;
- approved processing inventory, roles, notices, transfers and record-category retention schedule;
- named programme privacy lead and country contacts;
- delegated authorities, participant terms and partner instruments; and
- a full operational rehearsal and explicit residual-risk decision.

### Review questions

1. Who is the accountable launch approver, and which matters are reserved to the owner or CCC?
2. Who should be the programme privacy lead and country contacts?
3. Which brokers/counsel should receive the controlled instructions?
4. Which mandatory gate should cause automatic pause rather than escalation for discretion?

## Decisions and actions requested now

This is a working review, not a request to approve canon. Reviewers are asked to:

1. comment on the coaching role boundary and nominate the capacity-rehearsal evidence owner;
2. confirm or correct the provisional partner roles and identify the authorised signatory for each
   organisation;
3. authorise release of the controlled partner evidence request, broker RFQ and country-counsel
   instruction;
4. nominate the programme privacy lead and country contacts for legal confirmation; and
5. identify any mandatory launch gate missing from the assurance case.
