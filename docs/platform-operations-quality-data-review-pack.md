# QIPS platform, operations, quality and data review pack

Status: committee working pack; not a CCC decision pack; not approved for publication

Date: 29 July 2026

## Purpose

This pack joins four controls needed to operate a migration-ready programme. W09, W10 and W11 are
active verification workstreams. W12 has a substantive v0.9 draft but remains formally blocked by
W03, W17, Q009 and unsigned partner terms. W03, W05 and W07 remain the three current formal CCC
dossiers.

## W09 — platform and migration

Use separable public-site, learning-environment, live-session and programme-office surfaces. Keep
Base44 as the replaceable control-room projection. Fit-test the existing RCI Moodle estate against
the full monthly cycle, access, low-bandwidth, WCAG 2.2 AA, security, restore and portability gates.
Do not infer a vendor, tenant, region or production-data approval.

## W10 — global operations

Represent every event in UTC and an IANA zone, generate participant-local views and preserve an
equivalent route where the actual cohort would face unreasonable local hours. The v0.9 runbook
covers T-21 readiness through month close. Exact 2027 dates, holidays, delivery teams, attendance
consequences and F023 remain unresolved.

## W11 — quality and evaluation

Measure access, delivery conditions, capability, workplace transfer, credible project signals,
sustainability/spread and programme learning. Satisfaction cannot stand in for effectiveness.
Every workstream output must pass source, canon, alternatives, limitations, safety, privacy,
accessibility, ownership, acceptance-test and publication-control checks.

## W12 — data and research assets

Separate programme operations, governed evaluation, optional research/publication and prohibited
data. Patient-identifiable information and copied clinical records do not enter ordinary programme
systems. Retention durations, lawful bases, systems, cross-border transfers and research reuse stay
unapproved pending W17/counsel and partner/ethics decisions.
