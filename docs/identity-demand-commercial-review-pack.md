# QIPS identity, demand and commercial review pack

Status: committee working pack; not a CCC decision pack; not approved for publication

Date: 29 July 2026

## Purpose

This pack brings three connected workstreams to a reviewable point without exceeding the current
Council agenda limit. W03, W05 and W07 already occupy the three formal decision slots. The W13,
W14 and W16 dossiers therefore remain working drafts for the next agenda.

## What is established

The offer is a 12-month hybrid professional programme. Each month includes eLearning, a live
faculty session, a small-group coaching call and an artefact. The ordinary-week commitment is about
four hours and the total is about 228 hours. A 40-hour in-country observership runs from month 3 to
week 2 of month 4. Applications are planned for October–31 December 2026 and cohort one for
January–December 2027 across Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan. SQHN leads and contracts.

## W13 — identity architecture

### Review proposition

Authorise clearance—not publication—of **QIPS Professional Programme** with the mandatory
descriptor **Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety**. Retain an SQHN-led descriptive title as the
fallback.

### Lighter, brighter visual direction

The prototype palette uses warm canvas `#FFF9F1`, white, mist aqua `#DFF3F5`, sky `#DCEEFF`, coral
`#F28C68`, deep teal `#0F5257` and midnight `#16324F`. The hierarchy is sponsor → programme →
descriptor → approved Partners → separately approved credential/recognition.

### Feedback requested

1. Does the candidate sound credible and clear in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan?
2. Is the healthcare quality and patient safety descriptor sufficiently specific?
3. Which registered SQHN name, mark files and usage rules are authoritative?
4. Are there linguistic, cultural or existing-brand conflicts the clearance search should test?

## W14 — claims and demand system

### Review proposition

Approve a gated two-track system: restricted institutional discovery and a public interest journey
that uses approved claims only. Do not yet authorise public application, payment or paid promotion.

The funnel distinguishes discovery, interest, readiness, application, offer and paid enrolment.
Q006 requests only de-identified aggregate counts from SQHN's prior promotions so capacity and
conversion are based on a denominator rather than an opinion.

### Feedback requested

1. Who can supply aggregate delivered, visited, started, completed, paid and attended counts?
2. Which SQHN and partner channels may be used, and under what consent?
3. Who owns the privacy notice, retention decision and data-protection contact?
4. Which institutional sponsors should be interviewed before any public campaign?

## W16 — pricing and unit economics

### Review proposition

Approve the structure of a governed tier-ladder model with separate individual and institutional
routes. Do not approve a price. The accompanying workbook deliberately reports **DECISION
INCOMPLETE** while required inputs are missing.

The inherited nine-sheet model and its evidence files were not present in GitHub or Drive. The
reconstructed portable model records the F024/F025 ladder and cohort size as proposals, uses
formula-driven calculations and prevents a breakeven or operating-result conclusion until the
evidence gate passes.

### Feedback requested

Provide evidence and an accountable owner for indemnity; credential/recognition; host/preceptor;
faculty/coaching; platform; payment route and fees; partner share; tax and FX; refund/bad debt;
scholarships; capacity and demand mix. ITF reimbursement remains zero until primary terms exist.

## Non-publication rule

Until the relevant decisions and evidence exist, do not publish the exact programme name, award or
recognition, faculty, partner affiliations or logos, price/deposit/discount, cohort capacity, named
host sites, or outcome claims. The restricted concept note contains visible `[TO CONFIRM: …]`
markers for this reason.

## Portability rule

GitHub stores governed text, registers, schemas and formula-building logic. Google Drive carries
human-editable review documents and the native Sheet. Base44 displays approved status and links.
No platform is the sole copy, and no personal applicant or payment data belongs in the repository
or general control-room dataset.
