## W03-01 — credential architecture

### Decision requested

Approve a controlled programme-completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications for cohort one, with no QIPS post-nominal and no professional-certification claim.

### Why now

The architecture affects the assessment blueprint, public naming and price. It narrows open question Q002 but does not close it.

### Options considered

1. **Completion certificate only.** Fastest honest route and lowest recognition administration, with a narrower external signal.
2. **Completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications.** Adds only the recognition a named body grants, profession by profession. Recommended.
3. **Governed professional certification.** A possible later architecture requiring independent governance, standard setting, security, appeals, verification, revocation and maintenance.

### Benchmark conclusion

[NAHQ's CPHQ](https://nahq.org/credentials/) is a governed professional certification; [ISQua Fellowship](https://isqua.org/fellowship/) supports maintained fellowship status; [ACHS QIL](https://www.achs.org.au/qil) links work-based evidence to a named designation; [CPSP](https://cpsp.edu.pk/general-info.php) and the [WACP fellowship control form](https://wacpcoam.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/fellowship_included_control_forms.pdf) sit within specialist-college rules. None authorises QIPS to describe programme completion as professional certification or to create a post-nominal.

### Conditions

1. W02 and W03 present one reconciled completion blueprint by 31 August 2026.
2. SQHN supplies written awarding authority and named authorised signatories before the exact certificate is approved.
3. A CPD claim appears only after the regulator record contains the approval identifier, profession, jurisdiction, credits, validity dates and exact permitted wording.
4. The verification prototype passes forged-identifier, duplicate-issuance, correction, revocation and service-unavailable tests before issuance.
5. W15 confirms partner acknowledgements and logo rights before artwork is locked.

### Proposed canon if approved

1. Cohort one uses a controlled programme-completion certificate; it is not represented as a professional certification.
2. Cohort one uses no QIPS post-nominal.
3. Any CPD recognition is profession- and jurisdiction-specific and may be published only after written approval from the named body.

### Verification

**PASS WITH FINDINGS; zero open blocking findings.** The source set was corrected, unsupported equivalence was removed and Q002 remains open. Official links require a final human check immediately before the sitting.

## W05-01 — faculty operating model

### Decision requested

Approve accountable core faculty plus bounded guests, organised by provisional coverage slots and controlled by evidence, conflict, appointment, preparation and consent gates.

### Why now

The programme needs an accountable delivery system before it can make faculty claims. This decision approves the system, not any person, and does not close Q003.

### Options considered

1. **Distributed partners and volunteers.** Broad and flexible, but fragile on continuity, rights, conflict control and backup.
2. **Accountable core plus bounded guests.** Each required slot has verified primary and backup responsibility, appointment, development and consent. Recommended.
3. **Permanent academic unit.** Strongest continuity, but disproportionate before recurring demand and revenue are established.

### Benchmark conclusion

[IHI](https://www.ihi.org/education/courses/hospital-flow-professional-development-program) connects named faculty to relevant experience and disclosures; [ISQua Experts](https://isqua.org/isqua-experts/) offers a curated multidisciplinary network; [ACCME standards](https://accme.org/rules/standards/) separate valid content from marketing and manage relevant relationships; the [Ghana College rules](https://gcps.edu.gh/rules-regulations/) and [NPMCN accreditation guidance](https://npmcn.edu.ng/old/accreditation-guidelines/) emphasise prepared personnel, structured delivery and evaluation. QIPS should add explicit slot ownership, consent and backup without implying external accreditation or endorsement.

### Conditions

1. W02 and W05 reconcile the provisional twelve-slot map to the approved monthly calendar by 31 August 2026.
2. Every named person passes evidence review, conflict resolution, appointment and granular publication consent before public release.
3. Every required slot has a primary and backup, or an approved risk treatment, before enrolment commitments.
4. W03 records assessor calibration and conflict clearance before a faculty member assesses participant work.
5. Restricted candidate and appointment records remain outside GitHub and committee-facing Base44 views.

### Proposed canon if approved

1. Cohort one uses accountable core faculty for required coverage, supplemented by bounded guests.
2. No faculty identity or affiliation is published before evidence review, appointment, conflict clearance and explicit publication consent.
3. Teaching appointment does not by itself authorise assessment; assessors require separate calibration and conflict clearance.

### Verification

**PASS WITH FINDINGS; zero open blocking findings.** The verifier prevented provisional coverage slots from being read as appointments, preserved the individual-affiliation boundary and separated teaching from assessment authority. No personal candidate data appears in the repository.

## W07-01 — admissions architecture

### Decision requested

Approve eligibility screening, two independent evidence scores and moderated selection, with institutional nomination as one application source under the same standard.

### Why now

Applications open in October 2026. The programme needs a controlled process before forms and reviewer recruitment are built, while Q006, W01 personas and W03 credential level still prevent final threshold and capacity decisions.

### Options considered

1. **Rolling first-qualified offers.** Simple, but timing and information access dominate when demand exceeds capacity.
2. **Eligibility, double scoring and moderation.** Transparent and auditable, with added reviewer and calibration cost. Recommended.
3. **Employer-nominated institutional cohort.** Useful as one source, but not as the sole route because it can reproduce organisational hierarchy.

### Benchmark conclusion

[Africa CDC](https://africacdc.org/kofi-annan-fellowship-in-global-health-leadership/) publishes eligibility and applied-project expectations; the [Commonwealth Scholarship Commission](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/advice-for-applicants/) publishes merit, impact and plan dimensions; [Wellcome](https://wellcome.org/research-funding/guidance/how-wellcome-makes-funding-decisions/how-wellcome-assesses-funding-applications) separates eligibility, independent scoring, committee ranking and conflicts; [WHO GOARN](https://pandemichub.who.int/news-room/news/item/18-02-2026-call-for-applications-5th-cohort-of-the-goarn-berlin-fellowship-programme) connects eligibility to an applied institutional role; and the [Ghana College rules](https://gcps.edu.gh/rules-regulations/) use documentary eligibility and structured selection. QIPS should retain the controls while using a multidisciplinary practice-readiness standard.

### Calibration result

Eighteen fictional cases were independently scored. Across the twelve-case borderline extension, the mean absolute reviewer difference was 2.29 points, the largest difference was 12.50, and 67 of 72 criterion ratings matched exactly. One case triggered moderation and was resolved against the evidence anchor. These synthetic results test the instrument; they do not establish reliability or an entry threshold.

### Conditions

1. Q006 supplies the historic denominator and conversion record, or records them as unavailable after a named search, by 15 September 2026.
2. W01 personas and W03 credential level are reconciled to eligibility wording before the application form is published.
3. Reviewers calibrate on the eighteen fictional cases and declare conflicts before live scoring.
4. The initial live pilot uses moderation at ten total points or a two-level criterion difference; the CCC reviews the rule after observed data.
5. The form, calculation, evidence gates, reasonable-adjustment route and reconsideration process pass an end-to-end test before applications open.

### Proposed canon if approved

1. Cohort-one admissions separates eligibility, evidence sufficiency, competitive selection and financial support.
2. Eligible applications receive two independent evidence scores and moderation of material differences; institutional nomination is one source under the same standard.
3. Title, institutional prestige, sponsorship, ability to pay and reasonable-adjustment need do not increase or reduce the merit score.

### Verification

**PASS WITH FINDINGS; zero open blocking findings.** The verifier recalculated all eighteen fictional cases, exercised the moderation trigger and prevented synthetic agreement from being represented as reliability. Threshold, capacity and conversion claims remain withheld.
