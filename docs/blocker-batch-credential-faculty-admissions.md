# QIPS blocker-batch working pack

Credential, faculty and admissions architecture for committee review

Status: working recommendations; not approved public wording or canon

Date: 28 July 2026

Workstreams: W03, W05 and W07

## Purpose and decision boundary

This pack gives the committee one coherent view of three connected cohort-one design decisions:
what the programme may award, who may teach it, and how participants may be selected. It does
not approve a credential, appoint any person, admit any applicant or change canon. The detailed
working papers and evidence registers remain in the three workstream folders.

Until the CCC decides otherwise, public material must not claim a professional certification,
post-nominal, CPD/CEU recognition, named faculty appointment or published admissions threshold.

## Recommended direction at a glance

1. **Credential — controlled completion certificate.** For cohort one, use a digitally
   verifiable programme-completion certificate and pursue profession-specific CPD applications.
   Do not create a post-nominal or use `certified` as a shortcut.
2. **Faculty — evidence before publicity.** Recruit against twelve coverage slots. Publish a
   name, role, biography, image, credential or affiliation only after verification, role
   acceptance, conflict review and written consent.
3. **Admissions — readiness rather than prestige.** Separate eligibility, competitive scoring
   and ability to take up a place. Score access to a real improvement challenge, initiative,
   protected time, learning readiness and contribution; keep ability to pay outside the academic
   and practice score.

## W03 — credential architecture

### Four concepts that must remain separate

- A **certificate of completion** records that approved programme requirements were met.
- A **professional certification** is a governed competence-assessment scheme with impartiality,
  verification, complaints/appeals and maintenance controls.
- A **post-nominal** is authorised and governed by an issuing body; it is not a marketing
  abbreviation.
- **CPD recognition** is granted under a named regulator or professional body's rules for a
  defined profession and jurisdiction.

### Options considered

**Option A — completion certificate only.** Lowest overclaim risk and feasible for cohort one,
but the labour-market signal depends on strong assessment evidence and public verification.

**Option B — completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications.** Retains the
controlled certificate and publishes only each recognition actually granted, including the
profession, jurisdiction, credit amount and validity period. This is the working recommendation.

**Option C — governed professional certification with maintenance.** Potentially the strongest
long-term signal, but it needs independent certification governance, an assessment blueprint and
standard setting, security, appeals, verification, recertification and revocation rules. It is a
later architecture, not a cohort-one label.

### Proposed certificate control

The certificate should state the recipient's verified legal name, exact approved award title,
issuer and authorised signatories, award date and cohort, unique credential identifier and public
verification route, applicable programme-standard version, and approved partner acknowledgement.
It should contain no unsupported accreditation mark, unlicensed logo or unapproved post-nominal.

Completion requirements must be reconciled with W02 and explicitly cover required eLearning and
live participation, monthly artefacts, capstone evidence, the 40-hour observership and portfolio,
integrity/conduct, remediation, appeal and interrupted-study rules.

### Committee decisions required

- Select the cohort-one credential option and title territory.
- Confirm SQHN's legal awarding authority and authorised signatories.
- Approve the verification route and owner.
- Reconcile the assessment and completion rules with W02.
- Name the professional bodies to approach after actual cohort professions are evidenced.

## W05 — faculty appointment and development

### Publication gate

A person's name, photograph, biography, title, institution, credential or affiliation may enter
public QIPS material only when the claim is verified, the programme role and topic are accepted,
and the individual gives written publication consent. An individual's affiliation must not be
presented as institutional endorsement.

### Twelve coverage slots

1. Healthcare quality and patient-safety foundations.
2. Safety science, human factors and just culture.
3. Quality governance and measurement systems.
4. Communication, teamwork and structured handoffs.
5. PDSA, change management and stakeholder engagement.
6. Process mapping, root-cause analysis and improvement tools.
7. Risk management, medication safety and infection prevention.
8. Data collection, run charts, SPC and visual display.
9. Safety culture, patient partnership and leadership.
10. Accreditation, standards and regulatory readiness.
11. Capstone planning, implementation and sustainability.
12. Capstone analysis, presentation and professional development.

The roster represents coverage, not twelve invented names. A guest speaker does not automatically
fill a core slot, and one person should cover multiple slots only after workload and continuity are
tested.

### Appointment evidence and selection standard

Each core candidate needs verified identity and current role, CV and relevant credentials,
evidence of recent subject practice, teaching/facilitation evidence or development commitment,
accepted outcomes and dates, disclosures, availability and backup, and signed appointment,
confidentiality, IP and publication choices.

Candidates should be assessed in four distinct domains:

- **Subject credibility:** current practice, qualification, scholarship or portfolio tied to the
  assigned outcomes.
- **Teaching capability:** adult learning, facilitation, assessment and feedback, evidenced by a
  rehearsal or observed sample unless an exemption is recorded.
- **Context competence:** ability to adapt to Nigeria, Ghana, Pakistan or comparable constrained
  settings rather than importing assumptions.
- **Reliability and integrity:** preparation, disclosure, respectful conduct, availability and
  acceptance of programme rules.

### Development and session acceptance

The pathway is orientation, teaching design, rehearsal, assessment calibration where relevant,
observed delivery and documented improvement. A session is ready only when outcomes, de-identified
examples, sources, low-bandwidth interaction, accessibility, disclosures, moderation, backup,
recording/captioning notices and the follow-up artefact are all settled.

### Committee decisions required

- Nominate prospects against coverage slots and distinguish agreements from prospects.
- Approve the appointment/consent owner and restricted storage route.
- Set the honorarium and cancellation policy before offers.
- Reconcile topic assignments and dates with the final W02 calendar.

## W07 — admissions and selection

### Eligibility screen

Applicants should evidence an approved country connection; a relevant clinical or non-clinical
role, qualification or credible entry pathway; adequate working-language ability; access to a real
improvement context; ability to complete the published time, live, coaching and observership
requirements; protected-time support or an approved equivalent plan; the required identity and
qualification documents; and agreement to conduct, integrity, privacy and observership rules.

Minimum qualification and experience thresholds remain undecided. They should follow the learner
personas and credential level rather than be chosen for exclusivity.

### Proposed 100-point scored rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence sought |
|---|---:|---|
| Relevance of role or intended pathway | 15 | Connection to quality/safety responsibility or a credible transition plan |
| Improvement challenge | 25 | A specific, important and feasible problem with access to a setting or team |
| Initiative and follow-through | 20 | A concrete example of completing or sustaining meaningful work |
| Protected-time and completion plan | 15 | A credible weekly plan, sponsor/equivalent support and observership feasibility |
| Reflection and learning readiness | 15 | Insight about limits, feedback needs and expected practice change |
| Contribution and equity impact | 10 | Potential to transfer learning to teams, underserved settings or system priorities |

Each criterion should use written 0–4 anchors from absent to exceptional, followed by independent
scoring by two trained reviewers and reconciliation of material differences. Prestige, title
seniority, familiarity and ability to pay are not criteria. Scholarship allocation should be
separate unless the CCC explicitly approves a transparent combined mechanism.

### Fair workflow and equivalent route

Administrative staff check eligibility and completeness. Independent reviewers score narrative
evidence, conflicts are removed, and a moderated panel applies pre-approved tie-break and equity
rules. Offers remain conditional on document verification and acceptance requirements.

Employer support may evidence project access and protected time, but an equivalent route must be
available for self-employed, between-role and early-career applicants who can demonstrate lawful
project access, a responsible contact and a feasible completion plan.

### Committee decisions required

- Set the minimum qualification and experience threshold.
- Approve employer support as mandatory or one of several equivalent routes.
- Approve weights, a minimum score and any country/sector constraints.
- Approve the scholarship budget and equity priority.
- Name the selection panel and decision authority.
- Approve the calendar, evidence list and reconsideration route.
- Reconcile the cohort cap with observed demand and delivery capacity.

## External and human inputs that remain indispensable

- W03: awarding authority, signatories, exact title, verification owner and W02 assessment map.
- W05: actual candidates, verified credentials, availability, appointment terms and consent.
- W07: W01 learner personas, W03 credential level, trial scoring, application-demand denominator
  and selection-panel authority.

These gaps are deliberate control points. They are not facts an AI, designer or programme-office
administrator may infer.

## Evidence base used for this research round

W03 reviewed official credential and continuing-development structures from NAHQ's CPHQ,
ISQua Fellowship, ACHS Quality Improvement Lead, Nigeria's MDCN, Ghana's Medical and Dental
Council and Pakistan's PMDC. W05 reviewed ACCME independence/disclosure controls, an IHI faculty
model and peer-reviewed faculty-development syntheses. W07 reviewed official selection guidance
from Africa CDC, the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission, WHO and Wellcome.

The complete evidence entries, access dates, applicability notes and constraints are recorded in
the three workstream evidence registers.
