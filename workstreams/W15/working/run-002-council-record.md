# W15 run 002 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

What partnership structure can move negotiations forward without laundering unverified identity,
authority or affiliation claims into programme fact?

## Advisor positions

- **Contrarian:** remove every external affiliation claim unless the counterparty supplies exact
  authorised wording; reputational aspiration is not evidence.
- **First Principles:** separate identity, authority, contribution and claim, then require evidence
  for each.
- **Executor:** use a master agreement with role schedules and a single evidence checklist unless
  counsel finds a genuine reason for a four-party joint instrument.
- **Outsider:** protect participants through teach-out, refund and continuity obligations, not just
  partner branding.
- **Expansionist:** build an accreditation roadmap only after product fit, evidence burden and claim
  rights are understood.

## Synthesis

Adopt the evidence-gated partner operating standard as the negotiation baseline. The committee is
not yet asked to approve a partner agreement or affiliation. The chairman adopted the convergence
and did not overrule the majority.

## Conditions

Q004, Q005 and Q008 remain open. Sensitive evidence stays restricted. Public material continues to
withhold unsupported institutional claims and marks.
