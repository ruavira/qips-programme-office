## Research conclusion

The likely expansion of QAI is now evidence-backed but not yet contractually verified. The programme must obtain the named counterparty's registration document and the 2020 MoU before Q005 can be recommended for closure. No reviewed official source supports the proposed `in affiliation with` statement for Shifa or ISQua, so Q008 remains blocking and both logos remain withheld.
