# DRAFT DOSSIER W15-01 — Partnerships, Affiliations and Accreditation

Status: blocked working draft; not on the CCC agenda

## 1. The decision requested

When the evidence is returned, approve the partner operating standard and the resulting definitive
agreement, authority schedule and exact claims matrix.

## 2. Why it matters now

Q004, Q005 and Q008 block host-site authority, public identity, finance, data and launch commitments.
Deferral without a structured request produces more informal statements, not evidence.

## 3. Canon constraints

F014, F015, F016, F021 and F022 apply. The standard cannot silently alter partner roles or create an
institutional affiliation.

## 5. Options

- **Informal MoU network:** fast but insufficient for authority, participant protection and claims.
- **One definitive four-party framework:** coherent if all parties genuinely share obligations, but
  negotiation-heavy and vulnerable to one-party delay.
- **SQHN master framework plus role schedules:** recommended subject to counsel; preserves F014,
  isolates contributions and allows controlled replacement.

## 6. Council verdict

All advisors required evidence-gated claims and teach-out. The chairman recommended the master
framework plus schedules as the drafting baseline, without treating it as signed.

## 7. Recommendation

Issue the controlled evidence request and instruct counsel from the standard. Return only when the
actual parties, authorities and unresolved commercial terms are visible.

## 8. Conditions

Verified corporate bundles; current instruments; agreed commercial/IP/data/exit terms; authorised
claims; counsel review; CCC approval.

## 9. Proposed canon if later approved

To be drafted only from the signed instrument. No proposed fact is safe before the counterparties
and authorities are verified.

## 10. What it would unblock

Q004, Q005 and Q008 as applicable; W04, W12, W13, W14 and W16 decisions and public claims.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

Maintain a machine-readable claims ledger with expiry and takedown rules; design every mark and
critical contribution to be replaceable without rewriting the programme.

## 12. Not established

The exact parties and authorities, current QAI instrument, Shifa/ISQua wording, revenue share, IP,
exit and host-site signing authority.

## 13. Verification

PASS WITH BLOCKING FINDINGS. The negotiation system is usable; the decision evidence does not yet
exist.
