# W15 partner verification and claims pack

Status: working pack for document requests and partner review

Date: 2026-07-28

## Immediate operating rule

Until written evidence is received, public material may name SQHN as lead sponsor and list the canon-approved partners, but it must not say that QIPS is affiliated with, accredited by, certified by, endorsed by or delivered in partnership with Shifa International Hospitals or ISQua. Partner logos are withheld until written permission is recorded.

## Public claims control

### Allowed now

- `Presented by SQHN with partners Ruavira Collective Inc., QAI and The Arete Connoisseurs (TAC).`
- `The programme draws on internationally recognised healthcare-quality frameworks.`
- A named faculty member's accurately verified personal credential, once the individual approves the wording and publication.

The first sentence reflects current canon but still needs the partner legal names reconciled before final publication.

### Withhold until written approval

- `In affiliation with Shifa International Hospitals and ISQua.`
- `In partnership with ISQua.`
- `ISQua-accredited`, `ISQua-certified` or `ISQua-endorsed` programme.
- Shifa or ISQua logos, badges or marks.
- Jointly issued certificate, joint faculty, host-site or accreditation claims.
- Any claim that the 2020 QAI–SQHN MoU automatically covers QIPS.

### Individual-faculty wording

Permitted only after evidence and consent: `[Name] is an ISQua [verified credential/status] and participates in a personal capacity. ISQua does not accredit or endorse this programme.`

## Partnership term-sheet issue list

The negotiating draft must settle:

1. Exact parties, registration details, addresses and authorised signatories.
2. Purpose, programme scope and the hierarchy between the term sheet, definitive agreement and canon.
3. Each partner's deliverables, staff time, faculty, technology, host-site or marketing contribution.
4. Decision rights, reserved matters and the authority to sign participant, vendor and host-site instruments.
5. Revenue collection, approved costs, revenue share, taxes, refunds, bad debt and financial reporting.
6. Ownership and licensing of pre-existing IP, newly created curriculum, templates, software, data and trademarks.
7. Brand architecture, approved descriptors, logo licences, claim approval and withdrawal mechanics.
8. Data-controller/processor roles, transfer mechanisms, breach duties, retention and return/deletion on exit.
9. Faculty appointment, consent, conflicts, confidentiality and moral rights.
10. Host-site responsibility, safeguarding, indemnity, insurance and incident escalation.
11. Quality standards, audit rights, complaints, appeals, academic integrity and certificate control.
12. Term, renewal, suspension, termination, transition assistance and treatment of enrolled participants.
13. Confidentiality, publicity, warranties, liability allocation and dispute resolution.
14. Conditions precedent: verified corporate documents, board approvals, insurance evidence, approved affiliation letters and final counsel review.

## Partner response requested

Each organisation should return one controlled evidence bundle containing:

- registration or incorporation document;
- current registered address and authorised representative;
- board resolution, power of attorney or delegation for the proposed signatory;
- approved one-line identity and QIPS role;
- written acceptance or amendment of the term-sheet issues;
- exact permitted public wording and brand assets with usage conditions;
- existing MoUs or agreements relevant to QIPS;
- disclosure of conflicts, regulatory limits and conditions that must be satisfied before signature.

## Exit criteria for W15 research

- QAI is linked to an exact legal entity and current instrument.
- Every named partner has evidence of authority and an agreed programme role.
- Shifa and ISQua have either approved exact wording or are removed from public affiliation claims.
- A negotiation-ready partner agreement can be reviewed by counsel and then submitted to the CCC.
