# QIPS partner operating and claims standard v0.9

Status: negotiation and verification draft; not an agreement or public authorisation

Date: 2026-07-29

## Operating principle

The programme must be able to answer four different questions independently:

1. **Identity:** what exact legal person is involved?
2. **Authority:** who may bind that person and on what evidence?
3. **Contribution:** what does the person promise to supply, by when and at whose cost?
4. **Claim:** what exact public wording and mark use has been approved?

A logo, prior event, historic memorandum, personal affiliation or friendly email answers none of
these questions by itself.

## Partner evidence gate

Each proposed partner receives a controlled evidence request and must return:

- registry evidence, legal name, number, address and governing jurisdiction;
- named authorised representative and evidence of delegation;
- approved one-line identity and QIPS role;
- contribution schedule with owner, acceptance test, date and cost treatment;
- disclosure of conflicts, restrictions and third-party dependencies;
- exact claim wording and mark files with territory, medium, term and withdrawal conditions;
- all current instruments that could govern QIPS; and
- data, confidentiality, IP, insurance and exit requirements.

The W15 lead records received evidence and expiry/review dates. Sensitive instruments remain in a
restricted Drive location; GitHub and Base44 store only metadata, status and links.

## Decision-rights schedule for the definitive agreement

| Matter | Provisional owner | Required approval before action |
|---|---|---|
| Contract with participants and receive payment | SQHN under F014 | Verified legal identity, bank/tax route and authorised signatory |
| Approve canon or public programme promise | CCC | Recorded verdict and minute |
| Approve partner identity, role and mark use | Each named organisation | Written authorised wording/licence |
| Appoint faculty or coaches | [TO CONFIRM: appointing entity] | W05/W06 gate and delegated authority |
| Sign host-site instrument | [TO CONFIRM: authorised entity/person] | Definitive partner agreement, W04 and W17 clearance |
| Own and license pre-existing IP | Originating party | Schedule identifying the asset and permitted use |
| Own or license new programme IP | [TO CONFIRM: agreement decision] | Signed IP schedule and contributor terms |
| Control/process programme data | Purpose-specific parties | W17 controller/processor map and data terms |
| Approve refunds, costs and revenue share | [TO CONFIRM: finance authority] | W16 policy and definitive agreement |
| Suspend delivery for safety/legal risk | Programme director plus W17 escalation | Defined trigger, notification and ratification route |

No provisional owner may act until the corresponding evidence and agreement clause are approved.

## Agreement architecture

Use one definitive multi-party framework only if all parties genuinely share programme-level
obligations. Otherwise use an SQHN master framework plus role-specific schedules. In either form,
the signed set must cover:

1. parties, purpose, term and document hierarchy;
2. contributions, service levels, acceptance and dependencies;
3. governance, reserved matters, deadlock and delegated authorities;
4. cash collection, taxes, approved cost, reporting, refunds and revenue allocation;
5. background and new IP, contributor rights, licences and handover;
6. identity, public claims, mark use, approval time and takedown;
7. faculty, coaching, assessment and host-site responsibilities;
8. data roles, transfers, processors, security, breach and exit handling;
9. safeguarding, incidents, insurance, indemnities and liability allocation;
10. audit, complaints, quality correction and records;
11. confidentiality, conflicts, sanctions and anti-bribery controls; and
12. suspension, termination, teach-out, transition and dispute route.

## Accreditation roadmap rule

The programme may investigate a recognition or accreditation route only after confirming the
service's actual scope, eligibility, evidence burden, lead time, cost, surveillance/renewal and
permitted claims. The investigation itself is not a claim. Individual membership, fellowship or
faculty participation must not be converted into institutional status.

## Exit and continuity gate

Before launch, the agreement must say what happens to enrolled participants, paid funds, records,
platform access, IP, marks, faculty and host-site commitments if any partner withdraws. A mark must
be removable without rebuilding the programme. A critical contribution must have either a tested
replacement route or an explicit stop/go trigger.

## Closure test for Q004, Q005 and Q008

- **Q004:** signed definitive set, verified parties and authorities, revenue/IP/exit terms, and
  named host-site signing authority.
- **Q005:** exact QAI legal identity linked to the relevant instrument and current QIPS role.
- **Q008:** separate authorised Shifa and ISQua responses containing exact permitted wording, or a
  recorded decision to remove the claims.

Until all relevant tests pass, no dossier may claim that the questions are closed.
