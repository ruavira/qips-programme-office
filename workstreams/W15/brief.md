## The question this workstream exists to answer

> Whose name adds credibility, what does each partner actually do, and what is written down?

## What it must produce

- Partner map with role, contribution and value exchange
- Partner agreement: revenue share, IP, exit, signing authority
- Affiliation wording approved in writing by each named body
- Accreditation and recognition roadmap
- Institutional and regulator relationship plan

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Partnership structures in multi-organisation education programmes
2. What affiliation claims are defensible and what invites challenge
3. Accreditation and recognition routes worth pursuing and their lead times
4. Regulator relationships as a demand driver

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- ISQua EEA
- JCI and SafeCare institutional relationships
- University partnership models
