# W16 — Pricing, Finance and Revenue Operations

**Directorate D4 · Business and Governance**
Maps to the owner's original list: *(d) cost, fees*
Proposed cohort-1 position: **NOW**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What does it cost, what do we charge, how does the money actually arrive, and when does it stop needing new money?

## What it must produce

- Published price ladder and payment terms
- Unit economics and breakeven model, kept live
- Payment rails, collections and instalment operations
- Refund, deferral and non-payment policy
- Budget, cash flow and financial controls
- Funding and underwriting strategy with concentration limits

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Value-based pricing evidence from comparable programmes
2. Ability and willingness to pay in each target market
3. Payment rails, FX and collection realities
4. Financing models that have worked and failed in LMIC professional education

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- ISQua income-band ladder
- IHI volume discounts
- WACP multi-currency publishing
- AUC local and foreign rates
