# W16 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

What commercial architecture should be modelled when material costs, demand and contracting terms
remain unresolved?

## Synthesis

The council favoured a governed tier ladder with distinct institutional and individual routes. The
Contrarian required the model to fail closed when inputs are missing. First Principles separated
cost, affordability and recognition. The Executor required transaction-level reconciliation. The
Outsider added transparent eligibility and scholarship rules. The Expansionist required portable
data and annual tier maintenance.

## Conditions

No price recommendation, breakeven claim or public amount is authorised. The next commercial
decision needs verified inputs for indemnity, award, hosts, delivery, platform, payment, tax/FX,
partners, refunds, scholarships, capacity and demand mix.
