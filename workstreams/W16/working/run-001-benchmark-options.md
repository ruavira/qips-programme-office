# W16 run 001 — benchmark and options

## Design principles

- Separate affordability policy from true delivery cost.
- Price by an approved customer/tier rule; never infer purchasing power from nationality alone.
- Show processor, FX, tax and refund effects explicitly.
- Model step costs such as additional coaching groups rather than assuming every cost is linear.
- Withhold a breakeven conclusion while required inputs are missing.

## Options

### A — one global price

Simple to explain and reconcile, but likely weak on access and local purchasing realities.

### B — governed tier ladder with institutional and individual routes

Recommended for modelling. Requires a published eligibility rule, annual tier review, FX policy,
scholarship authority and controls against arbitrary exceptions.

### C — country-by-country prices

Operationally flexible but administratively heavy and more exposed to inconsistency, FX changes and
perceived unfairness.

## Decision boundary

Option B may proceed as a model architecture. No amount proceeds to publication until model checks
pass, Q006 supports demand assumptions and the CCC approves price and policies.
