# W16 commercial architecture and reconciliation

Status: working instrument; not approved for publication

## Commercial layers

1. **Customer:** individual, sponsor or institution.
2. **Eligibility:** objective price-tier and scholarship rules, versioned annually.
3. **Offer:** price, currency, inclusions, tax treatment, validity and capacity condition.
4. **Payment:** approved merchant, route, instalment/deposit, processor fee and FX rule.
5. **Contract:** cancellation, refund, deferral, substitution, non-payment and force-majeure terms.
6. **Reconciliation:** invoice/offer ID, processor reference, gross, fee, tax, FX, net, refund and
   participant/application reference.
7. **Recognition:** award or CPD wording remains separate and cannot be inferred from price.

## Publication gate

Price remains withheld until all required cost inputs have named owners and evidence, the workbook
checks pass, payment/refund/FX/tax rules are approved, merchant onboarding is verified in each
route, Q006 supports a demand range, and the CCC approves the exact amounts and wording.

## Residency and portability

The model is an XLSX/native Google Sheet with formulas and a source register. Base44 may display
approved outputs but must not become the only copy. Personal payment data and processor credentials
never enter GitHub or the general control-room dataset.
