## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What exactly does a graduate hold at the end, who awards it, what did they have to prove to get it, and who recognises it?

## What it must produce

- Assessment strategy and blueprint with standard-setting method
- Capstone rubric and assessor calibration protocol
- Certificate specification: exact title, post-nominals, awarding entity, wording
- CPD and professional-body recognition applications
- Academic integrity, appeals and misconduct policy

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Standard setting and defensible pass marks in professional certification
2. Post-nominals and what earns the right to award them
3. CPD accreditation routes in Nigeria, Ghana, Pakistan and internationally
4. What comparable certificates actually say on the document

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- CPHQ (NAHQ)
- ISQua Fellowship
- ACHS QIL post-nominals
- CPSP and WACP award structures
