# DOSSIER W03-01 — Assessment, Certification and Credentialing
**For decision at the next CCC sitting · Prepared by workstream W03 · Run 1 · Verification: PASS WITH FINDINGS**

## 1. The decision requested

Approve a controlled programme-completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications
for cohort one, with no QIPS post-nominal and no professional-certification claim.

## 2. Why it is on the agenda now

The credential architecture affects the final W02 assessment blueprint, W07 public entry
requirements, W13 naming and W16 price. Deferring the architecture preserves the present circular
dependency and encourages public copy to collapse completion, certification and CPD recognition.
This decision narrows Q002 but does not close it.

## 3. What canon already establishes

- F001: the offer is a 12-month hybrid professional programme.
- F002: the controlled vocabulary applies.
- F006: the programme includes a 40-hour observership.
- F014: SQHN is lead sponsor and contracting entity.
- F021: unconfirmed facts remain visible placeholders.
- F022: individual faculty affiliation is not institutional accreditation or endorsement.

No approved fact grants SQHN professional-certification authority, a post-nominal or regulator
recognition.

## 5. The options

### Option A — controlled completion certificate only

Fastest honest route and lowest recognition administration. Its weakness is a narrower external
signal. Cohort-one feasible; certificate and assessment governance still require costing.

### Option B — completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications

Adds only the recognition a named body grants, profession by profession. It is administratively
fragmented and decisions may arrive on different timelines. Cohort-one feasible and recommended;
application fees and reporting costs remain to be confirmed.

### Option C — governed professional certification

Potentially the strongest later signal. It requires independent governance, standard setting,
security, appeals, public verification, revocation and maintenance. It is a later architecture,
not a cohort-one relabelling exercise.

## 6. Council verdict

All advisors rejected a cohort-one post-nominal and professional-certification claim. Four
favoured Option B; one favoured Option A because regulator applications could distract from
delivery. The clash was resolved by making CPD approvals independently releasable and never a
condition for the completion award. The chairman did not overrule the majority.

## 7. Recommendation

Approve Option B as the cohort-one credential architecture. Approve only the architecture and
claims boundary; keep the exact title, issuer statement, signatories, completion blueprint and
recognition statements unapproved.

## 8. Conditions

1. By 31 August 2026, W02 and W03 present one reconciled completion blueprint.
2. Before the exact certificate is approved, SQHN supplies written awarding authority and named
   authorised signatories.
3. Before any CPD claim appears, the regulator record contains approval identifier, profession,
   jurisdiction, credits, validity dates and exact permitted wording.
4. Before issuance, the verification prototype passes forged-identifier, duplicate-issuance,
   correction, revocation and service-unavailable tests.
5. W15 confirms every partner acknowledgement and logo right before certificate artwork is locked.

## 9. What becomes canon if you approve

1. “Cohort one uses a controlled programme-completion certificate; it is not represented as a
   professional certification.”
2. “Cohort one uses no QIPS post-nominal.”
3. “Any CPD recognition is profession- and jurisdiction-specific and may be published only after
   written approval from the named body.”

No existing fact is superseded.

## 10. What this unblocks

The decision gives W02, W13 and W16 a stable architecture to design against. Those workstreams
remain formally dependent on W03 until the exact award, completion blueprint and authority are
approved. Q002 remains open.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

- Make the public verification record more informative than a decorative paper certificate by
  exposing the standard version and status.
- Use one portable identifier and evidence structure across country-specific CPD approvals.
- Design the completion record so it can survive a future independent certification scheme without
  promising that scheme now.

## 12. What we could not establish

- SQHN's exact awarding authority and signatories.
- The final W02/W03 completion and assessment blueprint.
- Current provider routes for every target profession and country.
- Any approved QIPS CPD credits, certification status or post-nominal.
- The exact award title and partner acknowledgement rights.
