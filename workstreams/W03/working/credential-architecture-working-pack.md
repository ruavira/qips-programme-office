# W03 credential architecture working pack

Status: research recommendation; not canon and not approved public wording

Date: 2026-07-28

## Immediate claims boundary

Until Q002 is closed, public material may say only that successful participants are intended to receive a programme-completion certificate, with the exact title, issuer, signatories, recognition and verification method still subject to approval. It must not use `certified`, `professional certification`, a QIPS post-nominal, CPD/CEU numbers or professional-body recognition as an established fact.

## Four different things that must not be collapsed

1. **Certificate of completion:** evidence that a person met the programme's approved completion requirements.
2. **Professional certification:** a governed assessment scheme that certifies competence against a defined standard, normally with impartiality, verification, complaints/appeals and maintenance controls.
3. **Post-nominal:** letters whose use is authorised and governed by the issuing body; it is not simply an abbreviation chosen for marketing.
4. **CPD recognition:** credits or recognition granted under a named regulator or professional body's rules to a defined professional group.

## Credential architecture options

### Option A — completion certificate only

- Award: a digitally verifiable certificate of programme completion.
- Issuer: SQHN, with partner acknowledgement only where legally and contractually approved.
- Post-nominal: none.
- CPD: no claim until separately approved.
- Case for: lowest overclaim risk and feasible for cohort 1.
- Case against: weaker labour-market signal unless the assessment evidence and verification page are strong.

### Option B — completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications

- Award: the same controlled completion certificate.
- Recognition: submit the programme or relevant activities to selected professional bodies; publish only each approval actually granted, with profession, jurisdiction, credit amount and validity period.
- Post-nominal: none for cohort 1.
- Case for: gives useful recognition without pretending that one regulator represents every participant.
- Case against: administratively fragmented and approvals may arrive on different timelines.

### Option C — governed professional certification with maintenance

- Award: certification against an approved competency standard, potentially with a protected post-nominal.
- Requirements: independent certification governance, defensible assessment blueprint and standard setting, identity/security controls, appeals and misconduct process, public verification, surveillance or recertification, trademark and revocation rules.
- Case for: strongest long-term credential signal.
- Case against: cannot honestly be completed before the standard, assessment system, impartial governance and legal rights exist; unsuitable as an invented cohort-1 shortcut.

## Working recommendation

Adopt Option B for cohort 1: issue a high-integrity programme-completion certificate, apply for profession-specific CPD recognition, and build Option C as a later governed certification pathway. Do not create a post-nominal for cohort 1.

## Title territories for CCC consideration

These are options, not approved wording:

- `Certificate of Completion in Healthcare Quality Improvement and Patient Safety`
- `QIPS Foundation Professional Programme Certificate in Healthcare Quality and Patient Safety`
- `Certificate in Applied Healthcare Quality Improvement and Patient Safety`

Withhold titles beginning `Certified` and any title that implies regulator, university, ISQua or partner endorsement not stated in a signed instrument.

## Proposed completion evidence to reconcile with W02

The final blueprint should state, with weights and pass rules:

- completion of required eLearning and live participation;
- satisfactory monthly artefacts;
- successful capstone proposal, implementation evidence, analysis and presentation;
- completed 40-hour observership and analytical portfolio;
- academic-integrity and conduct compliance;
- any remediation, resubmission and appeal route;
- the rule for missing, deferred or interrupted components.

No component is binding until W02/W03 reconcile the assessment map and the CCC approves it.

## Certificate specification

The controlled certificate should include:

- approved legal name of the recipient;
- exact approved award title;
- issuer's exact legal name and authorised signatory/signatories;
- approved partner acknowledgement, if any;
- award date and cohort;
- unique credential identifier and public verification URL/QR route;
- statement of completed requirements or link to the credential specification;
- version of the programme/assessment standard;
- expiry or maintenance statement only if applicable;
- no unlicensed logo, unsupported accreditation mark or unapproved post-nominal.

## Recognition application map

For each target body, register: profession, jurisdiction, provider eligibility, activity or programme route, application owner, required documents, fee, lead time, credit formula, permitted wording, logo rule, approval period, reporting duty and renewal date.

Applications should begin with bodies matching the actual cohort professions. A blanket `internationally recognised` or `CPD accredited` statement is prohibited.

## Decision inputs required

- CCC selection of Option A, B or C and an exact title territory.
- SQHN legal authority and authorised signatories.
- Final W02/W03 assessment blueprint and completion rules.
- Written partner role and brand permissions from W15.
- Named professional bodies to approach, based on applicant/faculty evidence.
- Owner and system for digital credential verification.

## Exit criteria

Q002 can be recommended for closure only when the exact title, issuer, signatories, completion requirements, post-nominal decision, verification method and every permitted recognition statement are approved and evidenced.
