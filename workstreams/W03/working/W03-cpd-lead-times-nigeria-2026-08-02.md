## (b) Per regulator

### 1. MDCN — Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria

**The only Nigerian health regulator found that publishes an end-to-end service standard.** That makes it
the anchor of the critical path.

> "Accreditation of a Provider shall not take more than three (3) months from the time of initial
> application to the granting of license by the MDCN, and such accreditation shall be for two (2) years
> only and subject to renewal thereafter."
> — [What You Should Know About MDCN CPD Activity](https://www.mdcn.gov.ng/sub-page/education/what-you-should-know-about-mdcn-cpd-activity) [OWN], accessed 2026-08-01

**Credit rules** (same source):
- "A credit unit is equivalent to one (1) contact hour on any particular subject under qualified,
  approved tutelage." Practical training: "a 2-hour engagement should constitute one credit unit."
- "The total credit unit to be obtained annually is 20 and a total of 40 units for biannual license
  renewal."
- Credits must come from **at least three of seven** categories: conferences/seminars/workshops; grand
  rounds or clinical meetings; self-learning e.g. via online; journal clubs/research/publications;
  revision and update courses; **miscellaneous (accreditors, examiners, resource persons)**; management,
  ICT and medical education.
- Exemptions: doctors aged 70+, and documented debilitating illness of 6+ months.

**Online/blended:** "Self-learning activities e.g. via online" and "E-Learning" are named recognised
categories. **No cap or restriction found — NOT FOUND rather than "uncapped".** The pages do not address
the question.

**Faculty credit: yes.** Category 6 is "Miscellaneous: e.g. Accreditors, Examiners, Resource persons."

**Per-activity approval is a separate step from provider licensing:**
> "For any CPD course or activity to be accredited, the nature and content of the said activity as well
> as the Providers and the Resource persons profiles must be sent to the Accreditors for scrutiny and
> approval."

No minimum notice for this second step is published. Material gap: the 3-month standard covers becoming
a provider, not clearing an activity.

**Application route and eligibility** — [Requirements For The Application Of CPD Providers' Form](https://www.mdcn.gov.ng/sub-page/education/requirements-for-the-application-of-cpd-providers-form) [OWN]:
- Online form via the MDCN portal; payments through the applicant profile page.
- Upload: Corporate Affairs Commission certificate; medical degree certificate of the Chief Executive;
  current practising licence of the Chief Executive; credentials (MBBS, full certificate, additional
  qualification, current annual licence) of 3–4 resource persons.
- **Binding eligibility condition:** *"The Chief Executive/Chief promoter of the institution organizing
  CPD must be a registered medical/dental practitioner. In addition, at least three (3) of the resource
  persons must be medically qualified."*

**Fees (NGN, non-refundable — "Any payment made is non-refundable"):**

| Item | Independent provider | Specialty society/association | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application | ₦20,000 | ₦20,000 | one-off |
| Visitation / recognition | ₦1,000,000 **plus transport for three MDCN members** | ₦200,000 | one-off |
| Renewal | ₦250,000 | ₦100,000 | annual |

**INFERENCE (inputs shown):** SQHN is a professional society, and MDCN prices societies at ₦200,000
against ₦1,000,000 — an ₦800,000 difference plus avoided transport for three officials. **Whether SQHN
qualifies under MDCN's definition is not stated anywhere I could find** and must be confirmed in writing.

**Scheme is live:** MDCN publishes a 14-page [List of Recognized CPD Provider Institutions](https://mdcn.gov.ng/public/storage/documents/sub_document_9942220993.pdf) [OWN][PDF] (HTTP 200, 380,989 bytes) naming teaching hospitals,
the National Postgraduate Medical College of Nigeria, and hospital management boards.

**Access problems recorded:** `https://www.mdcn.gov.ng/sub-page/education/how-to-be-mdcn-accredited-cpd-provider`
— the step-by-step application guide — **returned HTTP 500 on two separate attempts** (2026-08-01). No
Wayback snapshot exists (`archive.org/wayback/available` → `"archived_snapshots": {}`). Direct request to
any `www.mdcn.gov.ng` page returns **HTTP 403** behind a Cloudflare JavaScript challenge; readable only
through a JS-capable fetcher.

### 2. NMCN — Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria

Published and detailed on everything **except lead time**.

Source: **Guidelines for Continuing Education Programme for Private Providers** [OWN][PDF], 6 pages, read
in full. Working URL: `https://www.nmcn.gov.ng/docs/GUIDELINES%20FOR%20PRIVATE%20PROVIDERS%20OF%20MCPDP%20IN%20NIGERIA.pdf`

**⚠ [STALE] — 2017 document.** PDF metadata: `CreationDate: Fri Jan 13 16:27:50 2017 UTC`. The naira
figures below are nine-year-old prices still published as current. Treat every fee as requiring written
confirmation.

**Modalities for MCPDP accreditation (verbatim, numbered as in the document):**
1. "Organisations that are Nurse and Midwife driven will be an added advantage"
2. "Certificate of registration with Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)"
3. "30% of Board membership should be made up of Nurse and Midwives who must possess current NMCN license."
4. "The provider shall apply to the Council for approved be commencement." [sic]
5. "A comprehensive document highlighting the contents, resource persons and their qualifications"
6. "Application fee of N50,000.00 (non-refundable)"
7. "Inspection visit will be carried out to the head office of the provider before commencement."
8. "A Council representative shall monitor the conduct of the initial programme"
9. "Certificate of attendance must be signed by the provider and Council's representative"
10. "Approval fee of N250,000.00 shall be paid by the provider to NMCN after approval"
11. "Registration shall last for a period of three years and renewable ... payment of N100,000"
12. **"5% of the total income shall be remitted to the Council."**

Item 12 is a **standing revenue share on programme income**, not a one-off fee.

**Application route:** download the form, "complete and send the **hard copy** with relevant documents of
their organization and evidence of payment of application fee to NMCN." A paper process — add courier
time.

**Credit rules:**
- "One continuing education unit is defined as ten contact hours of participation" — **1 CEU = 10 hours**.
- "a minimum of 6.0 CEU ... within each three-year cycle of licensure."
- **"3 units of these CEU i.e. 30 contact hours must mandatorily be from the Nursing and Midwifery
  organized MCPDP."** A private provider can only ever supply the *other half* of a nurse's requirement.
- "each session or activity must be at least 60 minutes"; non-instructional time excluded.
- Other recognised nursing conferences: max 2 units. ICN/ICM/WACN/NANNM: max 3 units.

**Online/blended: NOT FOUND.** The "Delivery method" section lists only "Lectures, discussions, Practical
demonstrations, Clinical practices, Where possible automated simulation should be used." Online delivery
is neither permitted nor prohibited — genuinely unaddressed. **For a hybrid programme this is the largest
open regulatory question at NMCN.**

**Faculty credit: NOT FOUND.** Silent.

**Certification mechanics:** "Council's logo will be embossed on the certificates ... The Council shall be
a party to the signatories on the attendee certificates." NMCN co-signs. **The CPD attendance certificate
is a separate artefact from the programme award and must be kept visually and legally distinct** — this
interacts directly with the Item 2 decision that the award is a completion certificate, not a
certification.

**Scope covers the subject matter:** "all aspects of clinical nursing and midwifery as well as basic
medical sciences, social sciences and **health management sciences**", with listed sub-specialties
including "**Management/Leadership**" and "EthicoLegal issues."

**LEAD TIME: NOT FOUND.** No service standard, processing time, meeting calendar or deadline anywhere.
What is knowable: **four sequential gates** — hard-copy application + ₦50,000 → physical inspection of the
provider's head office → approval + ₦250,000 → a Council representative attending the initial programme.
Two require scheduling Council officials to travel. **Highest-risk unknown in the Nigerian set.**

**Fees page checked separately:** [NMCN Fees and Charges](https://nmcn.gov.ng/fees-and-charges/) [OWN],
effective **1 September 2021** [STALE], lists only institutional accreditation fees (₦2,250,000 Colleges
of Nursing Sciences; ₦1,968,750 Schools; ₦1,125,000 advisory visit; ₦3,281,250 new colleges). **No MCPDP
line**, so the 2017 guideline figures are the only published provider fees.

### 3. PCN — Pharmacists Council of Nigeria

**No open third-party CPD provider accreditation route could be found.** PCN appears to run MCPD itself,
in numbered cycles.

Strongest evidence is the gazetted fee schedule — a statutory instrument. [Pharmacy Council of Nigeria
Schedule of Fees and Charges Regulations](https://pcn.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Pharmacy-Council-of-Nigeria-Schedule-of-Fees-and-Charges-Regulations.pdf)
[OWN][PDF] (HTTP 200, 8 pages, 1,581,988 bytes). **The PDF is an image scan — `pdftotext` extracted 8
characters.** Rendered at 200 dpi and OCR'd, all 8 pages.

Front matter: *Federal Republic of Nigeria Official Gazette*, Lagos, **17 January 2023**, Vol. 110,
Government Notice No. 97; **S.I. No. 53 of 2023**, made under the Pharmacy Council of Nigeria
(Establishment) Act, No. 31 of 2022. **[STALE]** (2023).

Regulation 6, in full:
> "6. The fees for mandatory Continuing Professional Development for Pharmacist shall be **N20,000**,
> which may either be an **online programme or in a classroom setting**."

Other lines OCR'd: Pharmacy Technicians MCE **₦10,000**; Patent Medicines Shops continuing education
**₦19,000** (new) / **₦10,000** (renewal).

**Every one is participant-side or premises-side. The gazetted schedule contains no application,
accreditation, renewal or levy fee for an organisation seeking MCPD provider status** — even though the
same instrument prices provider-type activities in adjacent areas in detail (Advisory Visit to New
Schools of Health Technology ₦2,000,000; Accreditation visit ₦1,050,000; Re-accreditation ₦1,500,000).

**INFERENCE (inputs shown):** PCN most likely does not operate an open provider-accreditation route.
Inputs: (1) the gazetted schedule prices provider accreditation for pharmacy *schools* but has no MCPD
provider line; (2) PCN's site links to MCPD only via `pcnmcpd.com`, which states "PCN MCPD Cycle 5 has
ended" and Cycle 6 "will soon be available in online format" [OWN] — cycle-based central delivery, not
rolling third-party accreditation; (3) the [Pharmacy Practice Department](https://pcn.gov.ng/about-pharmacy-council-nigeria/department-units/pharmacy-practice-department/)
page says only that the department "coordinates continuing education programmes"; (4) the [Education and
Training Department](https://pcn.gov.ng/about-pharmacy-council-nigeria/department-units/education-and-training-department/)
page describes accreditation of pharmacy *programmes and technician training* only. **Reasoning, not a
found rule.** Settle by direct enquiry.

**One useful positive:** the gazette wording *"which may either be an online programme or in a classroom
setting"* is explicit statutory recognition that MCPD may be delivered online — the clearest
online-delivery permission of any regulator reviewed.

### 4. MLSCN — Medical Laboratory Science Council of Nigeria

**Cleanest published lead time of all five, and it is a hard pre-delivery deadline.**

Source: [CPD Policy](https://old.mlscn.gov.ng/index.php/cpd-policy/) [OWN].

> "Any individual or organization interested in organizing a CPD accredited activity and becoming an
> accredited provider of CPD activities must complete the **Application to Offer a CPD Activity** for each
> CPD activity and **submit it to MLSCN at least two months before the intended start of the event**."

Three structural points: provider accreditation and activity approval are **one combined application**;
it is **per activity**; and the two months is a **minimum notice before delivery**, which is firmer than a
processing estimate.

**Credit rules:** "10 CPD credits during the 2013 calendar year ... for licensure renewal by March 2014."
**[STALE]** — written in 2013/2014 terms, still the published policy. Credits per contact hour: NOT FOUND.

**Online explicitly recognised** — approved activities include "Completion of an AMLSN online eLearning
courses accredited by MLSCN" and "Completion of other online eLearning courses ... approved by MLSCN". No
cap found.

**Faculty credit: yes, broadly** — "Organizing a CPD activity"; "Serving as an invited lecturer/part-time
teacher"; "Serving as an Internal and External examiner; Assessor, Coordinator or Facilitator"; "on-site
inspector for accredited programs".

**Fees: NOT FOUND.**

**Access problems recorded:**
- `https://web.mlscn.gov.ng/...` — **TLS failure: "certificate verify failed: Hostname mismatch"**
  (`ssl_verify_result=1`, `http_code=000`). A compliant client cannot reach it.
- `https://web.mlscn.gov.ng/index.php/registering-as-cpd-provider/` — HTTP 200, but **no body content**:
  navigation, a COVID-19 IVD widget, news items and a fraud disclaimer only. **The page a provider would
  go to in order to apply is empty.**
- A "Guidelines for CPD Providers" navigation item resolves to no URL.

### 5a. Health managers / non-clinical quality professionals — NONE FOUND

**No Nigerian body accredits CPD for health managers or non-clinical healthcare quality professionals.**
Four separate searches (see register).

Closest adjacent: **HRORBN** (Health Records Officers Registration Board of Nigeria). Its [FAQ](https://hrorbn.gov.ng/faq)
[OWN] confirms a CPD requirement exists — "HRORBN requires registered professionals to complete a certain
number of CPD hours every year" — but hours, credit system and whether outside organisations can be
accredited are unpublished. `hrorbn.gov.ng` serves as a JavaScript application. Contact:
info@hrorbn.gov.ng.

**Consequence:** for the non-clinical quality cohort — precisely the audience a QIPS programme is built
for — **there is no Nigerian CPD recognition route to seek.** The completion certificate stands alone for
these participants, and any claim implying otherwise would be unsupportable.

### 5b. MRTB — Medical Rehabilitation Therapists Board *(found incidentally)*

Reported for completeness; rehabilitation therapists are a plausible cohort.
Source: [MRTB CPD Conditionalities](https://www.mrtb.gov.ng/en/Registration/CPD/Conditionalities) [OWN].

- **Notice:** "All documents should have reached the Board **at least 8 weeks before the intended date**"
- **Second deadline:** "Certificates ... **at least 4 weeks before programme commencement**"
- **Fees:** provider registration **₦35,000 annually**; per-programme **₦20,000**; facilitator **₦10,000
  annually**
- **Eligibility:** "CPD Facilitators should comprise of **at least 75% of licensed Medical Rehabilitation
  Therapists**"
- **Annual requirement:** "a cumulative minimum of **30 units**"
- **⚠ ONLINE CAPPED:** "**Not more than 5 CPD Points per year gained from participation from on-line
  activities**". An online-heavy offering delivers at most 5 of 30 points.
- **Duration cap:** "A maximum limit of 3 days ... with exceptions of workshops which have a maximum limit
  of 5 days" — a 12-month programme must be decomposed into ≤3-day units, each applied for 8 weeks ahead.

## (c) Critical path — latest lodgement for a decision before 9 January 2027

Today = 1 August 2026. Cohort 1 = 9 January 2027. Interval = 161 days.

**MDCN — latest lodgement 9 October 2026 (69 days)**
```
Published maximum: 3 months, application → grant of licence
9 January 2027 − 3 months = 9 October 2026
```
*Caveat:* the 3 months buys **provider licensure only**. Activity-level approval is a further step with
no published duration. Lodge earlier.

**MLSCN — latest lodgement 9 November 2026 (100 days)**
```
Published rule: at least 2 months before the intended start of the event
9 January 2027 − 2 months = 9 November 2026
```
Per activity.

**MRTB — documents 14 November 2026 (105 days); certificates 12 December 2026**
```
8 weeks before: 9 Jan 2027 − 56 days = 14 Nov 2026
4 weeks before: 9 Jan 2027 − 28 days = 12 Dec 2026
```

**NMCN — CANNOT BE COMPUTED. NOT FOUND.** No standard, no cycle, no deadline. Four sequential gates, two
requiring officials to travel. **Treat as the binding constraint until the Council states a time in
writing, and lodge it first, not last.**

**PCN — NOT APPLICABLE on current evidence.** No route found to lodge into. The action is an enquiry.

| Route | Latest lodgement | Days from 1 Aug | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MDCN** | **9 Oct 2026** | **69** | Published 3-month maximum |
| MLSCN | 9 Nov 2026 | 100 | Published 2-month pre-event rule |
| MRTB | 14 Nov 2026 | 105 | Published 8-week pre-event rule |
| NMCN | unknown — lodge first | — | No published standard |
| PCN | n/a — enquiry only | — | No route found |

**The Nigerian country decision is gated by 9 October 2026.**

Two observations that sharpen it:

1. **The MDCN deadline collides with applications opening.** Applications open **October 2026**; the last
   safe MDCN lodgement is **9 October 2026**. Same fortnight. If MDCN accreditation is to be truthfully
   described in launch material, the decision must be final in **September 2026 at the latest**, allowing
   time to assemble the evidence pack.

2. **The MDCN eligibility condition may be the real long pole, not the calendar.** MDCN requires the Chief
   Executive of the organising institution to be a **registered medical or dental practitioner**, and at
   least three resource persons to be medically qualified. If the entity that would hold the licence does
   not have a registered practitioner as chief executive, that is a **governance change, not paperwork**,
   and it must be resolved before 9 October. Check against the sponsoring entity's constitution today.

**Recommended sequencing:** lodge **NMCN first** (unbounded, inspection-gated, paper-based), **MDCN by
9 October**, then **MLSCN by 9 November** and **MRTB by 14 November**. Open written enquiries to **PCN**
and **HRORBN** immediately — for both the deliverable is an answer, and a negative answer is itself a
planning input.
