## Claims release rule

For every approval, the programme office must record the body, profession, jurisdiction,
approval identifier, approved activity/programme, credit amount, validity dates, exact permitted
wording, logo rule, reporting obligation, renewal date and source document. Until those fields are
complete, the public claim remains withheld.

## Public verification record specification

The public credential route should disclose only:

- credential identifier;
- participant's approved public name;
- exact award title;
- issuer;
- cohort and award date;
- programme-standard version;
- status: valid, corrected, superseded or revoked;
- CPD recognition only where the individual approval record permits it;
- a privacy contact and correction request route.

Assessment submissions, identity documents, grades, disability/accommodation data, employer
records and disciplinary detail must not appear on the public record.

## Operational controls

1. Generate identifiers independently of email address, name, country or date of birth.
2. Require two-person approval before issuance, correction, replacement or revocation.
3. Preserve an audit event for every status change without exposing restricted evidence publicly.
4. Link the certificate QR code to the canonical verification domain, not directly to Base44.
5. Keep a platform-neutral export so verification can move without changing issued identifiers.
6. Test the route for forged identifiers, altered names, duplicate issuance and unavailable service.
