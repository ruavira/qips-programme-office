## (b) Per body

### 1. Medical and Dental Council, Ghana (MDC) — the best-documented route

**Premises confirmed** on [https://www.mdcghana.org/cpd-policy/](https://www.mdcghana.org/cpd-policy/) [OWN]:
- **"15 credit points per annum from a minimum of 3 events"** for medical and dental practitioners.
  Physician Assistants and Certified Registered Anaesthetists: **"10 credit points per annum from a
  minimum of 3 events"**.
- Online cap, verbatim: **"1 credit/event with certificates (Maximum of 4 credits/year)."**
- Additional constraints not in the brief: **maximum 5 credits per day**; Form B counts **"Max 6 [hours]
  per day"**.
- Ethics requirement within the first year of permanent registration, then every 3 years.

**Version/date: NOT FOUND** — no version number or revision date on the policy or CPD landing page. The
scheme is demonstrably live: [https://www.mdcghana.org/accredited-cpd-programmes/](https://www.mdcghana.org/accredited-cpd-programmes/)
302-redirects to [https://manager.mdcghana.org/api/viewCpdSchedule](https://manager.mdcghana.org/api/viewCpdSchedule) [OWN],
listing **283+ accredited programmes for 2026**, credits 1–33.

**Two-stage application**, forms at [https://www.mdcghana.org/cpd-accreditation-forms/](https://www.mdcghana.org/cpd-accreditation-forms/) [OWN]:
1. **MDC/CPD FORM A — CPD Provider Certification** [PDF]. Certification period **January to December**.
   Fields: provider name/address, organisation type, prior certification, anticipated annual events,
   facilities, contact person. **States no fee, no submission address, no deadline, no processing time.**
2. **MDC/CPD FORM B — Programme Accreditation** [PDF]. Submitted to **The Registrar, Medical and Dental
   Council, P.O. Box AN 10586, Accra**.
3. A **CPD Renewal Form** is also listed.

**Evidence required with Form B:** full meeting programme with hourly breakdown; complete speaker list
with credentials, institutional affiliations and speaking experience; five numbered learning objectives
using measurable action verbs; teaching methods; evaluation methodology; commitment to keep attendance
records, issue certificates and distribute evaluation forms; confirmation the Guidelines have been read;
"all form sections and required fee."

**Fees** — [https://www.mdcghana.org/fees-and-charges/](https://www.mdcghana.org/fees-and-charges/) [OWN]:
- **CPD Provider Accreditation — GHS 1,645.00** (annual, since certification renews annually)
- **CPD Programme (first 2) — GHS 823.00**
- **CPD Additional Programme — GHS 164.00**
- **No effective date or fee year published** — NOT FOUND. Page last-modified 2026-01-27.

**LEAD TIME.** The only published timing rule: **"submit their applications 3 months before the proposed
date of the event."** **No service standard for the decision itself — NOT FOUND.** The rolling 283-programme
2026 schedule is consistent with administrative rather than committee decision-making, but that is not
stated.

**Before delivery?** Yes: **"NOT advertise programmes before accreditation is obtained."** Post-event:
"submit a report to Council within 4 weeks", and give attendees "verifiable documentary evidence of
attendance stating the approved credit." Council inspectors may attend as observers.

**Eligibility.** "Any institution(s) or individual(s) who has the capacity to organise CPD programmes"
meeting Council criteria, requiring "prior accreditation by Council, renewable annually." The 2022
providers list ([https://www.mdcghana.org/accredited-cpd-providers/](https://www.mdcghana.org/accredited-cpd-providers/) [OWN] **[STALE]**)
shows 45 providers and establishes that **foreign and non-Ghanaian bodies are accreditable** — it includes
**MOTEC LIFE-UK** and the **World Continuing Education Alliance**, alongside GCPS, KNUST, University of
Ghana Medical Centre, Ghana Health Service divisions and 29 hospitals.

**⚠ The largest MDC risk — audience eligibility.** Form B states verbatim:
> **"events aimed primarily at training grade physicians, residents or non medical health professionals
> do not qualify for verifiable CPD credit approval."**

A multi-professional QIPS cohort dominated by nurses, pharmacists and laboratory scientists risks being
read as "aimed primarily at non medical health professionals" and refused verifiable credit. **This is a
programme-design constraint, not a paperwork constraint**, and it needs a written ruling from the
Registrar before Ghana is committed.

**Teaching credit:** "Resource Person at accredited event 1 credit/paper (In addition to programme
credit)."

**Non-clinical content is accreditable:** Form B's non-clinical list includes **Education & Training,
Health Service Policy/Management, Ethics**.

### 3. Pharmacy Council of Ghana (PC) — the hardest calendar

**Mandatory, double gate.** [https://pcghana.org/service/continuous-professional-development/](https://pcghana.org/service/continuous-professional-development/) [OWN]:
"Each program must be accredited by the Council in order for the Pharmacist to receive credit." The
[CPD Policy and Guidelines](https://testsite.pcghana.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CPD-POLICY-GUIDELINES-FOR-PHARMACISTS.pdf) [PDF],
linked from [https://pcghana.org/downloads-3/](https://pcghana.org/downloads-3/), is explicit:
> **"All institutions, agencies and firms desiring to run CPD programmes must be registered as providers
> and the programme they intend to deliver must also be accredited by the PC."**

No date or version; file path `/2021/02/` — **[STALE]** on that basis.

**🔴 LEAD TIME — the decisive finding:**
> **"The CPD Technical Committee shall meet thrice every year… within the months of February, June and
> October."**

Accreditation decisions are **meeting-gated three times a year**. **No published papers-closing date —
NOT FOUND.**

**Before delivery?** Yes: "Providers MUST NOT advertise programmes before accreditation is obtained."

**Credit rules.** Minimum **10 credits annually**, ≥7 from Categories 1 & 2, ≥3 from Category 3, including
at least one Law and Ethics programme. Per-event ceilings: Cat 1 up to 3; Cat 2 up to 5; Cat 3 up to 3.
"A practitioner's license may not be renewed if he/she fails to meet the minimum 10 credits." Credits per
contact hour: NOT FOUND.

**ONLINE CAP:** "Online quizzes with certificates: 1 credit/event (Maximum of 25% of total credits
required/year)" — 2.5 of 10.

**Teaching credit:** "Facilitators at approved CPD programme: up to 3 credits/event."

**Forms** (from [https://pcghana.org/downloads-3/](https://pcghana.org/downloads-3/) [OWN]): CPD Provider
Accreditation Form [PDF] — certification period **January to December**; decision signed off by the **Head
of CPD Committee**; states no fee, address, deadline or processing time. Application for CPD Program
Accreditation [PDF] and Reporting Template [PDF] also listed. Submission: **Registrar, Pharmacy Council,
P.O. Box AN 10344, Accra North**.

**Fees: NOT FOUND.** The CPD page says only "pay the required fee". The published schedule at
[https://oldwebsite.pcghana.org/fees-payments/](https://oldwebsite.pcghana.org/fees-payments/) [OWN] lists
30+ fees (pharmacist relicensure GHS 200; new facility licence GHS 1,000) and **contains no CPD
accreditation line at all**; it states "fees can be changed without notice" and carries no effective date.
[https://pcghana.org/fees-and-charges/](https://pcghana.org/fees-and-charges/) rendered without its fee
tables — recorded as unreadable.

**A separate online-platform route, undocumented.** [https://pcghana.org/service/online-cpd/](https://pcghana.org/service/online-cpd/) [OWN]
describes an API scheme — Council registers the platform, generates credentials, accredits topics, issues
topic IDs, attendance posted to `https://manager.pcghana.org/api_cpd_online/submitattendance`. But it
states verbatim: **"Getting accreditation for your website as an Online CPD platform - Instructions coming
soon."** For a mostly-online programme this is the route that matters most, and it is a blank.

### 4. Allied Health Professions Council (AHPC) — the commercial risk

**Source:** "AHPC CPD Protocols and Guidelines", **© AHPC 2022 [STALE]** [PDF]. Not currently reachable on
ahpc.gov.gh; two identical third-party copies: [staag.org.gh](https://staag.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/AHPC-CPD-PROTOCOLS-AND-GUIDELINES.pdf) [3P],
[gsmpghana.org](https://gsmpghana.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/AHPC-CPD-PROTOCOLS-AND-GUIDELINES.pdf) [3P].

Mandatory; covers medical laboratory science among others (AHPC established by Act 857, 2013). Provider
accreditation is a listed service at [https://ahpc.gov.gh/services/](https://ahpc.gov.gh/services/) [OWN].

**Annual requirement:** **20 credit points** (practitioners), **15** (technicians), **10** (assistants).

**ONLINE CAP:** "Completion of online eLearning course: 1 point per course event (max. 4 per year)", and
the course must be "recognized or approved or accredited by Council". **Same 4-per-year structure as
MDC — but against 20 points it is a 20% ceiling.**

**🔴 LEAD TIME — two gates that stack.** Flowchart (Figure 1): submission of CPD application **"in the last
quarter of the year"** → payment of processing fees → approval and issuing of approval letter → points
awarded → programme runs → **"payment of quota of proceeds to AHPC"** + certified participant list →
practitioner logs points → Council verifies. And separately: **"CPD applications should be submitted three
(3) months prior to the execution of the programme."** Meeting frequency and turnaround: **NOT FOUND.**

**🔴 FEES AND REVENUE SHARE.** Amounts **NOT FOUND**, but the structure is explicit:
- institutions must "pay an annual accreditation fee approved by Council"
- organisations must "pay for the CPD point awarded by the Council including the application fees"
- collaborative arrangements: "remit a percentage of such workshop fees paid by participants to Council"
- **"50% of gross proceeds"** where Council fully facilitates; **"25% of gross proceeds"** where Council
  provides certificates/supervisor
- "Professional Bodies etc. should charge a reasonably low rate"

**A 25–50% gross-proceeds levy is a pricing decision, not an administrative one.** Whether SQHN's
arrangement falls into either bracket is not determinable from the document — **NOT FOUND**. It must be
resolved before, not after, any application, and it interacts directly with F024.

**Submission:** Registrar, AHPC, **PO Box KB 943, Korle Bu, Accra**; **registrar@ahpcghana.org**.

**Teaching credit:** facilitators receive "extra point in addition to the allocated points"; trainers 3
per activity (max 3/yr); publications 3 (max 3/yr); preceptorship 3 / 5 / 10 points by duration.

**⚠ Conflicting AHPC document.** An undated "CPD Guidelines – Allied Health Professionals" [PDF](https://ces-assets.s3.amazonaws.com/public/documents/CPD-Guidelines-Allied-Health-Professionals.pdf) [3P]
states **10–12 CPD points per year**, irreconcilable with 20/15/10. Which is current: **NOT FOUND**. The
Ghana Society for Medical Physics ([link](https://gsmpghana.org/continuing-professional-development-cpd/) [3P])
states 20 points, supporting the 2022 protocols — but that is a society page, not the Council.

**Precedent that the route works for an external institute:** "ERDSI Officially Accredited by AHPC to Run
CPD Programs", **30 November 2025** ([link](https://erdsi.org/2025/11/30/erdsi-officially-accreditated-by-ahpc-to-run-cpd-programs-a-milestone-for-professional-development-in-ghana-and-beyond/) [3P])
— a non-hospital research/training institute obtained AHPC provider accreditation. No process detail,
fees, dates or duration disclosed.

**Blocked:** [https://ahpc.gov.gh/accreditation/](https://ahpc.gov.gh/accreditation/) → **HTTP 404**.
[https://ahpc.gov.gh/cpd/](https://ahpc.gov.gh/cpd/) → content-free landing page.

### 5. Ghana College of Physicians and Surgeons — a partner, not a route

**GCPS does not accredit external CPD providers.** Decisive evidence: GCPS **appears on the MDC's own list
of accredited CPD providers** ([link](https://www.mdcghana.org/accredited-cpd-providers/) [OWN], 2022
**[STALE]**), alongside the Ghana Medical Association, the West African College of Physicians, KNUST and
29 hospitals. It sits inside the MDC scheme; it does not operate one.

What GCPS does operate ([https://gcps.edu.gh/](https://gcps.edu.gh/) [OWN]): a virtual lectures LMS at
`lms.gcps.edu.gh`; a Faculty Development Programme; a peer-reviewed journal; a library; a mandatory
Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis course for residents; and a collaboration with the Royal College of
Pathologists and THET producing 24 video-based lectures.

**Implication:** GCPS is a **delivery, co-badging and faculty-supply partner**, and an already-accredited
MDC provider that could in principle host a jointly-run programme. But a GCPS-organised event would still
require **MDC Form B programme accreditation**. Using GCPS removes no regulatory step — it may remove the
Form A provider-certification step and its GHS 1,645 fee, if GCPS applies as provider of record. Terms and
timeline: **NOT FOUND**.

**Nursing analogue:** the **Ghana College of Nurses and Midwives** runs "Open Pathways" CPD open to
"practicing healthcare professionals", awarding "relevant CPD Credit Points", including **"Introduction to
Patient Safety"** and **"Clinical Leadership"** ([link](https://gcnm.edu.gh/cpd-pathways/) [OWN]). Given
how thin the N&MC evidence is, this may be the more practical nursing pathway. Terms: NOT FOUND
(info@gcnm.edu.gh).

### 6. Health managers and non-clinical quality professionals — no route

**NOT FOUND: no Ghanaian body accredits CPD for health managers or non-clinical quality professionals.**
Ghana's health regulators are constituted profession-by-profession under the **Health Professions
Regulatory Bodies Act, 2013 (Act 857)**. A hospital quality manager holding no clinical registration falls
outside all of them.

The **Association of Health Service Administrators, Ghana** exists and runs a "Continuing Professional
Education Programme" ([https://ahsag.org.gh/](https://ahsag.org.gh/) [OWN]) — but it is a **voluntary
association, not a regulator**, and no evidence was found that it accredits third-party CPD. Its
conference/CPE page returned **HTTP 404**.

**The workaround is real and evidenced.** Two findings show quality/management content is accreditable
through the *clinical* councils:
1. **MDC Form B** offers non-clinical categories: "Education & Training, Health Service Policy/Management,
   Ethics, Other."
2. **SafeCare precedent:** "Health staff in Ghana now receive CPD points through SafeCare training and
   tools" ([link](https://www.safe-care.org/health-staff-in-ghana-now-receive-cpd-points-through-safecare-training-and-tools/) [3P])
   records that **all four councils — MDC, AHPC, Pharmacy Council and N&MC — recognised CPD points** for
   SafeCare assessor training and an **"Introduction to Quality Improvement in Healthcare"** course. This
   is the closest analogue to the SQHN programme that exists in Ghana. No publication date, process,
   timeline or fee detail — NOT FOUND on all four.

**Aggregator route.** **Continuous Education Services (CES)**, [https://www.ces.edu.gh/](https://www.ces.edu.gh/) [OWN],
is a Ghanaian platform **accredited by the Pharmacy Council, MDC, AHPC and N&MC**. It offers "Custom-Made
Courses" and a [Become a Facilitator](https://www.ces.edu.gh/become-a-facilitator) route stating it will
"ensure that your work goes through all regulatory requirements where necessary" — but publishes **no
requirements, timeline, fees or revenue share**, directing enquirers to phone/WhatsApp or
support@ces.edu.gh. **WCEA** likewise holds accreditation with MDC (2022 list) and N&MC (Feb 2024 meeting).

**INFERENCE** (inputs: CES and WCEA both already hold accreditation from all four councils; both offer
third-party course hosting; neither publishes terms): riding an existing accredited platform is the **only
route found that could plausibly compress lead time below the councils' own 3-month notice rules**, because
provider-level accreditation already exists. Untested and unpriced. **The highest-value enquiry to make
this week.**

### The date the Ghana in/out decision must be taken

**Inputs (all found):** Pharmacy Council practical deadline **30 Sep 2026**; AHPC window opens **1 Oct
2026**; MDC and AHPC 3-month rule gives **9 Oct 2026**. MDC Form B requires a full hourly programme, a

**INFERENCE — pack-preparation allowance:** 4 weeks to assemble regulator-ready documentation across four

> **The Ghana decision must be taken by ~1 September 2026.**

If curriculum and named faculty are **not** fixed, that date moves earlier — MDC Form B cannot be completed
without a confirmed hourly programme and named speakers with credentials.

### The design constraint that outranks all of the above

| Body | Annual requirement | Max from online | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| MDC (doctors/dentists) | 15 credits | 4 | **27%** |
| MDC (PAs/CRAs) | 10 credits | 4 | **40%** |
| AHPC (practitioners) | 20 points | 4 | **20%** |
| Pharmacy Council | 10 credits | 25% | **25%** |
| N&MC (staff nurse, July 2020 [STALE]) | 20 points | 10 | **50%** |

**No Ghanaian regulator except possibly N&MC will let an online programme satisfy even half of a
practitioner's annual CPD obligation.** A mostly-online programme cannot be marketed in Ghana as *meeting*
annual CPD requirements. It can only be positioned as a *contribution*.

**The one lever that might change this. INFERENCE**, inputs: (a) MDC's cap is written against "online
courses / distance learning with certificates" at 1 credit/event; (b) AHPC's against "completion of online
eLearning course"; both phrasings describe **asynchronous self-paced** courses; (c) MDC's live 2026
schedule includes entries titled "Webinar Series" carrying ordinary programme credits, and MDC awards up
to 5 credits and counts 6 hours per day. Reasoning: **live synchronous virtual sessions may be accredited
as ordinary events at ordinary rates, entirely outside the asynchronous cap.** If correct, making the
credit-bearing component the **live cohort session** rather than the self-paced module transforms the
Ghanaian value proposition — and F003 already contains exactly such a session every month.

**Inference, not a found rule.** Getting it in writing from MDC and AHPC is worth more than any other
single action, and should be asked *before* 1 September, because the answer may change the programme
design the applications describe.
