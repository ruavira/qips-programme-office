# W03 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-28

## Framed question

Which credential architecture should cohort one use while the exact assessment blueprint,
professional-body approvals and partner rights remain unresolved?

## Advisor positions

### Contrarian

Choose a completion certificate only. Profession-specific CPD applications create administrative
fragmentation and a marketing temptation before the programme has proved delivery. Build a strong
verification record first.

**Strongest objection to this position:** it may underuse legitimate regulator routes that would
make the award more useful to participants without turning it into a certification.

### First Principles

Choose a controlled completion certificate plus separate CPD applications. A completion award
answers what the participant did; a regulator approval answers whether a named profession grants
credits. Keeping those decisions separate is the cleanest logical architecture.

**Strongest objection to this position:** applicants may hear the aggregate phrase “CPD
applications” as if approval is already assured unless every public claim is released one body at
a time.

### Executor

Choose the same architecture, but do not wait for every jurisdiction before starting the
application work. Freeze the certificate fields, name one owner, assemble a reusable evidence
pack and track each regulator route independently. Use no post-nominal.

**Strongest objection to this position:** the assessment blueprint and authorised signatories are
not yet settled, so an early application may need material revision.

### Outsider

Treat the public verification record as the product, not the paper certificate. Publish the
standard version, requirements met and current status. This creates portable evidence without
borrowing the status of a college fellowship or professional certification.

**Strongest objection to this position:** a verification page cannot compensate for an unclear
award title or weak completion standard.

### Expansionist

Use the cohort-one completion certificate as the first layer of a later certification pathway.
Design identifiers, revocation and governance so they can survive a future independent scheme,
but do not announce that scheme as committed.

**Strongest objection to this position:** designing for a future certification can add cost and
complexity before demand and authority exist.

## Convergence and clash

All advisors rejected a cohort-one post-nominal and rejected calling programme completion a
professional certification. Four favoured profession-specific CPD applications; the Contrarian
favoured stopping at the completion award. The real disagreement was sequencing, not the claims
boundary.

## Chairman's synthesis

Advance **Option B: controlled programme-completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD
applications**. The Contrarian's concern becomes a release control: no CPD statement appears
until the named body grants it in writing, and no delayed application holds up an otherwise
approved completion award.

The chairman did not overrule the majority.

## Conditions

1. CCC approval is limited to the architecture, not an exact title, issuer statement, signatory
   set, credit quantity or post-nominal.
2. W02 and W03 must approve one completion blueprint before the award title is finalised.
3. SQHN must confirm awarding authority and authorised signatories.
4. W15 must confirm partner acknowledgement and logo rights.
5. Every CPD claim remains withheld until its regulator record is complete.
6. The verification record must remain portable outside Base44.
