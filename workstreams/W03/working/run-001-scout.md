## Human inputs that cannot be invented

- The legal awarding entity and authorised signatories.
- Whether the CCC intends a completion certificate, a certification scheme or a staged pathway between them.
- Approval to create any post-nominal.
- Regulator decisions on CPD credit or recognition.
- Final assessment blueprint and pass requirements from W02/W03 approval.

## Exit criteria for scouting

- The current certificate claims are classified as allowed, conditional or prohibited.
- At least three defensible credential architectures can be compared.
- The evidence and application route for each requested recognition is explicit.
- No marketing dependency is released on the basis of an unapproved title or recognition claim.
