Status: working analysis; not a credential decision or public claim

## What the comparison establishes

The comparators do not support a single continuum from attendance certificate to certification.
They show different governed products with different authorities, assessment methods and
maintenance obligations. QIPS therefore needs a deliberately named completion award for cohort
one and a separately governed future decision if it wishes to become a certification scheme.

## Options

### Option A — controlled completion certificate only

- **What it is:** one digitally verifiable certificate issued after the approved completion rules are met.
- **Case for:** fastest honest route; one claims standard; lowest regulator and maintenance burden.
- **Case against:** professional recognition remains limited to the evidence printed or linked to the certificate.
- **Cost:** certificate design, recipient verification, assessment governance and an appeals route; amounts remain to be costed.
- **Timing:** feasible for cohort one.

### Option B — controlled completion certificate plus profession-specific CPD applications

- **What it is:** Option A plus separate applications to relevant professional bodies after the intended cohort professions and provider routes are confirmed.
- **Case for:** adds useful recognition where a named regulator grants it without implying one approval covers every participant.
- **Case against:** different professions and countries may approve different credits, wording and periods; some decisions may arrive after recruitment begins.
- **Cost:** application fees, evidence assembly, reporting and renewal administration; no amount is assumed until each body confirms it.
- **Timing:** recommended for cohort one, with public claims released one approval at a time.

### Option C — governed QIPS professional certification

- **What it is:** an independent certification scheme with a competency standard, defensible examination/assessment, impartial decision rights, identity/security controls, complaints and appeals, public verification, revocation and maintenance.
- **Case for:** strongest potential long-term labour-market signal and a possible protected post-nominal.
- **Case against:** the programme currently lacks the independent governance, standard-setting evidence, legal rights and maintenance system needed to support the claim.
- **Cost:** new governance, psychometric/assessment expertise, legal protection, technology and continuing surveillance; materially higher than a completion award.
- **Timing:** later architecture, not a cohort-one relabelling exercise.

## Working recommendation

Advance Option B to council testing. Its minimum non-negotiable conditions are an exact award
title, SQHN awarding authority, authorised signatories, an approved W02/W03 completion map,
unique verification identifiers, an appeal/correction route, and publication of CPD recognition
only after written approval from the named body.

## Unresolved benchmark questions

- No source establishes that SQHN presently has authority to grant a professional certification or protected post-nominal.
- No source establishes that QIPS is approved for CPD by any regulator or profession.
- The applicable nursing, pharmacy and allied-health provider application routes are incomplete and must be confirmed directly with each body.
- R004 still requires the CCC to approve the staged W02 spine → W03 architecture → W02 final blueprint dependency.
