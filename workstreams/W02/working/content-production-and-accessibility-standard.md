# W02 content production and accessibility standard

Status: working standard for prototype production

## Source and claims controls

- Every clinical, safety, policy, standards and quantitative claim carries a source and access date.
- Country examples identify what transfers and what requires local confirmation.
- External frameworks are paraphrased or used under permission; protected diagrams and marks are
  not copied merely because they are educational.
- Content distinguishes evidence, local policy, programme rule, professional judgement and example.
- Patient-level or employer-confidential information never appears in examples; use synthetic data.

## Monthly package

One overview, measurable outcomes, accessible eLearning segments, downloadable low-bandwidth
alternative, worked example, practice, live-session plan, coaching pre-read, artefact brief, rubric,
source list and revision record. The artefact must be feasible before production is approved.

## Accessibility floor

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA for digital content and forms. Provide semantic headings, keyboard access,
visible focus, sufficient contrast, text alternatives, captions/transcripts, descriptive links,
clear error messages, accessible authentication and no meaning conveyed only by colour. Documents
use real headings, tagged tables with header rows, reading order and plain language. Timed activity,
audio/video and synchronous participation have an equivalent route or documented reasonable-
adjustment process.

Automated checks are necessary but insufficient; a keyboard/screen-reader and human content review
is recorded for every release candidate.

## Connectivity and language

- Core content works on a phone and is downloadable in bounded files.
- No required understanding depends on streaming video alone.
- Transcripts and key tools are printable; submissions tolerate intermittent connectivity.
- Public and learning copy uses programme vocabulary from `canon/glossary.md`.
- English is the working language only when approved; language/support needs must be tested in W01.

## Quality gate

Nothing releases without content-owner approval, source check, instructional-design review,
accessibility record, technical test, artefact/rubric alignment, claims check and version identifier.
