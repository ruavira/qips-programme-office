# QIPS programme blueprint v0.9

Status: reconstructed working blueprint; not approved for publication or production lock

## Outcome architecture

By completion, a participant should be able to:

1. diagnose a quality or safety problem in its system and stakeholder context;
2. define a process, aim, measures and ethical data plan;
3. analyse causes, risks, human factors and equity implications;
4. design, test and adapt changes through disciplined rapid cycles;
5. interpret variation and communicate evidence without overstating causality;
6. lead a psychologically safe, patient-centred and accountable improvement process;
7. assess governance, standards and readiness without collapsing compliance into improvement;
8. sustain, spread and hand over work with a traceable portfolio.

These are working outcomes. W01 task evidence, W03 completion architecture and human calibration
must establish final level, performance conditions and assessment standard.

## Workload ledger

The approved total is about 228 hours. A provisional reconciliation is 96 hours of eLearning and
independent practice, 24 hours of live faculty work, 24 hours of coaching preparation/participation,
44 hours of artefact and capstone work, and 40 observership hours. This adds to 228 and leaves about
188 hours outside the observership—approximately 3.9 hours per week across 48 ordinary weeks.

The allocation is a planning hypothesis, not a public promise. Each monthly design record must
replace estimates with timed prototypes and keep the total reconciled.

## Evidence ledger per month

Each month records outcome, source, content owner, examples by country/setting, eLearning duration,
live plan, practice, artefact specification, rubric link, accessibility check, faculty/coach role,
required prior knowledge, data/ethics risk and revision history.
