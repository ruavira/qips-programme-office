# W02 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

Which architecture should govern the 26 inherited topics and twelve monthly artefacts?

## Synthesis

The council chose **backward mapping from the twelve artefacts**. The Contrarian rejected topic
coverage as proof of capability. First Principles required prerequisites and performance evidence.
The Executor required one production record and acceptance gate per month. The Outsider protected
low-bandwidth and reasonable-adjustment routes. The Expansionist retained standards, leadership and
career/spread capability without making the programme a certification scheme.

## Conditions

W01 task evidence and W03 completion governance must calibrate final level and rubrics. Monthly
timing must be prototyped against the 228-hour ledger. No content production proceeds more than two
months ahead without the preceding artefact and rubric passing alignment review.
