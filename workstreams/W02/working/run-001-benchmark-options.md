# W02 run 001 — benchmark and options

## Benchmark

| Comparator | What it does | QIPS difference | Why better, not cheaper |
|---|---|---|---|
| IHI Improvement Advisor | Structured learning units, action periods and ongoing support around real work | One visible artefact every month with pre-read coaching | Converts a long applied journey into recoverable monthly evidence |
| ACHS QIL | Real work-based improvement project with coaching and peer community | Add a governed observership portfolio and twelve-step capstone ledger | Makes systems observation and project evidence separately assessable |
| WHO guide | Eleven multi-professional patient-safety topics plus educator guidance | Integrate safety with quality governance, measurement and implementation | Builds cross-functional capability while retaining safety foundations |
| NAHQ framework | Eight domains and tiered skills | Crosswalk outcomes after W01 task evidence rather than copying a framework | Prevents external prestige from substituting for local role analysis |

## Options

### A — teach all 26 topics in brochure order

High content coverage but weak dependency control; data and project work arrive too late.

### B — backward-map twelve artefacts to competencies and topics

Recommended. Every month produces one capstone component; topics arrive when the artefact needs
them, and the observership is a distinct portfolio.

### C — reduce the offer to a capstone studio

Applied but too narrow for people without a shared safety, systems and measurement foundation.
