# DOSSIER W02-01 — Curriculum and Instructional Design
**For decision on the continuous CCC docket · Prepared by workstream W02 · Run 2 · Verification: PASS WITH FINDINGS**

## 1. The decision requested

Approve the v0.9 backward-mapped architecture as the controlled prototype spine and authorize
months 1–2 prototyping. Do not approve final outcomes, rubrics, award requirements, production
lock or publication.

## 2. Why it is on the agenda now

The inherited architecture file could not be located. W03, W04, W05 and content production need a
traceable programme spine, while the existing W02–W03 state created a circular dependency. A
prototype decision allows evidence-generating work to proceed without misrepresenting the design
as final.

## 3. What canon already establishes

- The offer is a 12-month hybrid professional programme.
- Monthly learning combines independent work, live interaction, coaching and a recoverable artefact.
- A 40-hour observership is separate from the capstone.
- Unapproved claims, titles, prices, faculty and recognition remain placeholders.

## 4. Benchmark

Applied quality-improvement programmes commonly combine structured learning, real projects,
feedback and portfolio evidence. WHO and NAHQ frameworks support broad capability crosswalks but
do not determine the final local competency level, assessment threshold or country adaptation.

## 5. The options

### Option A — topic-order curriculum
Simple to schedule but weakly connected to applied evidence and completion decisions.

### Option B — backward mapping from twelve learner artefacts
Creates one evidence spine across learning, coaching, observership and assessment. Recommended.

### Option C — capstone-led studio
Strongly applied but too dependent on unresolved host, faculty and assessment capacity for the
first production wave.

## 6. Council verdict

Choose Option B, reconcile the monthly workload and require a monthly acceptance gate. Prototype
months 1–2 first and use the results to calibrate the remaining ten months.

## 7. Recommendation

Approve Option B for controlled prototype design. Authorize W02 to produce and test the month 1
and month 2 learning maps, eLearning outlines, live-session plans, artefact briefs, draft rubrics,
accessibility equivalents and timing logs.

## 8. Conditions

1. Prototype outputs remain DRAFT and may not be marketed or published as the final programme.
2. W01 human-task evidence and W03 completion governance are required before final calibration.
3. Every prototype records participant time, faculty preparation time, coach review time,
   accessibility findings and unresolved assumptions.
4. Source, copyright, intellectual-property and synthetic-data checks precede production lock.
5. Country adaptation for Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan is explicit rather than implied.
6. Any material divergence between GitHub, Base44, Drive or a deployment surface is recorded in
   `docs/workspace-synchronization-register.md` and resolved against GitHub.

## 9. What becomes canon if you approve

Nothing. This is a prototype authorization and architecture decision, not a canon promotion.

## 10. What this unblocks

- W04 observership outcome alignment.
- W05 faculty-slot and preparation briefs.
- Controlled months 1–2 content prototyping.
- Evidence for the final W02/W03 completion blueprint.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

- One learner evidence ledger across twelve months.
- Artefact-first sequencing rather than topic accumulation.
- Low-bandwidth equivalents designed alongside, not after, the primary experience.
- A reusable production-control pattern for later months and future programmes.

## 12. What we could not establish

- The contents of the missing original architecture source.
- Final competency levels, rubrics and completion thresholds.
- Timed participant, faculty and coaching feasibility.
- Final country adaptations and publication-ready accessibility.

## 13. Verification report

Verdict: PASS WITH FINDINGS; no blocking finding prevents controlled months 1–2 prototyping.
Verification does not authorize final production, publication, award claims or completion rules.
