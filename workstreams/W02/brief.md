# W02 — Curriculum and Instructional Design

**Directorate D1 · Programme**
Maps to the owner's original list: *(b) full instructional design*
Proposed cohort-1 position: **NOW**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What must a graduate be able to do, and what sequence of learning, practice and feedback reliably gets them there in twelve months?

## What it must produce

- Competency framework mapped to an external standard
- Month-by-month curriculum map with the artefact ladder
- Learning design specification per month (eLearning, live, coaching, artefact)
- Content production plan and authoring standards
- Accessibility and language standard

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Competency frameworks in healthcare quality (IHI, ISQua, NAHQ, CPHQ blueprint)
2. Instructional design evidence for long-duration blended professional programmes
3. Workplace-based project learning and what makes capstones fail
4. Cognitive load and completion evidence for working adults at 4 hours a week

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI Improvement Advisor
- ACHS Quality Improvement Lead
- AUC Hospital Management Diploma
- Health Foundation GenerationQ
