## The question this workstream exists to answer

> How do we know the programme is any good, and how would we find out if it were not?

## What it must produce

- Programme evaluation framework with an explicit outcome model
- Participant, faculty and host site feedback instruments
- Internal quality assurance cycle and annual programme review
- External review and accreditation readiness path
- Continuous improvement log tied to the fact registry

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Programme evaluation models for professional education
2. Outcome measurement beyond satisfaction in quality improvement training
3. External evaluation and accreditation of education programmes
4. How comparable programmes publish their own results

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- ISQua EEA education standards
- Kirkpatrick and Moore frameworks in CPD
- IHI programme evaluation practice
