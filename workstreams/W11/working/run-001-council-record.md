# W11 run 001 — council record

## Options considered

| Option | Advantage | Failure mode | Council position |
|---|---|---|---|
| End-of-programme satisfaction survey | Cheap and familiar | Cannot establish capability, transfer or results | Reject as sole evaluation |
| Maximal research dataset from cohort 1 | Rich evidence | Excess burden, unclear lawful basis and false precision | Reject |
| Layered minimum dataset with delayed transfer and governed project evidence | Decision-useful and proportionate | Requires coordination and follow-up discipline | Recommend |

## Chairman synthesis

Quality assurance must govern the programme while it runs, not audit it after the fact. Measures exist to trigger decisions. No percentage target should be invented before baseline and pilot evidence.

## Conditions

- W12 approves fields and uses before collection.
- W03 provides valid assessment evidence and moderation design.
- W02 provides final outcome/artefact mapping.
- Any external-evaluation or accreditation statement requires a named body and written scope.
