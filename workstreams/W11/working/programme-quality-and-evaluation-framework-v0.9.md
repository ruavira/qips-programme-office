# W11 programme quality and evaluation framework v0.9

Status: verified working draft; thresholds remain uncalibrated

## Outcome model

| Level | Question | Core evidence | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach and access | Did the intended people enter and participate equitably? | Funnel, access needs, device/bandwidth, attendance/equivalence and attrition reasons | Reach is not effectiveness |
| Experience and conditions | Was delivery relevant, safe, accessible and supported? | Short pulse feedback, incidents, support response, faculty/coach/host feedback | Satisfaction is not capability |
| Capability | Can participants demonstrate the required judgement and methods? | Moderated assessments, rubric dimensions and artefact quality | Scores alone prove workplace change |
| Transfer | Are capabilities used in the workplace with sponsor support? | Delayed participant/sponsor follow-up, artefact progression and barriers/enablers | Self-report alone proves change |
| Improvement results | Did governed projects show credible signals without harm? | Baseline, process/outcome/balancing measures, PDSA records and run charts | Association proves causation |
| Sustainability and spread | Are changes maintained, adapted or responsibly stopped? | Sustainability/spread records and follow-up at an approved interval | Every project should spread |
| Programme learning | What should the programme keep, change, stop or investigate? | Triangulated findings, equity review, action log and reassessment | One cohort establishes general effect |

## QA system

### Before each month

- content/rubric alignment, source currency and country adaptation reviewed;
- workload prototype and accessibility evidence current;
- faculty, coach, platform and operations readiness passed;
- data collection is necessary, lawful, minimised and assigned;
- known risks and participant adjustments have owners.

### During delivery

- monitor access, support, incidents, participation and artefact flow;
- use short feedback for rapid correction without over-surveying;
- log deviations, changes and consequences;
- protect assessment independence and confidentiality.

### At month close

- reconcile delivery against design;
- review disaggregated patterns only where lawful and sufficiently safe;
- classify actions as correct now, test next cycle, escalate or accept;
- close actions only with evidence.

### At cohort and annual review

- assess all outcome levels with limitations;
- review unintended effects, equity, complaints and data incidents;
- seek an independent challenge on methods and conclusions;
- publish only claims supported by approved, privacy-safe evidence.

## Output acceptance checklist

Every workstream output passes only when:

1. decision and audience are explicit;
2. canon and dependencies are reconciled;
3. live sources and access dates are recorded;
4. alternatives and trade-offs are genuine;
5. assumptions, limitations and missing evidence are visible;
6. safety, privacy, accessibility, equity and operational ownership are addressed;
7. acceptance tests are measurable and recovery exists;
8. no proposed/publication-blocked claim is presented as approved;
9. verification findings are closed or carried as blocking;
10. the repository, Drive review record and control-room status agree.

## Evaluation governance

- Programme Director owns use of findings; W11 owns method integrity and the action log.
- W12 owns the approved data specification and disclosure controls.
- Operational quality improvement, programme evaluation and human-subject research are classified before collection; research follows the required ethics route.
- Negative and null findings are retained and reported with the same provenance as favourable findings.
- Thresholds are proposed only after a baseline/pilot distribution, risk assessment and decision-use statement.

## Required instruments

- baseline/context profile;
- monthly access and delivery pulse;
- faculty/coach/host structured debrief;
- assessment/rubric reliability review;
- delayed transfer follow-up for participant and sponsor;
- project-results extraction aligned to SQUIRE elements;
- complaints/incidents and corrective-action log;
- cohort evaluation report and decision-action register.

All instruments need W12 field definitions, purpose, retention and access approval before use.
