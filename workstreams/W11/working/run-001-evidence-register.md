# W11 run 001 — evidence register

Status: working evidence; accessed 29 July 2026

| Source | Finding used | QA/evaluation implication |
|---|---|---|
| [CDC Program Evaluation Framework 2024](https://www.cdc.gov/evaluation/php/evaluation-framework/index.html) | High-quality evaluation should address relevance/utility, rigor, independence/objectivity, transparency and ethics, through six steps and cross-cutting collaboration, equity and use. | Use a utilisation-oriented cycle with explicit standards and action ownership. |
| [CDC building an evaluation plan](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/evaluate-training-building-an-evaluation-plan.html) | Process and outcome questions require fit-for-purpose methods selected early. | Define questions, users, measures, timing and decisions before delivery. |
| [CDC measuring effectiveness](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/about/evaluate-training-measuring-effectiveness.html) | Satisfaction does not determine effectiveness; assess learning and delayed workplace transfer when possible. | Separate experience from capability, transfer and results. |
| [CDC quality training standards references](https://www.cdc.gov/training-development/php/qts/quality-training-standards-references.html) | Follow-up support, workplace opportunity and supervisor/peer support affect transfer. | Measure context and support, not only participant effort. |
| [SQUIRE 2.0](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/squire/) | Improvement reports should address context, intervention, study, results and ethical considerations. | Align capstone-quality and publishability checks without treating all projects as research. |
| [WHO health-workforce education technology assessment](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240070929) | Digital education performance depends on multiple interacting levels. | Include platform, organisation and access conditions in interpretation. |

## Evidence limits

- ISQua education/accreditation requirements have not been licensed or formally mapped; no accreditation-readiness claim is made.
- Outcome thresholds require baseline and pilot distributions, not arbitrary percentages.
- Independent external evaluation has no named evaluator, terms or budget.
