**Directorate D2 · People**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> Who coaches, how are they trained and supervised, and what happens when a coach stops?

## What it must produce

- Coach competency framework and selection criteria
- Internal coach development curriculum and calibration protocol
- Coaching session standard, agenda and artefact review protocol
- Coach agreement with named alternate and continuity plan
- Coach supervision and quality assurance model

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Improvement coaching models and what distinguishes coaching from teaching
2. Coach-to-participant ratios and the evidence behind them
3. Volunteer versus paid coaching: attrition evidence in professional communities
4. Coach credentialing routes and whether to build or buy

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI Improvement Coach PDP
- Health Foundation GenerationQ executive coaching
- NHS improvement coaching models
