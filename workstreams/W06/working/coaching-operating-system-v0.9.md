# QIPS coaching operating system v0.9

Status: restricted working design; not an appointment, ratio or public promise

Date: 2026-07-29

## Purpose and boundary

Coaching helps a participant think, test and learn through their own improvement work. It is not a
second live faculty session, therapy, consultancy, line management, clinical supervision or an
assessment decision. A coach may clarify the artefact standard and ask evidence-seeking questions,
but may not author the artefact, invent data or privately change an award requirement.

## Competency framework

Every coach must demonstrate all six domains through evidence and observed practice:

1. **Improvement method:** aims, measures, systems, variation, change theory and PDSA.
2. **Coaching craft:** listening, contracting, inquiry, feedback and productive challenge.
3. **Group facilitation:** participation balance, psychological safety, conflict and time control.
4. **Evidence discipline:** distinguish observation from inference; surface limitations and prevent
   fabricated or patient-identifiable material.
5. **Ethics and boundaries:** confidentiality, conflicts, safeguarding, referral and role clarity.
6. **Programme navigation:** monthly artefacts, escalation routes, platform use and accessibility.

A famous faculty profile is not evidence of coaching competence. Teaching, coaching, supervision
and assessment require separate clearance even when one person holds more than one role.

## Selection and readiness gate

A candidate coach supplies a restricted evidence record, conflict declaration, availability and
backup commitment. Readiness requires:

- verified improvement experience and evidence of supporting others;
- completion of the QIPS coach-development sequence;
- a satisfactory observed simulation scored by two calibrated reviewers;
- signed role, confidentiality, safeguarding, data and intellectual-property terms;
- successful platform and low-bandwidth rehearsal; and
- named primary and alternate coverage.

No identity is published before the W05 evidence-and-consent gate. No coach is described as IHI- or
ICF-certified unless that exact credential is independently verified and publication is authorised.

## Internal development sequence

| Phase | Evidence of readiness |
|---|---|
| Orientation | Role boundary, programme architecture and escalation check |
| Improvement-method calibration | Common case analysis and data-interpretation check |
| Coaching practice | Recorded or observed simulation with consent and structured feedback |
| Group practice | Facilitation of a multi-participant scenario, including conflict and silence |
| Ethics and safety | Confidentiality, safeguarding, conflict and referral scenarios passed |
| Supported launch | First two calls observed or co-facilitated; corrective actions closed |
| Continuing calibration | Periodic sample review, reflective supervision and annual re-clearance |

This sequence builds capability internally as required by F009. External resources may support
development but do not replace the programme's own evidence of readiness.

## Monthly coaching-call standard

### Before the call

- Participants submit the monthly artefact four days before the call under F004.
- The coach reviews each artefact using the current protocol and records questions, not grades.
- Access or safety concerns are escalated before the call; patient-identifiable content is not
  copied into coaching notes.
- The alternate coach has the minimum continuity brief and access needed to step in.

### During the call

1. Recontract confidentiality, boundaries and purpose.
2. Check progress, conditions and material risks.
3. Use one participant's evidence at a time; balance airtime.
4. Test assumptions, measures and learning without taking ownership of the work.
5. Agree one next action per participant, with owner and date.
6. Escalate assessment, welfare, safeguarding, clinical or legal matters through the correct route.

### After the call

- Record attendance, the agreed next action and any escalation using minimum necessary data.
- Do not create shadow assessment marks or narrative case files.
- Trigger continuity action when a coach misses a rehearsal, call or review deadline.
- Capture programme-level improvement signals separately from private coaching content.

## Capacity and ratio test

No fixed ratio is proposed without evidence. Before approval, W06 must run a timed sandbox using a
representative monthly artefact set and both primary and alternate coaches. Capacity includes:

- artefact review time;
- call time and preparation;
- documentation and escalation;
- supervision and calibration;
- absence cover; and
- time-zone and accessibility constraints.

The selected ratio must keep all work within the coach's contracted capacity at the 90th-percentile
review time observed in the sandbox, with contingency for one absent coach. If it does not, reduce
the group size or add cleared coaches; do not reduce review depth silently.

## Supervision and quality assurance

- A coaching lead owns calibration, boundaries, coverage and corrective action.
- Coaches attend confidential reflective supervision; only necessary actions and aggregate themes
  leave that space.
- A risk-based sample of calls or records is reviewed with participant consent and controlled
  access.
- Signals include timeliness, continuity, equitable participation, escalation quality, participant
  usefulness and downstream artefact improvement—not satisfaction alone.
- Repeated boundary, confidentiality or evidence failures suspend coaching authority pending review.

## Continuity and exit

Every coach agreement names an alternate and requires structured handover. The continuity record
contains programme status, agreed actions and risks, not personal speculation. On withdrawal,
access is removed, restricted records are returned or disposed of under the approved schedule, and
participants receive one accountable contact. The programme does not promise continuity until the
full monthly rehearsal proves it.

## Approval gates

This design cannot become an operating promise until:

1. W02's monthly artefact specifications and workload are approved.
2. W05's appointment and publication system is approved and actual coverage is verified.
3. W17 approves agreements, safeguarding, confidentiality and data handling.
4. W09 proves platform roles and low-bandwidth continuity.
5. A full-month capacity sandbox demonstrates a sustainable ratio and alternate coverage.
