# DRAFT DOSSIER W06-01 — Coaching Faculty and Coach Development

Status: blocked working draft; not on the CCC agenda

## 1. The decision requested

When upstream conditions are met, approve the accountable internal coaching system: observed
readiness, standard monthly practice, named alternates, reflective supervision and a capacity-tested
ratio.

## 2. Why it matters now

F005 promises monthly small-group coaching and F009 requires internal capability. Without a
controlled operating model, the promise can become informal volunteer labour with inconsistent
boundaries and no continuity.

## 3. Canon constraints

F003, F004, F005, F009, F019 and F021 apply. W02 and W05 remain upstream dependencies.

## 5. Options

- **Distributed volunteer network:** fast and relational, but fragile and opaque on capacity.
- **Accountable internal system:** common standard, observed readiness, supervision and alternates;
  recommended despite its real staffing cost.
- **External professional service:** stronger contractual continuity but conflicts with F009 unless
  used only for bounded development support.

## 6. Council verdict

All advisors rejected an invented ratio and status-based appointment. The chairman adopted the
accountable internal system without overruling the majority.

## 7. Recommendation

Prepare the accountable internal system and select a ratio only after the timed monthly sandbox.

## 8. Conditions

Approved W02 and W05 decisions; W17 legal and safety clearance; representative capacity test;
verified primary/alternate coach coverage; platform rehearsal.

## 9. Proposed canon if later approved

1. “QIPS coaching authority requires observed readiness, signed role terms and continuing
   supervision; teaching or reputation alone does not confer it.”
2. “Every coaching group has cleared primary and alternate coverage.”
3. “The coaching ratio is set from a documented capacity test and may not be protected by silently
   reducing artefact review or supervision.”

## 10. What it would unblock

Operational staffing, coach recruitment, the W10 monthly runbook and the W16 true-cost model.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

Use aggregate capacity telemetry to adjust group allocation without exposing private coaching
content; build a supervised future coach pathway from graduates only after cohort-one evidence.

## 12. Not established

Actual coaches, sustainable ratio, compensation, availability, final artefacts and approved terms.

## 13. Verification

PASS WITH BLOCKING FINDINGS. The design is internally coherent, but unresolved dependencies and
missing field evidence prevent submission.
