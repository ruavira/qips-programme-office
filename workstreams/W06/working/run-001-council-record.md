# W06 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

What coaching model should QIPS prepare while its upstream programme and faculty decisions remain
unapproved?

## Advisor positions

- **Contrarian:** do not create a pseudo-credential or publish a ratio before timed operational
  evidence exists.
- **First Principles:** separate coaching from teaching, assessment, therapy and clinical advice;
  manage competence by observed behaviour.
- **Executor:** standardise the monthly call, artefact review, alternate coverage and escalation now.
- **Outsider:** protect participants from status hierarchies by balancing airtime and offering a
  confidential route around the assigned coach.
- **Expansionist:** create a supervised internal coach pipeline that may later draw from alumni.

## Synthesis

Advance an **accountable internal coaching system** with observed readiness, a named alternate,
reflective supervision and a capacity-tested ratio. Do not assign a number until a representative
monthly sandbox proves it. The chairman adopted the convergence and did not overrule the majority.

## Conditions

W06 remains a working design until W02 and W05 are approved, W17 clears the terms and safety route,
and a full-month rehearsal proves workload and continuity.
