# W09 — Technology and Learning Platform

**Directorate D3 · Platform and Operations**
Maps to the owner's original list: *(h) technologies including LMS, website, electronic tools, virtual sessions*
Proposed cohort-1 position: **NOW**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What must the technology do, what do we already have, and what is the smallest stack that delivers the monthly cycle reliably in three countries?

## What it must produce

- Platform requirements specification tied to the monthly cycle
- LMS decision and configuration standard
- Website and enrolment stack decision
- Virtual session standard: recording, captions, bandwidth floor, attendance
- Data architecture, integrations and reporting
- Low-bandwidth and mobile-first standard

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. LMS selection for cohort-based professional programmes
2. Low-bandwidth and mobile delivery evidence in African and South Asian settings
3. Virtual session tooling: recording, captioning, breakout and attendance at scale
4. Build versus buy for enrolment, payments and CRM

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI platform
- Coursera and edX delivery standards
- Moodle in LMIC health education
