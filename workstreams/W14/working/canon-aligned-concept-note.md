## The need

Healthcare professionals are often asked to improve safety, reliability and patient experience
while working inside real operational constraints. This programme is being designed to help
working professionals use practical improvement methods, measurement and structured reflection on
problems in their own settings. It does not claim that a national standard, new profession or
professional certification has already been established.

## The programme

The programme runs for 12 months. Each month includes one eLearning release, one live faculty
session, one small-group coaching call on Zoom and one artefact. Artefacts are submitted four days
before the coaching call so coaches can read the work and use the call for judgement and
unblocking. The published commitment is about four hours a week in an ordinary week and about 228
hours in total.

Participants also complete a 40-hour observership from month 3 to week 2 of month 4 at a qualified
host site in their own country. Travel and accommodation are met by the participant or sponsoring
organisation. Host sites are assessed against a programme standard. `[TO CONFIRM: qualified sites,
agreements, supervisors, insurance and placement calendar]`
