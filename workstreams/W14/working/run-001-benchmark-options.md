## Options

### Option A — brochure-first lead capture

A prominent form unlocks the brochure and routes leads to follow-up. It can grow a list quickly but
collects personal data before the programme's core commitments are settled. Technically feasible;
not recommended before Q009 and the public claims gate.

### Option B — public proof page plus staged interest and application

Publish approved facts first. Invite minimal, consented interest; branch individual and
institutional routes; open the full application only when name, award, price, policies and privacy
controls pass. Slower list growth, better trust and cleaner data. Recommended.

### Option C — institution-only launch

Sell first to facilities and sponsors through named-account outreach. Stronger ability to pay and
protected time, but it excludes credible self-pay demand and relies on a still-unproven B2B motion.
Use as a parallel route, not the entire funnel.

## Recommendation

Approve Option B with a parallel institutional branch. Build every page from the claims register;
do not allow page-builder copy to become an independent factual source.
