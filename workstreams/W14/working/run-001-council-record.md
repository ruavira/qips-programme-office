## Framed question

What demand system can begin now without publishing unapproved name, award, faculty, capacity or
price claims?

## Advisor positions and synthesis

The Contrarian rejected a paid campaign before a denominator exists. First Principles separated
interest, application, offer and payment. The Executor prioritised SQHN owned channels and direct
institutional conversations. The Outsider proposed publishing the working rhythm and proof rather
than prestige. The Expansionist favoured partner amplification only after permissions.

The council converged on a **two-track proof-led funnel**: restricted institutional discovery and
an approved-claims interest journey. Paid acquisition and public application remain gated.
