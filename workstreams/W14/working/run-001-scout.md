## Inherited material

The brochure content master is detailed and substantially aligned to the 12-month architecture,
but it contains many placeholders and several lines that must remain withheld. The two concept
notes are historical: they describe 12–18 months, Nigerian validation, professional certification
and operating systems that are not approved. They cannot remain competing masters.

## Run boundary

This run will build a claims register, a two-track demand funnel, the Q006 data request, a
canon-aligned concept-note source and a publication gate. It will not launch a campaign, solicit
personal data, publish a price, identify faculty or overwrite the historical source files.
