**Directorate D4 · Business and Governance**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> How does a qualified applicant find this, believe it, and complete an application?

## What it must produce

- Positioning and messaging platform
- Channel plan with cost per enrolment assumptions
- Content and campaign calendar tied to the application window
- Landing page, brochure and collateral system
- Institutional and named-account sales motion
- Referral and ambassador programme

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Channel evidence for professional education in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan
2. Institutional selling into hospitals where the trigger is regulatory
3. Trust signals that convert for an unproven credential
4. Cost per enrolment benchmarks in professional education

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- MIT xPRO and Emeritus B2C funnel
- IHI marketing
- Local Nigerian professional education marketing
