# W05 faculty appointment and consent pack

Status: working operating pack; no individual appointment or public claim is approved

Date: 2026-07-28

## Publication gate

A person's name, photograph, biography, title, institution, credential or affiliation may enter public QIPS material only when the claim has been verified, the programme role and topic are accepted, and the individual has given written publication consent. Institutional affiliation must not be converted into institutional endorsement.

## Twelve-slot coverage roster

The roster is a coverage instrument, not twelve invented names. One person may cover more than one slot only after workload and continuity are tested; a guest speaker does not automatically satisfy a core slot.

1. Healthcare quality and patient-safety foundations.
2. Safety science, human factors and just culture.
3. Quality governance and measurement systems.
4. Communication, teamwork and structured handoffs.
5. PDSA, change management and stakeholder engagement.
6. Process mapping, root-cause analysis and improvement tools.
7. Risk management, medication safety and infection prevention.
8. Data collection, run charts, SPC and visual display.
9. Safety culture, patient partnership and leadership.
10. Accreditation, standards and regulatory readiness.
11. Capstone planning, implementation and sustainability.
12. Capstone analysis, presentation and professional development.

## Minimum appointment evidence

For every core faculty candidate collect:

- legal and preferred public name;
- current role, employer/institution and country;
- CV and relevant qualifications/licences;
- evidence of recent subject practice or scholarship;
- evidence of teaching/facilitation experience, or agreement to complete development;
- proposed topic, learning outcomes and delivery dates;
- two referees or equivalent organisational confirmation for high-responsibility roles;
- financial, organisational and intellectual conflicts;
- availability, connectivity and backup plan;
- fee/honorarium, expenses and tax/payment details in the restricted system;
- signed appointment, confidentiality/IP terms and publication choices.

## Selection standard

Score candidates against four separate domains rather than reputation alone:

| Domain | Evidence sought | Minimum control |
|---|---|---|
| Subject credibility | Current practice, qualification, scholarship or portfolio relevant to the assigned outcomes | Verified source and reviewer sign-off |
| Teaching capability | Adult-learning practice, facilitation, assessment and feedback evidence | Rehearsal or observed sample unless formally exempted with reasons |
| Context competence | Experience applicable to Nigeria, Ghana, Pakistan or comparable constrained settings | Candidate demonstrates adaptation, not imported assumptions |
| Reliability and integrity | Availability, preparation, disclosure, respectful conduct and policy acceptance | Complete appointment record and no unmanaged conflict |

## Conflict and independence control

Before content development, each planner and faculty member declares relevant financial and organisational relationships. The programme records the decision: no conflict, disclosed and managed, reassigned, content independently reviewed, or excluded. Learners receive the approved disclosure before the relevant session.

No sponsor, employer or partner may select content solely to market a product or service. Clinical and operational recommendations must be evidence-based, balanced and named by generic method where possible.

## Appointment and rights checklist

- role, topic, deliverable and deadline;
- faculty status versus guest-speaker status;
- preparation, rehearsal and attendance expectations;
- honorarium/expenses and cancellation terms;
- ownership and licence for slides, recordings, examples and tools;
- permission to record, caption, edit and replay;
- confidentiality, patient/data protection and case de-identification;
- conflict disclosure and content-independence duties;
- consent choices for name, biography, photograph, organisation and social promotion;
- complaints, safeguarding, conduct and removal route;
- term, substitution and withdrawal of publication consent.

## Faculty development pathway

1. **Orientation:** programme architecture, participants, monthly cycle, scope, safeguarding, data boundary and claims rules.
2. **Teaching design:** outcomes, active learning, low-bandwidth inclusion, accessible materials and assessment alignment.
3. **Rehearsal:** timed delivery with a moderator, technical check and structured feedback.
4. **Calibration:** shared review of sample participant work and feedback language where assessment is involved.
5. **Observed delivery:** programme lead or peer uses the session-quality standard.
6. **Improvement:** learner evidence, peer notes and faculty reflection produce a documented action.

Satisfaction alone is not the quality measure. The programme should also examine outcome coverage, engagement, accessibility, feedback quality, timing, incident handling and evidence of changed teaching practice.

## Session acceptance standard

A session is ready only when:

- approved outcomes and agenda are present;
- examples are de-identified and locally intelligible;
- content has a source trail and no unsupported partner/credential claim;
- interaction works for mobile and low-bandwidth participants;
- slides and activities pass accessibility checks;
- disclosure and biography wording are approved;
- moderator, backup faculty and technical fallback are assigned;
- recording/captioning decision and participant notice are settled;
- follow-up artefact and feedback route are linked to the monthly cycle.

## Guest-speaker boundary

Guest speakers provide bounded experience or case perspective. They do not set curriculum, assess participants, represent an organisation without authority, or fill an unstaffed core-faculty slot by default. They complete proportionate disclosure, consent and confidentiality controls.

## Immediate committee work

- Nominate candidates against the twelve coverage slots, not as a publicity list.
- Identify which candidates have already agreed and which are only prospects.
- Approve the appointment/consent owner and secure storage route.
- Set the honorarium policy before offers are made.
- Reconcile topic assignments with the final W02 calendar.

## Exit criteria

Q003 can be recommended for closure only when twelve approved coverage slots have named primary and backup faculty, verified role/credential evidence, accepted dates, signed appointment and conflict records, and explicit publication consent.
