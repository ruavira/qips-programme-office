# W05 run 001 — scout record

Date started: 2026-07-28

Stage: SCOUTING

Purpose: create the evidence, appointment and consent system for a twelve-person teaching faculty while keeping every public name, title, photograph and affiliation unpublished until individually verified and approved.

## Canonical question in scope

- Q003 — who are the twelve faculty, and have they consented in writing to be named? This blocks W14 and is due 2026-08-31.

## Outputs this run will assemble

1. Role-based faculty specification and minimum evidence standard.
2. Twelve-slot coverage roster mapped to the programme topics.
3. Nomination, due-diligence and conflict-disclosure form.
4. Appointment, intellectual-property and publication-consent requirements.
5. Faculty orientation, rehearsal, peer-observation and session-quality standard.
6. Guest-speaker policy separated from core-faculty accountability.

## Evidence plan

- Review international professional-programme faculty models and current continuing-education integrity standards.
- Separate subject expertise, teaching capability, local-context competence and availability.
- Test every proposed biography and affiliation against a primary source and individual consent.
- Use the final W02 curriculum map to prove complete topic coverage before roster approval.

## Human inputs that cannot be invented

- Candidate names and contact details.
- Credentials, employment/affiliation confirmation and permitted wording.
- Availability and accepted topic assignment.
- Honorarium or voluntary-service decision.
- Signed appointment, conflict declaration and publication consent.

## Exit criteria for scouting

- The twelve slots have defined roles and evidence requirements.
- A complete nomination-to-publication workflow exists.
- Missing or unconsented faculty cannot appear in public material.
- The committee can distinguish core faculty, guest speakers, coaches and host-site supervisors.
