## Options

### Option A — distributed partner and volunteer faculty

- **What it is:** topics are allocated across available partner nominees and invited volunteers.
- **Case for:** broad network, lower fixed commitment and potentially strong country representation.
- **Case against:** coverage, preparation, availability, IP, disclosure and replacement remain fragile; institutional affiliation can be mistaken for endorsement.
- **Cost:** coordination and repeated onboarding; no honorarium assumption is made.
- **Timing:** possible only as a supplementary model, not the cohort-one core.

### Option B — accountable core faculty with bounded guests

- **What it is:** each of twelve coverage slots has a verified primary and backup; appointments carry preparation, development, disclosure, rights and consent controls; guests add bounded perspectives.
- **Case for:** best balance of accountability, contextual diversity and continuity.
- **Case against:** requires earlier commitments, a real honorarium policy and disciplined publication gating.
- **Cost:** appointments, preparation, rehearsal, moderation, observation and backup coverage; amounts require an approved budget and candidate evidence.
- **Timing:** recommended for cohort one.

### Option C — permanent academic unit

- **What it is:** a salaried programme faculty team owns curriculum, teaching, assessment and scholarship, supplemented by invited experts.
- **Case for:** strongest continuity, institutional memory and quality-improvement loop.
- **Case against:** disproportionate before demand, revenue and future cohorts are established.
- **Cost:** recurring employment/contract obligations and management capacity.
- **Timing:** later-stage institutional model.

## Working recommendation

Advance Option B to council testing. The publication gate, four-domain selection standard,
faculty-development pathway, session acceptance test and guest boundary in the appointment pack
become the minimum operating controls. Names remain outside GitHub until the committee has a
restricted candidate register and the individual has consented to publication.

## Unresolved benchmark questions

- Current honorarium norms for the actual candidate pool are not established; collect candidate expectations and comparable institutional rates before setting policy.
- The twelve topics must be reconciled to the final W02 calendar before appointments are binding.
- No partner organisation's right to appoint or approve faculty has been established beyond canon's general partner roles.
- Faculty who assess participant work require a W03 calibration and conflict route in addition to teaching appointment.
