# W05 faculty nomination, review and consent prototype

Status: operating prototype; do not enter candidate personal data in GitHub

Date: 2026-07-28

## Storage boundary

GitHub stores this blank template, slot status and non-personal aggregate counts. Candidate names,
contact details, CVs, referee information, identity/licence evidence, payment/tax data, signatures
and private conflicts belong in a restricted Drive or successor records system approved under W17.

## Part B — independent review

Score each domain from 0 to 4 with a written reason. Reputation, personal familiarity and an
institution's prestige are not substitutes for evidence.

| Domain | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Subject credibility | No relevant evidence | Adjacent knowledge only | Adequate evidence for part of the outcomes | Strong current evidence for the assigned outcomes | Sustained expert practice plus evidence of updating the field |
| Teaching capability | No evidence or unwilling to develop | Presentation experience only | Plausible facilitation with development needed | Strong adult-learning, feedback and assessment evidence | Sustained excellent practice plus ability to develop/calibrate others |
| Context competence | No relevant context and no adaptation plan | Generic international claims | Some comparable-setting evidence | Strong relevant-country/comparable-setting adaptation | Sustained multi-setting practice with explicit learning from local partners |
| Reliability and integrity | Required records refused or material unmanaged conflict | Major gaps in availability/preparation | Adequate records with manageable gaps | Complete, timely and low-risk record | Complete record plus proven backup, collaboration and improvement behaviour |

Decision bands are not approved. Reviewers record `appoint`, `appoint subject to development`,
`guest only`, `hold for evidence` or `do not appoint`, with reasons and the specific slot impact.

## Part C — conflict and independence record

- Financial relationships relevant to the topic.
- Employment, consultancy, ownership or board relationships.
- Intellectual or professional positions that may bias content.
- Relationship to sponsors, partners, products or services mentioned.
- Mitigation: disclose / independent review / topic restriction / reassignment / exclude.
- Reviewer and approval date.

The programme gives participants the approved disclosure before the relevant live session. A
disclosure does not cure a conflict that cannot be managed.

## Part D — appointment and rights checklist

- exact role, outcomes, deliverables, dates and preparation deadlines;
- primary, backup or guest status;
- honorarium, expenses, payment conditions and cancellation terms;
- ownership/licence for slides, tools, examples, recordings and translations;
- permission to record, caption, edit, replay and archive;
- confidentiality, case de-identification and data-protection duties;
- safeguarding, conduct, complaints and removal route;
- rehearsal, accessibility, moderation and technical fallback;
- assessment/calibration duties where applicable;
- term, substitution and withdrawal arrangements.

## Part E — granular publication consent

The candidate selects each item independently:

- public name: yes / no;
- professional title: yes / no / approved wording attached;
- organisation: yes / no / approved wording attached;
- biography: yes / no / approved text attached;
- photograph: yes / no / approved image attached;
- programme topic and role: yes / no / approved wording attached;
- social promotion: yes / no / platform limits attached;
- recording availability: live only / cohort replay / time-limited public / no recording;
- effective date, expiry/review date and withdrawal contact.

Consent to teach is not consent to publicity. An individual's organisation is never described as
endorsing QIPS unless W15 holds separate written authority from that organisation.

## Part F — coverage dashboard fields

For each of the twelve slots, the programme office may expose only: vacant / prospects identified /
evidence review / terms issued / primary appointed / backup appointed / publication cleared. No name
enters the committee-facing control room before publication clearance.
