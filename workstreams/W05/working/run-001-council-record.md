# W05 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-28

## Framed question

Which faculty operating model gives cohort one accountable coverage without inventing appointments,
endorsements or a permanent academic structure before demand is established?

## Advisor positions

### Contrarian

Use a distributed partner and volunteer network. A first cohort should avoid building a core
faculty structure before the final calendar and candidate pool exist.

**Strongest objection to this position:** goodwill does not create coverage, backup, teaching
readiness, conflict control or enforceable delivery rights.

### First Principles

Assign one accountable primary and one backup to every coverage slot. Guests may add perspective,
but the programme promise must be owned by a bounded core.

**Strongest objection to this position:** twelve paired slots may imply more people and cost than
the final W02 calendar requires.

### Executor

Adopt the accountable-core model but appoint against outcomes and dates only after W02 reconciliation.
Use a restricted candidate record, a four-domain evidence review, one appointment instrument and
granular publication consent.

**Strongest objection to this position:** the full control sequence may slow recruitment of scarce
experts unless responsibilities and turnaround times are explicit.

### Outsider

Separate teaching capability from reputation. Test every candidate through a bounded teaching
sample, feedback and rehearsal; do not treat affiliation or seniority as evidence of teaching
readiness.

**Strongest objection to this position:** mandatory rehearsal can deter experienced contributors
if applied without proportion.

### Expansionist

Build the core as the seed of a later academic unit with teaching scholarship and a faculty
development pathway. Guests should enter through the same conflict and consent controls.

**Strongest objection to this position:** a future academic unit is not justified by one cohort
and could blur the immediate delivery problem.

## Convergence and clash

Four advisors favoured an accountable core plus bounded guests. The Contrarian preferred a
distributed model on cost and speed. All agreed that no individual should be publicised before
evidence review, appointment and consent, and that affiliation is not institutional endorsement.

## Chairman's synthesis

Advance **Option B: accountable core faculty plus bounded guests**. The operating unit is a
coverage slot, not a publicity list. Each required slot needs a primary, a backup or a recorded
risk treatment, and one accountable programme owner.

The chairman did not overrule the majority.

## Conditions

1. The twelve-slot map is provisional until reconciled with W02.
2. No name enters public material before evidence, conflict, appointment and consent controls pass.
3. Subject credibility, teaching capability, context competence and reliability/integrity remain
   separate evidence domains.
4. Faculty who assess participant work require W03 calibration and conflict controls.
5. Restricted personal records remain outside GitHub and the committee-facing control room.
6. Appointment terms cover dates, outcomes, honorarium, cancellation, intellectual property,
   recording, confidentiality, accessibility and backup.
