# DOSSIER W05-01 — Teaching Faculty and Faculty Development
**For decision at the next CCC sitting · Prepared by workstream W05 · Run 1 · Verification: PASS WITH FINDINGS**

## 1. The decision requested

Approve accountable core faculty plus bounded guests, organised by provisional coverage slots and
controlled by evidence, conflict, appointment, preparation and consent gates.

## 2. Why it is on the agenda now

Q003 blocks credible public faculty claims and W14 collateral. W02 cannot finalise delivery coverage
without an operating model, while candidate recruitment without one becomes an unauditable list of
names. This decision approves the system, not any person.

## 5. The options

### Option A — distributed partners and volunteers

Broad, flexible and initially less expensive, but fragile on continuity, rights, conflict control
and backup. Defensible only as a supplementary model.

### Option B — accountable core plus bounded guests

Each required slot has verified primary and backup responsibility, appointment, development and
consent. It creates more administration and possible honorarium cost, but is recommended for
cohort one.

### Option C — permanent academic unit

Strongest continuity and scholarship, but a fixed structure is disproportionate before recurring
demand and revenue are established. Later option.

## 6. Council verdict

Four advisors favoured Option B; the Contrarian favoured a distributed network on cost and speed.
All agreed that reputation does not prove teaching readiness and that no name may be public before
appointment and consent. The chairman adopted Option B and did not overrule the majority.

## 7. Recommendation

Approve Option B. The unit of management is the coverage slot, not a publicity list. Core faculty
own required outcomes; guests are bounded additions under the same conflict and consent controls.

## 8. Conditions

1. By 31 August 2026, W02 and W05 reconcile the provisional twelve-slot map to the approved monthly
   calendar.
2. Before public release, every named person has verified evidence, a resolved conflict record,
   executed appointment and granular publication consent.
3. Before enrolment commitments, every required slot has a primary and backup or an approved risk
   treatment.
4. Before a faculty member assesses participant work, W03 records assessor calibration and conflict
   clearance.
5. Restricted candidate and appointment records remain outside GitHub and committee-facing Base44
   views.

## 9. What becomes canon if you approve

1. “Cohort one uses accountable core faculty for required coverage, supplemented by bounded guests.”
2. “No faculty identity or affiliation is published before evidence review, appointment, conflict
   clearance and explicit publication consent.”
3. “Teaching appointment does not by itself authorise assessment; assessors require separate
   calibration and conflict clearance.”

No existing fact is superseded.

## 10. What this unblocks

The decision gives W02, W06 and W14 an operating system to design against. W05 remains incomplete
until actual candidates and consent exist, so Q003 remains open and dependent workstreams do not
formally wake.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

- Publish aggregate coverage readiness without exposing candidate identities.
- Treat backup ownership as part of the programme promise, not emergency administration.
- Use one evidence-and-development record that follows a faculty member across teaching, coaching
  and assessment roles while preserving role-specific clearance.

## 12. What we could not establish

- Any actual candidate's identity, evidence, availability, terms or consent.
- Final W02 outcomes, dates and resulting slot count.
- Honorarium norms or total faculty cost.
- Any partner organisation's appointment right.
- Whether proposed candidates can serve as assessors.
