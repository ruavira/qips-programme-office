## The question this workstream exists to answer

> Who teaches, on what basis are they qualified to, and how are they developed and quality-assured?

## What it must produce

- Faculty specification and appointment criteria
- Faculty contract, consent and IP terms
- Faculty development and calibration programme
- Session quality standard and peer observation protocol
- Guest speaker policy

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Faculty models in international professional programmes
2. Honoraria and engagement norms in LMIC health education
3. Faculty development evidence — what actually improves teaching quality
4. Conflict of interest and commercial influence policy

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI faculty model
- ISQua expert faculty
- Ghana and Nigeria postgraduate college faculty structures
