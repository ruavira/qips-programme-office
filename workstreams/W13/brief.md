## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What is this called, what does it look and sound like, and whose name is on it?

## What it must produce

- Programme name and naming rationale
- Brand architecture: SQHN, partners, programme, credential
- Visual identity system and asset library
- Voice and messaging guide
- Naming and trademark clearance

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Naming conventions in professional credentials and fellowships
2. Brand architecture for consortium and partner-delivered programmes
3. Name clearance, trademark and domain availability
4. How names signal level, commitment and geography to an LMIC professional audience
