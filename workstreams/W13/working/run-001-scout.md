# W13 run 001 — scout

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

What identity architecture and naming route can be approved now without inventing the exact
credential, partner rights or programme name that remain under CCC control?

- F001 and F002 establish a 12-month professional programme and its controlled vocabulary.
- F014 and F015 place SQHN as lead sponsor and every other organisation in the partner layer.
- F017 requires a temporary mix of RCI and SQHN identity assets.
- F020 establishes Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan for cohort one.
- F021 prohibits publication of unconfirmed facts.
- F022 restricts ISQua language to individual-faculty affiliation.

## Open questions and dependencies

- Q002 blocks a final credential lock-up and any recognition signal.
- Q005 blocks a complete partner line because QAI's registered identity and role are unverified.
- Q010 asks the CCC to approve the programme name.
- W15 still owns partner descriptions, affiliation wording and logo permission.

## Inherited material

The historical brochure uses **Foundation Professional Program in Healthcare Quality and Patient
Safety** and a dark teal/gold visual system. That title is not an approved fact. The brochure also
uses affiliation and award wording that remains gated. The user has asked for a lighter, brighter
public expression and for the identity to remain portable beyond Base44.

## Run boundary

This run may propose:

- a sponsor, programme, partner and credential hierarchy;
- three naming territories and a preferred working candidate;
- a lighter, brighter accessible visual direction;
- a trademark, domain and partner-permission clearance sequence; and
- a voice and claims boundary.

It may not approve or publish a programme name, credential title, partner logo, affiliation,
faculty identity, price or recognition claim.

## Working hypothesis

Use a sponsor-led endorsed architecture: SQHN is prominent but not fused into every future asset;
the programme has a short working name plus a descriptive line; partners sit in a controlled
endorsement layer; the credential remains a separate placeholder until W03 is decided. Advance
one preferred working name to clearance, not to publication.
