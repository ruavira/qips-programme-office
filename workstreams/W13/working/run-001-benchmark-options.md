## Three naming territories

### Territory A — sponsor-led descriptive

**Working candidate:** SQHN Professional Programme in Healthcare Quality and Patient Safety.

Strongest trust transfer and clearest category. It is long, difficult to use as a product family,
and may make later multi-country stewardship feel Nigeria-only. Defensible for cohort one.

### Territory B — short programme family plus descriptor

**Working candidate:** QIPS Professional Programme
**Descriptor:** Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety.

Strongest balance of recall, continuity with the internal QIPS identity and descriptive clarity.
The initials are descriptive and may be legally weak, so clearance and a consistent descriptor are
essential. Defensible for cohort one and recommended for clearance.

### Territory C — outcome-led ownable brand

**Working candidate:** Quality in Practice
**Descriptor:** A professional programme in healthcare quality and patient safety.

More human and ownable, but it discards accumulated QIPS recognition and could sound like a
consultancy or publication. Defensible as a reserve candidate, not the first route.

## Recommendation

Advance Territory B as the preferred working route and Territory A as the fallback. Approval at
this stage means permission to clear and prototype them, not permission to publish either name.

## Clearance ladder

1. Confirm the legal owner of the mark and the entity authorised to file.
2. Search exact, phonetic and conceptually similar marks through WIPO and each target national
   register in the relevant service classes.
3. Search legal names, professional programmes, web results, social handles and relevant domains.
4. Obtain counsel's written risk opinion for Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.
5. Secure the chosen domain and priority handles without launching them.
6. Obtain SQHN approval and every partner attribution and logo permission in writing.
7. Record the CCC verdict that closes Q010; only then release public assets.

## Proposed light visual direction

This is a prototype palette, not approved brand canon.

| Role | Token | Proposed value | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canvas | warm ivory | `#FFF9F1` | Main page background |
| Surface | white | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and forms |
| Primary light | mist aqua | `#DFF3F5` | Section fields and gentle bands |
| Secondary light | clear sky | `#DCEEFF` | Programme journey and information panels |
| Accent | warm coral | `#F28C68` | Limited calls to action and highlights |
| Brand ink | deep teal | `#0F5257` | Headings and key navigation |
| Text ink | midnight | `#16324F` | Body text on light surfaces |

Dark teal or midnight text must be used on the light fields; coral is never body text. Existing
SQHN and RCI assets are source material, not permission to alter or combine their marks.
