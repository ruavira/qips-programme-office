| E-W13-001 | https://isqua.org/fellowship/ | ISQua separates the Fellowship name, the FISQua credential, focused certificates, maintenance and country-band pricing. | Supports separating programme name, award and maintained credential. It grants QIPS no naming or credential right. |
| E-W13-002 | https://www.ihi.org/learn/courses/improvement-advisor-professional-development-program-united-states | IHI uses a role-led descriptive name, then states format, duration, fee, audience, outcomes and continuing-education status. | Supports a descriptive name and proof hierarchy. | E-W13-003 | https://www.achs.org.au/qil | ACHS uses a short designation, Quality Improvement Lead, over a nine-month applied programme. | Supports a capability-led territory. QIL and any associated designation remain ACHS properties. |
| E-W13-004 | https://nahq.org/credentials/cphq-certified-professional-in-healthcare-quality/ | NAHQ leads with a governed credential and explains its professional scope. | Shows why QIPS must not let a programme name imply professional certification. |
| E-W13-005 | https://reader.health.org.uk/short-history/introduction | The Health Foundation records GenerationQ as one of its leadership programmes and distinguishes it from its Quality Improvement Fellowship. | Supports a more ownable programme masterbrand, while showing that a coined name needs institutional meaning and long-term investment. |
| E-W13-006 | https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database | WIPO provides a multi-register search and advises additional national or regional searches and professional advice. | Establishes that a web search is not clearance. National registers and legal review remain required. |
