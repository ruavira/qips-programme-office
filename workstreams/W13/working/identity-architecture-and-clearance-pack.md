# W13 identity architecture and clearance pack

Status: working instrument; not approved for publication

## Identity hierarchy

1. **Lead sponsor:** SQHN, using its exact approved registered name and mark.
2. **Programme:** preferred working route “QIPS Professional Programme,” subject to clearance and
   CCC decision.
3. **Descriptor:** “Healthcare Quality & Patient Safety,” always present until the short name has
   independent recognition.
4. **Partner line:** “with Partners,” followed only by approved names and permitted marks.
5. **Credential line:** `[TO CONFIRM: exact completion-award title and issuer statement]`.
6. **Recognition line:** absent unless a named body has approved exact wording in writing.

## Lock-up rules

- SQHN leads; partners are not shown as a consortium or as co-accreditors.
- The programme mark never incorporates a partner logo into one inseparable graphic.
- The programme name and the completion award are not interchangeable.
- No logo is used merely because a person from that organisation serves as faculty.
- Country names describe recruitment scope, not validation, accreditation or endorsement.
- Base44, a website builder or a social profile never becomes the master asset store.

## Voice

**Calm, practical and exact.** Lead with the work participants will do, the monthly rhythm and the
evidence they will produce. Prefer concrete verbs: diagnose, map, test, measure, coach, observe,
present. Avoid prestige inflation, transformation guarantees and artificial urgency.

## Message ladder

### Approved foundation

- A 12-month hybrid professional programme.
- One eLearning release, one live faculty session, one small-group coaching call and one artefact
  each month.
- About four hours a week in an ordinary week and about 228 hours in total.
- A 40-hour observership at a qualified host site in the participant's country, with participant or
  sponsor travel and accommodation.
- Cohort one recruits in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan and runs January to December 2027.

### Held from publication

- exact programme and completion-award names;
- price, deposit, discounts or cohort capacity;
- faculty identities and affiliations;
- partner and affiliation wording or marks;
- CPD, accreditation, endorsement or recognition;
- named host sites and outcome statistics about QIPS itself.

## Asset portability

The approved kit will store source SVG/PNG marks, colour and type tokens, templates, licensing
records and export rules in a vendor-neutral Drive folder with checksums and a repository manifest.
Base44 and the public site consume exports. They do not own the identity source.

## Approval record required

For each released asset record: asset id, version, owner, source file, checksum, permission scope,
territory, expiry if any, approved wording, CCC decision reference and public destinations.
