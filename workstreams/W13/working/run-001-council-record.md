# W13 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

Which identity territory should proceed to legal and linguistic clearance without prematurely
settling Q010 or implying an unapproved credential?

## Advisor positions

- **Contrarian:** lead with SQHN and a plain descriptive title; a new acronym adds clearance and
  comprehension risk.
- **First Principles:** separate sponsor, programme, descriptor, partner and credential layers so
  one decision cannot imply another.
- **Executor:** clear “QIPS Professional Programme” first, retain a descriptive fallback and build
  only portable source assets.
- **Outsider:** test “Quality in Practice” because it expresses applied work, but do not confuse a
  creative route with clearance.
- **Expansionist:** design an identity system that can support future pathways without promising
  them now.

## Synthesis

Advance **QIPS Professional Programme** with the mandatory descriptor **Healthcare Quality &
Patient Safety** as the primary clearance candidate. Retain an SQHN-led descriptive name as the
fallback. This is a clearance instruction, not a naming approval. No advisor proposed releasing a
name before WIPO, relevant national-register and counsel checks.

## Conditions

1. Q010 remains open until the CCC approves an exact name and descriptor.
2. Search results, classes, territories, dates and legal advice must be retained.
3. Credential, recognition and partner wording remain separate decisions.
4. Prototype use is restricted and clearly watermarked.
5. Source assets remain vendor-neutral and checksum-registered.
