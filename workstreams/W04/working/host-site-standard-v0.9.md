# QIPS Host Site Standard v0.9

Status: reconstructed qualification instrument; no site is qualified by this document

## Non-negotiable gates

A site fails qualification if any of these is absent:

1. verified legal identity, operating authority and authorised agreement signatory;
2. written country-specific legal and insurance confirmation matching the proposed activities;
3. named accountable site lead, primary preceptor and alternate with protected capacity;
4. an expressly observational scope: no diagnosis, advice, treatment, procedure, prescribing,
   clinical decision, record entry, record copy, system credential or unsupervised access;
5. induction, identification, health/safety, confidentiality, conduct and incident procedures;
6. a patient-consent route where observation could occur in a patient context, including the
   patient's unrestricted right to decline without consequence;
7. a schedule showing 40 observable hours inside the approved month 3–week 2 month 4 window;
8. evidence that the quality systems promised can actually be observed without disclosing
   identifiable or commercially restricted information;
9. safeguarding, accessibility, cultural-safety and anti-harassment controls;
10. withdrawal, interruption and emergency contacts, with a continuity plan.

## Scored domains

Score each item 0 absent, 1 partial/unverified, 2 complete/verified. Multiply by the stated weight.
The assessor records a Drive evidence link, reviewer and date for every score.

| Domain | Weight | Evidence questions |
|---|---:|---|
| A Governance and agreement | 15 | Identity/authority; accountable lead; agreement rights, escalation and audit trail |
| B Scope, safety and legal | 20 | Observational boundaries; legal/insurance match; health/safety; incident and emergency controls |
| C Observable quality system | 20 | Governance/committee; incident learning; indicator definition/display; improvement activity; medication/IPC or equivalent system |
| D Learning and preceptor | 15 | Qualified preceptor/alternate; preparation; protected time; outcome-mapped schedule; feedback |
| E Privacy, consent and information | 15 | Confidentiality; patient choice; access restriction; de-identification; no removal/capture; breach response |
| F Capacity and continuity | 10 | Simultaneous capacity; dated slots; staffing resilience; missed-session/reallocation plan |
| G Access and participant welfare | 5 | Physical/digital accessibility; cultural safety; non-discrimination; travel information and reasonable adjustments |

Convert weighted domain results to 100. Any non-negotiable failure overrides the score.

## Qualification tiers

- **Not qualified:** any non-negotiable failure or score below 70.
- **Minimum:** 70–79; can deliver the core outcome-mapped observational schedule.
- **Preferred:** 80–89; demonstrates multiple mature quality systems, reliable alternate capacity
  and structured feedback.
- **Regional reference:** 90–100; also supports cross-setting case exchange, transparent learning
  review and repeated cohorts without weakening local capacity.

Tier names and thresholds are working proposals reconstructed from the inventory reference. They
require calibration on synthetic and real candidate sites before approval.

## Decision and surveillance

Two reviewers score independently; material disagreements are moderated. Qualification has a
version, permitted capacity, activities, conditions and expiry. Recheck before every allocation and
after material change, incident, complaint or withdrawal. Public naming requires separate consent.
