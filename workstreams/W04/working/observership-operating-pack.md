# W04 observership operating pack

Status: working instruments; agreement terms require local legal review

## Learning outcomes

Participants observe and analyse how a site:

- governs quality and patient safety and assigns accountability;
- reports, reviews and learns from incidents or near misses;
- defines, validates, displays and acts on an indicator;
- designs, tests, documents and sustains improvement;
- manages one safety system such as medication safety or IPC;
- engages staff and, where safely observable, patients/families in improvement.

The assessment judges the participant's analysis, not the host's performance.

## Forty-hour portfolio

1. approved observation plan and boundary acknowledgement;
2. attendance log signed by the preceptor, totalling 40 hours;
3. de-identified quality-governance map;
4. incident-learning pathway analysis using a synthetic or site-approved de-identified case;
5. indicator specification and use analysis without patient-level data;
6. improvement-system observation using a structured field note;
7. safety-system observation;
8. comparison to the participant's own context without ranking or disclosing either organisation;
9. reflection on ethics, equity, culture, feasibility and transfer limits;
10. preceptor confirmation of scope/behaviour and participant feedback on the site.

No photographs, recordings, screenshots, patient identifiers, records, login credentials or copied
internal documents enter the portfolio.

## Preceptor standard

The preceptor has relevant quality/safety experience, understands the outcomes and boundaries,
completes orientation, has protected time, declares conflicts, can create a culturally safe learning
environment, verifies attendance and behaviour, gives structured feedback and escalates concerns.
An alternate meets the same minimum requirements.

## Allocation and capacity

For each site and dated window record approved observation days, hours per day, simultaneous
observer limit, preceptor availability, activity availability and contingency slots.

`raw participant capacity = floor(approved observer-hours / 40)`

The permitted allocation is the lowest of raw capacity, preceptor capacity, room/access limits,
insurance limit, activity-specific capacity and any qualified-site condition. Do not multiply annual
availability into a six-week window. Reserve named contingency capacity before offers are final.

Allocation remains in the participant's country, records accessibility and conflicts, and does not
promise a named site before qualification, agreement and final schedule.

## Agreement term sheet

Country counsel should convert these heads into an approved agreement:

- parties, authority, term, sites and accountable contacts;
- approved activities, exclusions, hours, capacity and participant eligibility;
- preceptor/alternate duties, induction, attendance and feedback;
- patient consent, confidentiality, information security and data-controller roles;
- health, safety, safeguarding, conduct, discrimination and reasonable adjustments;
- insurance, indemnity, liability allocation and evidence maintenance;
- incidents, complaints, investigation cooperation and notification timeframes;
- fees/stipends/in-kind seats only after W16 and partner authority approval;
- IP, publicity, logo/site naming and no unapproved endorsement;
- cancellation, withdrawal, replacement, force majeure, remediation and continuity;
- audit/monitoring, records, governing law, dispute route and authorised signatures.

## Go/no-go record

Before each participant starts, confirm the current site certificate, agreement, insurance/legal
match, named preceptor/alternate, exact schedule, induction, participant acknowledgement, health or
background checks required by local advice, accessibility plan and emergency contact. Any mismatch
pauses the start and triggers reallocation; it is not waived informally.
