# Draft dossier W04-01 — host-site and observership architecture

## 1. The decision requested
Approve the reconstructed standard and instruments for testing; do not qualify sites or promise
capacity.

## 2. Why now
The source file is absent and the observership promise cannot be operationalised from canon alone.

## 3. Canon
Forty hours, month 3 to week 2 of month 4, in-country site, participant/sponsor travel and an
objectives-derived qualification standard are fixed.

## 4. Benchmark
Official observership and placement guidance supports no-hands-on boundaries, named supervision,
induction, confidentiality, patient choice, prepared supervisors and structured feedback.

## 5. Options
A five-day block, B governed block or split schedule, C project-site only. B is recommended.

## 6. Council verdict
Choose B under one evidence and capacity standard.

## 7. Recommendation
Calibrate v0.9 on synthetic then real candidate sites and send the final activity specification to
brokers and country counsel.

## 8. Conditions
Q001, legal advice, authority, two-reviewer calibration, portfolio pilot, preceptors, agreements and
contingency capacity precede allocation.

## 9. Proposed canon after approval
None. Qualification results and final rules require later decisions.

## 10. What this unblocks
Comparable site assessment, defensible capacity planning and matched insurance/legal instructions.

## 11. Innovation opportunities
A portable qualified-site register, outcome-based capacity and cross-setting learning evidence.

## 12. What we could not establish
Original standard, site availability, insurance, country rules, signatory authority, preceptors or
capacity.
