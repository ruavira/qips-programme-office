## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What must a participant observe, where can that be observed, and how is it made safe, lawful and assessable?

## What it must produce

- Host Site Standard and scored assessment instrument
- Observership curriculum, instruments and portfolio specification
- Host site agreement pack and preceptor standard
- Rotation calendar and concurrency model
- Indemnity, scope-of-practice and safeguarding protocol

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Clinical placement supervision models and supervisor time evidence
2. Observership vs internship: liability, scope and regulatory treatment
3. Placement capacity planning and concurrency in health systems
4. In-country placement networks in LMIC settings

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- AUC Cairo field visits
- IHI Breakthrough Series action periods
- Australian clinical placement subsidy models
