# W07 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-28

## Framed question

Which admissions architecture should cohort one use when applicant demand, final entry thresholds,
credential level and scholarship budget are not yet established?

## Advisor positions

### Contrarian

Use eligibility plus rolling first-qualified offers if verified demand is below capacity. A
competitive scoring system adds burden and false precision where no selection problem exists.

**Strongest objection to this position:** application timing and information access determine who
gets a place when qualified demand exceeds capacity.

### First Principles

Separate eligibility, evidence scoring and ability to take up the place. Use two independent
reviews and moderation because the programme is selecting for applied readiness, not prestige.

**Strongest objection to this position:** a numerical rubric can conceal judgement behind decimal
totals unless anchors and evidence locations are visible.

### Executor

Adopt the double-review model, automate the normalised calculation and trigger moderation only
for material differences. Keep document and project-access conditions outside the numerical score.

**Strongest objection to this position:** reviewing every eligible application twice may be
disproportionate if Q006 reveals high volume.

### Outsider

Accept employer nominations, open applications and employer-equivalent applications under one
standard. Exclude title, institutional prestige, sponsorship and ability to pay from merit scoring.

**Strongest objection to this position:** equivalent routes can become inconsistent unless project
access and protected time have common evidence rules.

### Expansionist

Use the same evidence model as the base for later scholarships, institutional cohorts and regional
recruitment, with observed funnel events and outcomes feeding future thresholds.

**Strongest objection to this position:** future analytics cannot justify collecting unnecessary
personal data in cohort one.

## Convergence and clash

Four advisors favoured eligibility, two independent scores and moderation. The Contrarian preferred
rolling offers only where demand is demonstrably below capacity. All rejected prestige and ability
to pay as selection criteria and rejected setting a threshold from synthetic cases.

## Chairman's synthesis

Advance **Option B: eligibility, two independent evidence scores and moderated selection**, with
institutional nomination as one source under the same standard. If Q006 later proves eligible demand
below capacity, the CCC may simplify the offer sequence without weakening the eligibility and
evidence records.

The chairman did not overrule the majority.

## Conditions

1. Use the normalised 100-point formula on every score record.
2. Keep eligibility, evidence sufficiency, competitive scoring and financial support separate.
3. Trigger moderation at ten total points or a two-level criterion difference for the initial
   live pilot; review that trigger after observed data.
4. Do not approve an entry threshold, cohort cap or country allocation from synthetic evidence.
5. W01 personas, W03 credential level and Q006 remain prerequisites for final public thresholds.
6. Scholarship decisions remain separate unless the CCC later approves a transparent combined rule.
