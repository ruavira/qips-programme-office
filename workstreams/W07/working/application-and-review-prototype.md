# W07 application and review prototype

Status: process prototype; final questions and thresholds await W01, W03 and CCC decisions

Date: 2026-07-28

## Data boundary

GitHub stores this blank instrument and aggregate funnel definitions only. Names, contact details,
identity/qualification files, adjustment information, references, employer details and reviewer
notes belong in an approved restricted system. Base44 may show aggregate status but must not become
the uncontrolled applicant-document store.

## Stage 1 — application

### Applicant context

- country connection and current location;
- clinical, non-clinical or transition pathway;
- current organisation or independent/project context;
- qualification and experience evidence required by the final threshold;
- working-language declaration and requested accessibility support route.

### Improvement challenge

In a bounded response, describe the service or system problem, who experiences it, the current
evidence, the setting/team to which you have lawful access, what is realistically changeable during
the programme and what remains outside your control. Do not submit patient-identifiable data.

### Initiative and follow-through

Describe one specific example of work you sustained or completed: your role, constraints, actions,
evidence of follow-through, what changed, what did not change and what you learned. Claims may be
verified with the named responsible contact.

### Completion plan

- ordinary-week plan for about four hours;
- live-session and monthly coaching-call feasibility;
- capstone access and responsible contact;
- 40-hour in-country observership route and travel implications;
- peak-period risks and mitigation;
- employer protected-time undertaking or equivalent plan.

### Reflection and contribution

- capability gaps the applicant expects the programme to expose;
- an example of using difficult feedback;
- how learning could transfer to a team, service or underserved setting;
- support or reasonable adjustment requested, collected separately from scoring where practicable.

### Declarations

- accuracy and verification consent;
- conflicts and relationships with programme personnel/partners;
- conduct and academic-integrity commitment;
- privacy notice acknowledgement;
- no patient-identifiable or unauthorised organisational information submitted;
- scholarship interest collected separately from academic/practice scoring.

## Stage 2 — administrative eligibility and evidence check

Record `met`, `conditional`, `not met` or `not applicable` for each published requirement. Staff do
not infer merit, rank applicants or reject an adjustment request at this stage. Incomplete evidence
receives the same documented cure period where the calendar permits it.

## Stage 3 — independent scoring

Each reviewer records 0–4, evidence location and one-sentence rationale for every criterion. The
system computes `(rating / 4) × weight`; reviewers never type or alter the total. They also record:

- project access: verified / conditional / not established;
- protected-time route: employer / equivalent / conditional / not established;
- conflict: none / recused;
- evidence sufficiency: sufficient / clarification required / insufficient;
- possible adjustment-processing issue, without scoring the protected information.

## Stage 4 — reconciliation and moderation

Reconciliation is triggered by the approved total-score difference or criterion-level difference.
Reviewers cite the anchor and evidence; they do not average unexplained impressions. The moderated
panel records the ranked outcome, tie-break rule, lawful equity rule, conflicts, conditions and
reason. Ability to pay, scholarship request, title seniority and institutional prestige are absent
from the decision fields.

## Stage 5 — conditional offer and verification

Offers identify unresolved document, access, payment/sponsorship or observership conditions and a
deadline. A conditional offer is not converted to enrolled status until the applicable conditions
are verified. The reconsideration route addresses process or evidence errors, not a fresh attempt
to improve the original narrative after the deadline.

## Employer support or equivalent record

Both routes confirm the same underlying facts: lawful access to an improvement context, about four
hours in an ordinary week, required live/coaching participation, capstone support, a feasible
observership, a responsible contact and no expectation that confidential patient data will be
shared with QIPS. Employment status itself is not the competence test.

## Funnel events

Timestamp and deduplicate: contactable enquiry, application started, completed, eligible, scored,
moderated, offered, accepted, deposit/payment confirmed, enrolled, pre-start withdrawal and
completion. Report observed rates only after denominators close; label any scenario assumption as
an assumption and keep it separate from observed QIPS performance.
