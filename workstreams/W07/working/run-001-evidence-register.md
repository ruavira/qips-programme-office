# W07 run 001 — evidence register

Accessed: 2026-07-28

This register supports admissions-process design. It does not approve final eligibility thresholds, scholarships, cohort size or conversion assumptions.

## E-W07-001 — supplied brochure content master

- Source: `QIPS-Foundation-Professional-Program-Brochure-Content-Master.docx`
- Status: verified internal source; all 12 rendered pages inspected during the programme-office audit
- Supports: the intended audience spans clinical and non-clinical health-sector roles in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.
- Control finding: minimum qualifications, experience, documents, application steps and selection criteria are all marked `TO CONFIRM`.

## E-W07-002 — Africa CDC Kofi Annan selection model

- Publisher: Africa Centres for Disease Control and Prevention
- Source: https://africacdc.org/kofi-annan-global-health-leadership-programme/
- Status: verified official programme source
- Supports: published eligibility, employer support, recommendation evidence, a bounded project statement and selection by an independent expert committee.
- Does not establish: suitable QIPS thresholds; the comparator is more senior and fellowship-oriented.

## E-W07-003 — Commonwealth Scholarship criteria

- Publisher: Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK
- Source: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/advice-for-applicants/
- Status: verified official programme source
- Supports: applications are assessed against published dimensions including merit, development impact and the proposed study/research plan.
- Does not establish: that academic merit should dominate a practice-based QIPS selection.

## E-W07-004 — WHO fellowship selection principles

- Publisher: World Health Organization, Eastern Mediterranean Region
- Source: https://www.emro.who.int/educational-development/information-resources/fellowships.html
- Status: verified official programme source
- Supports: selection should relate to documented health-service priorities and use an equitable committee process.
- Does not establish: QIPS applicant thresholds or a requirement for government nomination.

## E-W07-005 — Wellcome staged assessment and independent scoring

- Publisher: Wellcome
- Source: https://wellcome.org/research-funding/guidance/how-wellcome-makes-funding-decisions/how-wellcome-assesses-funding-applications
- Status: verified official process source
- Supports: eligibility checks can be separated from scored assessment, independent reviewers can score before committee ranking, and conflicts/equity require explicit controls.
- Does not establish: the content or weights of a QIPS rubric.

## E-W07-006 — WHO GOARN project-linked fellowship

- Publisher: World Health Organization Hub for Pandemic and Epidemic Intelligence
- Source: https://pandemichub.who.int/news-room/news/item/18-02-2026-call-for-applications-5th-cohort-of-the-goarn-berlin-fellowship-programme
- Status: verified official programme source
- Supports: eligibility can be tied to institutional role and an applied project that returns capability to the home institution.
- Does not establish: that employer sponsorship must be the only access route for QIPS.

## Research conclusion

Strong comparators separate eligibility from competitive selection, publish the criteria, require evidence linked to programme purpose and use independent committee review. QIPS should score readiness to complete an applied improvement programme, not prestige. Employer support is valuable but needs an equivalent route for self-employed, early-career or otherwise suitable applicants if the CCC wants those brochure audiences to remain eligible.
