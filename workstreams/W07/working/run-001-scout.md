# W07 run 001 — scout record

Date started: 2026-07-28

Stage: SCOUTING

Purpose: design a transparent, moderated admissions process that protects the programme standard while remaining accessible to clinical and non-clinical health-sector applicants in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.

## Canonical questions and dependencies in scope

- F018 establishes that admissions criteria are set on best practice.
- Q002 must settle the credential before final applicant claims and requirements are published.
- Q006 must provide the demand denominator before the funnel and cohort forecast can be approved.
- W01 must provide evidence-backed learner personas; W03 must settle the credential architecture.

## Outputs this run will assemble

1. Minimum eligibility screen and evidence checklist.
2. Structured application form and scored selection rubric.
3. Independent moderation, conflict and tie-break procedure.
4. Employer-support and protected-time undertaking.
5. Access, reasonable-adjustment and scholarship principles.
6. Funnel measurement specification that does not invent conversion rates.

## Evidence plan

- Compare Africa CDC, Commonwealth Scholarship, WHO fellowship and other transparent selection systems.
- Prefer application evidence tied to programme completion: role, live improvement opportunity, protected time, motivation and ability to participate.
- Separate eligibility from competitive ranking and financial-aid decisions.
- Record stage-by-stage counts before setting any conversion assumption.

## Human inputs that cannot be invented

- Approved minimum qualification and experience thresholds.
- Whether employer sponsorship is mandatory for all applicants or whether an equivalent access route is allowed.
- Scholarship budget and equity priorities.
- Named selection panel and conflict declarations.
- Existing SQHN enquiry, application, deposit and completion denominators.

## Exit criteria for scouting

- Every criterion is observable, scoreable and tied to programme success or fairness.
- The application can be moderated without relying on prestige, nationality or unsupported intuition.
- The funnel records facts before it forecasts.
- Final publication remains gated by W01, W03 and the relevant CCC decisions.
