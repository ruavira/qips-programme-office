# W07 admissions and selection working pack

Status: research recommendation; final criteria await W01, W03 and CCC decisions

Date: 2026-07-28

## Design rule

Admissions has three separate gates: eligibility, competitive selection and ability to take up the place. Financial need, scholarship allocation and marketing priority must not silently change the academic/practice score.

## Proposed eligibility screen

An application proceeds to scoring only when it includes evidence of:

- residence/work connection to an approved cohort-1 country;
- a clinical or non-clinical role, qualification or credible entry pathway relevant to healthcare quality and patient safety;
- sufficient working-language ability for the published programme language;
- access to a real improvement problem, team or service context suitable for the capstone;
- ability to complete the published time, live-session, coaching and observership requirements, with reasonable adjustments where needed;
- employer/sponsor protected-time support, or an approved equivalent plan for self-employed, between-role or early-career applicants;
- identity and qualification documents defined in the final application checklist;
- agreement to conduct, academic-integrity, privacy and observership rules.

The minimum qualification and experience thresholds remain undecided. They must follow the learner-persona evidence and credential level, not be chosen for exclusivity.

## Proposed scored rubric

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence and scoring anchor |
|---|---:|---|
| Relevance of role or intended pathway | 15 | Clear connection to healthcare quality/safety responsibilities or a credible transition plan |
| Improvement challenge | 25 | Specific, important and feasible problem with access to a setting/team; no invented outcome promise |
| Evidence of initiative and follow-through | 20 | A concrete example showing the applicant completed or sustained meaningful work |
| Protected-time and completion plan | 15 | Credible weekly plan, sponsor/equivalent support and observership feasibility |
| Reflection and learning readiness | 15 | Identifies limits, feedback needs and how the programme will change practice |
| Contribution and equity impact | 10 | Potential to transfer learning to teams, underserved settings or system priorities |

Score each criterion from 0–4 using written anchors. Multiply by the weight. Digital access is assessed for support planning, not used as a prestige proxy. Disability, caring responsibility or inability to secure a conventional employer letter must trigger an accommodation/equivalent-evidence route, not automatic rejection.

## Evidence anchors

- **0 — absent:** no relevant evidence or the response does not address the criterion.
- **1 — weak:** general aspiration with little example, access or feasibility.
- **2 — adequate:** relevant example and plausible plan, but material gaps remain.
- **3 — strong:** specific evidence, realistic constraints and clear transfer to practice.
- **4 — exceptional:** sustained evidence, reflective insight, feasible leverage and benefit beyond the individual without overclaiming.

## Selection workflow

1. Administrative staff run the published eligibility and completeness check.
2. Two trained reviewers independently score anonymised narrative fields where practicable.
3. Material score differences trigger reconciliation against the anchors, not bargaining over impressions.
4. The moderated panel reviews ranked applications, declared conflicts, country/sector coverage and access adjustments.
5. Tie-breaks use the highest improvement-challenge score, then protected-time feasibility, then documented equity priority approved in advance.
6. Decisions and reasons are recorded; unsuccessful applicants receive a consistent notification and any published reconsideration route.
7. Offers are conditional on document verification, acceptance, payment/sponsorship and any required employer undertaking.

## Conflict and fairness controls

- Reviewers declare personal, organisational, supervisory and financial conflicts.
- A conflicted reviewer does not score or advocate on that application.
- Institutional prestige, title seniority, personal familiarity and ability to pay are not rubric criteria.
- Scholarship decisions are made after the selection score unless the CCC approves a transparent combined mechanism.
- Aggregate monitoring examines country, profession, sector, gender and other lawful equity indicators without publishing sensitive small-cell data.

## Employer-support or equivalent undertaking

The undertaking should confirm:

- the applicant's role and access to a suitable improvement context;
- approximately four hours per ordinary week plus published peak activities;
- permission for required live sessions, coaching, data collection and capstone work;
- support for the in-country 40-hour observership and any travel obligations;
- a named workplace contact;
- no guarantee that confidential patient or organisational data will be shared with QIPS;
- whether the organisation pays fees and the applicable withdrawal/refund responsibilities.

An equivalent route should require the applicant to show lawful project access, protected time and a responsible workplace/project contact without forcing a false employment relationship.

## Application evidence set

- identity and contact information;
- country, profession/role, employer or operating context;
- CV and qualification evidence appropriate to the final threshold;
- improvement challenge statement;
- initiative/follow-through example;
- completion and protected-time plan;
- employer support or equivalent plan;
- reasonable-adjustment request route separated from scoring;
- conflict/relationship disclosure;
- privacy notice and declarations;
- scholarship request collected separately where practicable.

## Funnel measurement specification

Record counts and timestamps for: unique contactable enquiries, started applications, completed applications, eligible applications, scored applications, offers, accepted offers, deposits/payments, enrolled participants, withdrawals before start and completed participants. Record source, country, sector and approved campaign tags.

Do not populate a forecast with guessed conversion rates. The first approved model should show scenario ranges separately from observed QIPS rates and replace assumptions after each stage closes.

## Decisions required before publication

- minimum qualification and experience threshold;
- employer support as mandatory or one of multiple equivalent routes;
- scoring weights, minimum score and any country/sector constraints;
- scholarship budget and equity priority;
- selection-panel membership and authority;
- application calendar, evidence list and reconsideration route;
- cohort target/cap after the demand denominator and delivery capacity are reconciled.

## Exit criteria

W07 can progress to options and council when W01 personas and W03 credential level are available, the rubric has been trial-scored on synthetic applications, reviewer consistency is acceptable, the protected-time/equivalent route is approved and Q006 supplies an observed demand denominator.
