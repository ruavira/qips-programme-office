## The question this workstream exists to answer

> Who is eligible, how do we choose between applicants, and how do we fill a cohort without lowering the bar?

## What it must produce

- Eligibility criteria and published entry requirements
- Application form, selection rubric and moderation process
- Sponsorship undertaking and employer release agreement
- Scholarship and equity policy with selection method
- Recruitment funnel model with conversion assumptions

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Selection methods that predict completion in professional programmes
2. Equity of access and widening participation in LMIC professional education
3. Employer release and protected-time agreements
4. Funnel benchmarks: enquiry to application to deposit to enrolment

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- Africa CDC Kofi Annan Fellowship selection
- Health Foundation GenerationQ selection
- Commonwealth Scholarship selection methodology
