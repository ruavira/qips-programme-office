# DOSSIER W07-01 — Admissions, Recruitment and Selection
**For decision at the next CCC sitting · Prepared by workstream W07 · Run 1 · Verification: PASS WITH FINDINGS**

## 1. The decision requested

Approve eligibility screening, two independent evidence scores and moderated selection, with
institutional nomination as one application source under the same standard.

## 2. Why it is on the agenda now

Applications open in October 2026. The programme needs a controlled process before forms and
reviewer recruitment are built, even though Q006, W01 personas and W03 credential level still
prevent final thresholds and capacity decisions.

## 3. What canon already establishes

- F012: applications open October 2026 and close 31 December 2026.
- F018: admissions criteria are set on best practice.
- F020: cohort one recruits in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.
- F021: unconfirmed numbers and claims are withheld.
- F024 and F025 are PROPOSED and may not be used as approved price or capacity.

## 5. The options

### Option A — rolling first-qualified offers

Simple and low-burden, but timing and information access dominate when demand exceeds capacity.
Defensible only if verified eligible demand is below capacity.

### Option B — eligibility, double scoring and moderation

Transparent and auditable, but requires two reviewers, training, calibration and reconsideration.
Recommended for cohort one; total review cost depends on Q006.

### Option C — employer-nominated institutional cohort

Strong workplace support and payment potential, but can reproduce organisational hierarchy and
exclude suitable independent or early-career applicants. Useful as one source, not the sole model.

## 6. Council verdict

Four advisors favoured Option B. The Contrarian favoured Option A only if demand is demonstrably
below capacity. All rejected prestige, sponsorship and ability to pay as merit criteria and rejected
setting a threshold from synthetic cases. The chairman adopted Option B and did not overrule the
majority.

## 7. Recommendation

Approve Option B and allow institutional nomination as one source under the same standard. Approve
the process architecture, normalised formula and initial moderation trigger; keep the threshold,
cap, allocation and forecast unapproved.

## 8. Conditions

1. By 15 September 2026, Q006 supplies the historic denominator and conversion record or records
   them as not available after a named search.
2. Before the application form is published, W01 personas and W03 credential level are reconciled
   to the eligibility wording.
3. Before live scoring, reviewers calibrate on the eighteen fictional cases and declare conflicts.
4. For the initial live pilot, moderation triggers at ten total points or a two-level criterion
   difference; the CCC reviews the rule after observed data.
5. Before applications open, the form, calculation, evidence gates, reasonable-adjustment route and
   reconsideration process pass an end-to-end test.

## 9. What becomes canon if you approve

1. “Cohort-one admissions separates eligibility, evidence sufficiency, competitive selection and
   financial support.”
2. “Eligible applications receive two independent evidence scores and moderation of material
   differences; institutional nomination is one source under the same standard.”
3. “Title, institutional prestige, sponsorship, ability to pay and reasonable-adjustment need do
   not increase or reduce the merit score.”

No existing fact is superseded.

## 10. What this unblocks

The decision allows W07, W09, W10 and W14 to build forms, roles, dates and event tracking against a
stable process. It does not establish capacity or conversion, so Q006 remains open and no dependent
canon decision is implied.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

- Use paired evidence tests to calibrate reviewers before each cycle.
- Publish an applicant-facing explanation of evidence anchors and reconsideration.
- Preserve an employer-equivalent route while using one standard for all sources.
- Feed observed funnel and completion evidence into later thresholds without collecting unnecessary
  personal data.

## 12. What we could not establish

- Eligible demand, conversion or reviewer volume.
- Final qualification and experience thresholds.
- A score threshold related to observed completion.
- Approved cohort cap, country allocation or scholarship budget.
- Named panel members and total review cost.

## 13. Verification report

Verdict: PASS WITH FINDINGS; no blocking finding remains. The verifier recalculated all eighteen
fictional cases, confirmed the extended-set diagnostics, exercised the proposed moderation trigger,
separated project access from challenge scoring and prevented synthetic agreement from being
represented as reliability. Threshold, capacity and conversion claims remain withheld.
