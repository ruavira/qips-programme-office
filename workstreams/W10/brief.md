**Directorate D3 · Platform and Operations**
Maps to the owner's original list: *(j) cohort management*
Proposed cohort-1 position: **NOW**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> How does one month actually run, and how does a virtual programme stay genuinely open to people in time zones we did not design around?

## What it must produce

- Master cohort calendar with every date named
- Time zone and global participation model
- Monthly operating runbook with roles and handoffs
- Attendance, participation and make-up policy
- Incident and escalation protocol
- Cohort dashboard and weekly operating rhythm

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Time zone strategies in global cohort-based programmes
2. Synchronous versus asynchronous evidence for working professionals
3. Operating cadence and staffing ratios for cohort programmes
4. Religious, national and academic calendar conflicts across target countries

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI virtual programmes
- ISQua global education
- Global MBA cohort operations
- Africa CDC blended fellowship operations
