# Draft dossier W01-01 — needs research architecture

## 1. The decision requested
Approve the segmentation and field protocol, not the persona hypotheses or demand conclusions.

## 2. Why now
W07 eligibility, W14 demand design and W16 capacity require local evidence rather than brochure
audience descriptions.

## 3. Canon
Three countries, the 12-month rhythm, workload and timing are established; personas and eligibility
are not.

## 4. Benchmark
WHO and NAHQ support multi-professional, competency-based analysis; IHI tests workplace-project
access; free regulatory learning is a real substitute.

## 5. Options
A title-led, B task-and-context, C cadre-led. B is recommended.

## 6. Council verdict
Advance B with explicit disconfirmation and sampling-funnel reporting.

## 7. Recommendation
Field the practitioner and employer instruments in all three countries.

## 8. Conditions
Privacy approval, verified recruitment channels, no patient data, restricted transcripts, diverse
coverage and a supported/contradicted/unknown result for every hypothesis.

## 9. Proposed canon after approval
None. Human results require a later dossier.

## 10. What this unblocks
Primary data collection and later eligibility, demand and capacity decisions.

## 11. Innovation opportunities
Task-based personas, informal-quality-work mapping and a reusable country comparison dataset.

## 12. What we could not establish
Validated personas, market size, employer demand, willingness to pay, conversion or readiness.

## 13. Verification report
PASS WITH BLOCKING FINDINGS for fieldwork design; not passed for market conclusions.
