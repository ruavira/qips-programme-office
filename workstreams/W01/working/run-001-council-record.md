# W01 run 001 — independent council record

Status: advisory synthesis; not a CCC decision

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

What segmentation and research design should govern persona and need validation?

## Synthesis

The council rejected title-only personas. The Contrarian required disconfirming evidence and warned
against turning brochure audiences into facts. First Principles chose task, context, authority and
access as the segmentation base. The Executor required usable interview and coding instruments.
The Outsider required informal quality workers, primary/community settings and self-sponsored
routes. The Expansionist required institutional sponsor and payer/regulator segments.

Advance **task-and-context segmentation** and the three-country field toolkit. Results must show
who was invited and completed, not merely polished quotations.

## Conditions

No persona, eligibility threshold, market size, willingness-to-pay figure or demand forecast becomes
canon from this preparatory run. W01 returns after human fielding with de-identified evidence.
