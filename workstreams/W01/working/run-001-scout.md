# W01 run 001 — scout

Date: 2026-07-29

## Framed question

Which people and organisations have a real healthcare quality and patient-safety capability gap in
Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan, and what evidence is still required before eligibility, capacity and
demand claims can be approved?

## Canon and inherited material

Canon establishes the three countries, programme rhythm, workload and dates. The brochure contains
seven audience descriptions, but they are design hypotheses rather than validated personas. The
reported pricing evidence base is absent, and no interview transcript, survey dataset, job-advert
sample or employer demand register was found in GitHub or connected Drive.

## Run boundary

This run builds an evidence-backed segmentation hypothesis and field instruments. It does not claim
market size, conversion, willingness to pay, eligibility or persona validation. Real people must
provide the primary evidence; AI synthesis is preparation only.
