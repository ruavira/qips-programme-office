# W01 — Needs Assessment and Market Intelligence

**Directorate D1 · Programme**
Maps to the owner's original list: *(a) needs assessment and market analysis*
Proposed cohort-1 position: **NOW**

## The question this workstream exists to answer

> Who needs this programme, what capability gap does it close, and how do we know that gap is real rather than assumed?

## What it must produce

- Training needs analysis with primary and secondary evidence
- Learner persona set with sourced characteristics
- Market sizing and demand signal per country
- Competitor and substitute map, refreshed each cohort

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Published LMIC health-workforce capability gap evidence
2. Employer-side demand: what job adverts and regulators actually ask for
3. Learner-side demand: what this audience already buys and why
4. Substitutes and free alternatives that could take the market

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI Improvement Advisor
- ISQua Fellowship
- Africa CDC Kofi Annan Fellowship
- WHO/POCQI regional programmes
