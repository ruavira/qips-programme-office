## The question this workstream exists to answer

> How do we get people to the end, and what happens to them afterwards that makes the credential compound?

## What it must produce

- Retention model with at-risk detection and intervention protocol
- Learner support and accessibility standard
- Deferral, withdrawal and re-entry policy
- Alumni network and community design
- Career pathway and progression to advanced tiers

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Attrition drivers in long-duration online professional programmes
2. Early-warning indicators and what interventions actually work
3. Professional community and alumni network design
4. Career outcome evidence for quality and safety credentials

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI Open School community
- ISQua Fellowship alumni network
- Africa CDC fellowship alumni structures
