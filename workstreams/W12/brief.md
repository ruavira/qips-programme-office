## The question this workstream exists to answer

> What data does this programme generate, who owns it, and what is it worth beyond the programme?

## What it must produce

- Data asset inventory including the capstone improvement dataset
- Consent, ownership and publication terms in the participant agreement
- Research and publication strategy
- Outcome dataset specification and baseline standard
- Reporting pack for partners, regulators and funders

## Research lenses

Each run fans out parallel researchers, one per lens. A lens is a way of looking, not a
topic — two researchers on the same topic with different lenses find different things.

1. Improvement project datasets and what makes them publishable
2. Research ethics and consent for education-derived data in multiple jurisdictions
3. SQUIRE 2.0 and reporting standards for improvement work
4. How education programmes convert learner work into institutional evidence

## Benchmarks it must compare against

Every dossier from this workstream carries a comparison table against these, with a
column for what each does, a column for what we would do differently, and a column
stating why the difference is an improvement rather than a shortcut.

- IHI published collaborative results
- Health Foundation evaluation outputs
- Breakthrough Series published outcomes
