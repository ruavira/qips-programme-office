# W12 run 001 — council record

## Options considered

| Option | Advantage | Failure mode | Council position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Broad bundled consent for all reuse | Administratively simple | Not specific, potentially coercive and cannot cure unnecessary collection | Reject |
| No secondary use in cohort 1 | Lowest immediate research risk | Discards the opportunity to build governed evidence | Keep as fallback |
| Purpose-separated minimum dataset with optional governed research gate | Preserves operations and future evidence without automatic reuse | Requires legal, ethics and ownership discipline | Recommend |

## Chairman synthesis

The asset is not “the data”; it is a lawful, well-described and reproducible evidence chain. Patient-identifiable information never belongs in participant artefacts. No retention period, legal basis or Pakistan-law claim may be invented.

## Conditions

- W17 obtains jurisdictional advice and resolves Q009.
- W15/W17 agreements assign controller, processor, IP and publication authority.
- W03/W11 define necessary assessment/evaluation fields.
- Research use passes a separate classification and ethics/privacy gate.
