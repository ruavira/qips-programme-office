# Draft dossier W12-01 — data, consent and research assets

Status: working draft; outside the current CCC agenda

## Decision requested later

Approve the four-purpose classification, minimum data inventory, patient-data prohibition, retention template and research/publication gate. Do not approve a legal basis, duration, system, cross-border transfer or research protocol through this dossier.

## Recommendation

Collect the minimum necessary for operations and governed evaluation; separate optional research/publication; prohibit patient-identifiable artefacts; and make every research extract reproducible and disclosure-reviewed.

## Blocking evidence

Q009, jurisdictional advice, signed partner/data/IP terms, field-level specifications, ethics authority and restricted-system selection.

## Canon effect

None. This draft creates controls but no approved legal or publication claim.
