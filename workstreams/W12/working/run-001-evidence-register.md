# W12 run 001 — evidence register

Status: working evidence, not legal advice; accessed 29 July 2026

| Source | Finding used | Data/research implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf) | Nigeria has an enacted data-protection law and regulator framework. | Obtain Nigerian counsel/DPO review for roles, bases, rights, transfers, security and breach duties. |
| [Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843)](https://dataprotection.org.gh/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Data-Protection-Act-2012-Act-843.pdf) | Ghana regulates controllers and processing, including registration, security and retention principles. | Confirm controller registration, supervisor, purpose, security and cross-border obligations before collection. |
| [Ghana DPC organisation guidance](https://dataprotection.org.gh/for-organisations/) | The regulator calls for registration, a trained data-protection supervisor, privacy information and appropriate technical/organisational measures. | Treat Ghana operational compliance evidence as a launch gate. |
| [Pakistan Ministry draft Personal Data Protection Bill 2023](https://moitt.gov.pk/SiteImage/Misc/files/Final%20Draft%20Personal%20Data%20Protection%20Bill%20May%202023.pdf) | The located instrument is explicitly a draft, not proof of enacted comprehensive law. | Obtain current Pakistan legal advice; do not describe the draft as governing law. |
| [SQUIRE 2.0](https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/squire/) | QI reporting covers context, intervention, study, results and ethical considerations. | Structure project evidence for responsible reporting while classifying research separately. |
| [SQUIRE explanation](https://qualitysafety.bmj.com/content/25/12/e7) | Ethical concerns, including privacy and potential harms, should be reviewed and reported. | Build an ethics/privacy classification gate before reuse or publication. |
| [Zoom meeting history reports](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0073594) | Operational exports may include identity and detailed attendance traces. | Do not collect/export merely because the platform exposes them. |
