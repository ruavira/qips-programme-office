## The question this workstream exists to answer

> Who is legally responsible for what, in which jurisdiction, and what happens when something goes wrong?

## What it must produce

- Governance structure, terms of reference and decision rights
- Contracting entity, participant terms and consumer-facing policies
- Data protection compliance across Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan
- Indemnity and insurance programme
- Risk register with owners, triggers and contingency
- Safeguarding, ethics and conflict of interest policy
