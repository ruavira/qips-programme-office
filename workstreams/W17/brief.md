### 2026-08-01 — F027 go/no-go criteria, DEFERRED

The committee deferred F027 (*"Go/no-go kill date is 20 November 2026: 40 paid deposits, 9 signed
host-site MoUs, 12 consented faculty, all three at once"*) at Item 7 of the sitting of 1 August 2026.
Minute: `ccc/minutes/2026-08-01.md`. Two conflicts must be resolved, not restated.

**Constraint 1 — the go/no-go gate must be satisfiable by the admissions process that was actually
approved.** F012 is approved canon: applications close 31 December 2026. W07-01, approved at Item 4 of
the same sitting, adopted eligibility screening, then two independent evidence scores, then moderation,
then offers — over a closed field. A gate requiring 40 *paid deposits* on 20 November therefore demands
an outcome the approved process cannot produce forty-one days before its own field closes. Only rolling
first-qualified offers could, and that is Option A, which the council rejected.

Any future version must either measure something observable on 20 November under a moderated process
(for example qualified applications received against a stated conversion assumption, with deposits as a
second checkpoint in December), or propose a supersession of F012 with its own reasoning. It must not
re-propose 40 paid deposits on 20 November against an unchanged F012.

**Constraint 2 — faculty coverage counts must include backup.** W05-01 condition 3, approved at Item 3,
requires every required slot to hold a primary and a backup, or an approved risk treatment. A go/no-go
criterion of "12 consented faculty" counts twelve primaries and zero backups, and so would pass a gate
that the faculty operating model treats as failing. Any future version states either the full primary
plus backup figure, or twelve primaries together with an approved risk treatment for backup coverage.

**Not in dispute.** The committee did not reject the idea of a dated kill gate, the 20 November date
itself, or the three-part conjunctive structure ("all three at once"). Those are sound and should be
retained.
