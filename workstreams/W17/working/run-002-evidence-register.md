# W17 run 002 — evidence register

Accessed: 2026-07-29

This run combines governance, data and insurance evidence into a launch-control design. It is not
country-specific legal advice and does not quote or bind coverage.

## E-W17-007 — Nigeria GAID 2025

- Publisher: Nigeria Data Protection Commission
- Source: https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf
- Type: official directive
- Supports: the 2025 directive operationalises the NDP Act and addresses general compliance,
  controller/processor designation and registration, compliance returns and DPO designation.
- Does not establish: the programme's classification, lawful bases, transfer route, retention
  schedule or named DPO.

## E-W17-008 — Ghana organisational requirements

- Publisher: Data Protection Commission, Ghana
- Source: https://dataprotection.org.gh/for-organisations/
- Type: official regulator guidance
- Supports: organisations processing personal data in Ghana must address registration, renewal,
  data-protection supervision, privacy information and security; the regulator also identifies DPIA
  steps for new or materially changed processing.
- Does not establish: which QIPS party performs each duty or the approved record periods.

## E-W17-009 — Nigeria insurance-market verification route

- Publisher: National Insurance Commission, Nigeria
- Source: https://portal.naicom.gov.ng/Public/Public_Introduction.aspx
- Type: official regulator portal
- Supports: the portal enables verification of insurance policies and vendor/operator credentials.
- Does not establish: that any broker, insurer or policy is suitable for QIPS.

## E-W17-010 — Ghana insurance regulator and licensed entities

- Publisher: National Insurance Commission, Ghana
- Sources: https://nicgh.org/ and https://nicgh.org/intermediaries/brokers/
- Type: official regulator sources
- Supports: NIC licenses and supervises insurance entities and publishes licensed intermediaries;
  current status should be checked before engagement.
- Does not establish: QIPS coverage, price, territorial scope or exclusions.

## E-W17-011 — Pakistan insurance regulator route

- Publisher: Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan
- Sources: https://www.secp.gov.pk/licensing/insurance-companies-licensing/insurance-companies/
  and https://www.secp.gov.pk/data-and-statistics/insurance-companies/
- Type: official regulator sources
- Supports: insurers and brokers operate within a registration/licensing framework and SECP
  publishes market lists and requirements.
- Does not establish: QIPS coverage, admitted-placement requirements or broker suitability.

## E-W17-012 — Pakistan cyber-law status

- Publisher: Pakistan Code, Ministry of Law and Justice
- Source: https://www.pakistancode.gov.pk/english/LGu0xBD?action=notactive&page=1&year=2016
- Type: official legislation database
- Supports: the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 is an enacted federal instrument.
- Does not establish: a complete QIPS personal-data regime or resolve the status of proposed
  dedicated personal-data legislation.

## Evidence conclusion

Regulator sources provide routes to verify legal obligations and market actors, but the programme
still lacks purpose-specific counsel opinions, a named accountable privacy contact, approved record
periods and written insurance terms. Q001 and Q009 remain open. Those gaps require launch gates, not
invented answers.
