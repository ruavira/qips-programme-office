# QIPS governance, risk and launch standard v0.9

Status: restricted working design; not legal advice, insurance or CCC approval

Date: 2026-07-29

## Governance model

SQHN is the lead sponsor and payment-taking contracting entity under F014. That does not by itself
answer who may sign, who controls each processing purpose, or how partners share risk. Those matters
must be evidenced in a delegated-authority and responsibility schedule.

### Decision layers

| Layer | May decide | May not decide |
|---|---|---|
| Owner / sponsor | Strategic intent and matters expressly reserved to the owner | Quiet canon amendments or unapproved external commitments |
| CCC | Canon, open questions, cohort scope, public facts and decision dossiers | Operational shortcuts that contradict law or signed instruments |
| Programme director | Delivery within approved canon, budget, terms and controls | Price, credential, affiliation or risk acceptance reserved to CCC/owner |
| Workstream lead | Research, working designs and recommendations | Binding another party or publishing a PROPOSED fact |
| Country/host/partner role | Duties delegated in a signed instrument | Implied authority outside that instrument |

Every material decision records the question, authority, evidence, decision, conditions, owner,
date, review trigger and repository/minute reference.

## Launch assurance case

Cohort one does not launch because documents exist; it launches only when evidence demonstrates
that the programme can operate safely and honour its promises. The accountable launch approver
receives one assurance case covering:

1. approved canon and public-claims register;
2. verified parties, signatories, partner and host-site instruments;
3. participant terms, refunds, complaints, safeguarding and accessibility;
4. faculty, coach, assessor and alternate coverage;
5. assessment, certificate and appeal authority;
6. data inventory, notices, roles, transfers, security, retention and incident route;
7. insurance quotations, selected coverage, exclusions and certificates;
8. platform security, restore, portability and low-bandwidth rehearsal;
9. operational calendar and full-month simulation;
10. finance, deposit, cash, tax and stop/go evidence; and
11. open risks, owners, triggers, contingency and explicit residual-risk acceptance.

No single green summary may conceal a failed mandatory gate. Evidence is dated and linked.

Scores are deliberately withheld until risk owners agree probability and impact scales. Invented
numbers create false precision. Current state, triggers and gates remain actionable without them.

## Data-accountability decision

F016 establishes SQHN together with country partners as controllers, but counsel must determine the
purpose-specific allocation and any joint-controller arrangement. Before participant-data
collection:

- name the accountable programme privacy lead and country contacts;
- complete the processing inventory and data-flow map;
- confirm registration/classification and DPO/supervisor duties in each jurisdiction;
- approve notices, lawful routes, rights handling, processor terms and transfers;
- approve a record-class retention schedule and disposal evidence;
- conduct DPIAs where required or prudent; and
- rehearse detection, containment, escalation, notification and recovery.

Base44 remains limited to programme workflow metadata. GitHub remains text-first programme source.
Neither is approved for patient information, clinical records, identity documents, payment-card
data, safeguarding cases, insurance claims or raw research data.

## Insurance decision gate

Q001 closes only through comparable written terms, not a broker estimate. The W17 broker RFQ must
be issued through regulator-verifiable intermediaries for Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan. Each return
must identify insurer, insured parties, territories, activities, limits, aggregate, excess, basis,
exclusions, endorsements, additional-insured route, claims reporting, taxes/fees and expiry.

W04 verifies that the described observership is accurate. W16 reproduces the per-participant cost
at each scenario without treating F025 or F026 as approved. W17 records counsel/broker review and
the accountable risk-acceptance decision. No observership starts before selected coverage is bound
or the CCC approves a legally reviewed alternative.

## Incident and crisis route

Anyone may raise a safety, safeguarding, privacy, integrity or legal concern through one visible
route. The duty officer records minimum necessary facts, protects immediate safety, preserves
evidence, restricts access, notifies the accountable owner and applies the jurisdiction/contract/
insurer clock. Communications use verified facts only. Closure records cause, action, notification,
participant remedy, control change and follow-up test.

## Stop/go rule

The final stop/go date and thresholds remain PROPOSED in F027/F025. Before the CCC can approve them,
the assurance case must map every threshold to authoritative evidence and a pre-agreed consequence.
If any mandatory safety, authority, data, insurance or truthfulness gate fails, money or schedule
pressure cannot convert it to green. Only the correct authority may accept a documented residual
risk, and no authority may waive applicable law.
