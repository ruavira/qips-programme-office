# W17 run 001 — scout record

Date started: 2026-07-28

Stage: SCOUTING

Purpose: establish the evidence and human-input plan for the first governance, legal, risk and data-protection run.

## Canonical questions in scope

- Q001 — What is the per-learner medical indemnity and public-liability premium across Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan? This blocks W16 and W04 and is due 2026-08-21.
- Q009 — What is the data-retention period and who is the data-protection contact? This blocks W09 and W14 and is due 2026-09-30.

## Outputs this run will assemble

1. Decision-rights and governance constitution options.
2. Cross-jurisdiction data-boundary and retention options.
3. An indemnity and insurance request-for-quotation pack suitable for named brokers.
4. A programme risk register with owners, triggers and treatment routes.
5. Participant-facing policy and contracting requirements.

## Evidence plan

- Verify the current primary legislation and regulator guidance for Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.
- Compare the programme's proposed governance against ISQua guidance and established placement-governance models.
- Separate facts available from primary sources from facts that require a broker, lawyer, insurer, host institution or named accountable person.
- Record jurisdiction, effective date, source URL, access date and applicability for every external item.

## Human inputs that cannot be invented

- Insurance quotations and coverage exclusions.
- The contracting legal entity and authorised signatory.
- The named data-protection contact or officer.
- Risk owners and the organisation accepting residual risk.
- Legal advice on final contracts, participant terms and cross-border transfers.

## Exit criteria for scouting

- A verified source register exists for each legal and data-protection lens.
- The broker RFQ and legal-instruction questions are ready for external circulation.
- Governance and retention options can proceed to comparison without asserting unverified legal conclusions.

This is programme-planning material, not legal advice. Final legal instruments require review by appropriately qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdictions.
