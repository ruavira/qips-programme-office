# DRAFT DOSSIER W17-01 — Governance, Legal, Risk and Data Protection

Status: blocked working draft; not on the CCC agenda

## 1. The decision requested

When evidence and professional advice are complete, approve the governance, risk and launch
standard, responsibility schedule and residual-risk authority.

## 2. Why it matters now

W17 blocks W04 and W12. Q001 and Q009 leave the observership, price, data collection and launch
without defensible evidence.

## 3. Canon constraints

F014, F016 and F021 apply. F025, F026 and F027 remain PROPOSED and cannot operate as thresholds.

## 5. Options

- **Policy-library compliance:** produces documents quickly but leaves authority and evidence
  fragmented.
- **Central assurance case with country evidence:** recommended; one launch decision with explicit
  jurisdictional and purpose-specific evidence.
- **Country-independent operations:** maximises local control but fragments the participant journey,
  brand and cross-programme accountability.

## 6. Council verdict

All advisors supported non-averagable mandatory gates and one participant front door. The chairman
adopted the central assurance case without overruling the majority.

## 7. Recommendation

Build the assurance case and responsibility schedule now; return for decision only with counsel,
insurance and authority evidence attached or linked under restricted access.

## 8. Conditions

Country counsel; named accountable contacts; approved data inventory/retention/transfer controls;
comparable insurance terms; signed authorities and agreements; full operational rehearsal.

## 9. Proposed canon if later approved

Exact statements will be derived from approved instruments and appointments. No legal conclusion,
record period or premium is proposed now.

## 10. What it would unblock

Q001 and Q009 as supported; W04, W09, W12, W14 and W16 launch decisions.

## 11. Innovation opportunities

Use an evidence-linked assurance case where every green state expires with its source; keep the
control contract portable across platform and partner replacement.

## 12. Not established

Legal advice, insurance terms, record periods, privacy appointee, delegated authorities, approved
participant terms and residual-risk acceptance.

## 13. Verification

PASS WITH BLOCKING FINDINGS. The control architecture is actionable; the external evidence needed
for a decision remains absent.
