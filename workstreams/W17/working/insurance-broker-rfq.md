# QIPS insurance broker request for quotation

Status: working external-instruction draft; not an insurance contract or coverage opinion

Date: 2026-07-28

## Purpose

Obtain comparable written quotations that answer Q001: the per-participant cost and terms of medical/professional indemnity and public-liability protection for the QIPS observership in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.

No premium, limit, exclusion or coverage assumption may enter the financial model until it is stated in a broker or insurer response and checked against the final observership design.

## Programme facts for underwriting

- QIPS is a 12-month hybrid professional programme.
- Cohort 1 recruits in Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan.
- Each participant completes a 40-hour observership from month 3 to week 2 of month 4.
- Participants attend a qualified host site in their own country.
- The intended activity is observation and programme-directed learning. Participants are not authorised by QIPS to diagnose, treat, prescribe, perform procedures or replace host-site staff.
- Host sites will be qualified against a published Host Site Standard and will name a local supervisor.
- SQHN is the lead sponsor and intended contracting/payment entity. Partner and host-site responsibilities remain subject to written agreements.
- Quote scenarios are 40, 50 and 64 participants. These are underwriting scenarios, not approved cohort-size commitments.

## Quotations requested

Provide a separate quotation for Nigeria, Ghana and Pakistan and, if available, a multi-country programme quotation. For each option show:

1. total premium, taxes, fees and currency;
2. premium per participant at 40, 50 and 64 participants;
3. minimum premium and any adjustment or declaration mechanism;
4. policyholder and every party that may be named or added as an insured;
5. coverage sections, limits, sublimits, aggregate limits and deductibles/excesses;
6. policy period and whether cover can follow a rolling cohort or named placement dates;
7. territorial scope, jurisdiction and governing law;
8. claims-made or occurrence basis, retroactive date and run-off options;
9. incident-notification and claims-reporting deadlines;
10. insurer name, financial-strength information and local licensing/placement route;
11. conditions precedent, required controls and documents;
12. cancellation, participant substitution and refund terms.

## Coverage questions

The broker should state whether each exposure is included, excluded, unnecessary or available only by endorsement:

- professional or medical indemnity arising from an observer's act, omission or alleged clinical involvement;
- public/general liability for bodily injury or property damage at a host site;
- vicarious liability alleged against SQHN, a partner, host site, supervisor or faculty member;
- personal accident, emergency medical expense or evacuation for the participant;
- safeguarding, harassment or abuse allegations;
- privacy, confidentiality and cyber incidents involving participant or host-site information;
- defence costs, regulatory investigations and reputational/notification expenses;
- cross-border virtual teaching and coaching activity;
- faculty, coaches, host-site supervisors and volunteers;
- contractual indemnities in participant and host-site agreements.

## Required endorsements and evidence

Ask the broker to identify whether the following are available and what they cost:

- host sites and named partners as additional insureds where appropriate;
- waiver of subrogation where commercially reasonable;
- primary and non-contributory wording;
- worldwide programme activity with local admitted cover where required;
- certificates of insurance for each host site;
- participant schedule or declaration endorsement;
- extension for innocent breach or failure to disclose by an individual participant.

## Mandatory exclusions disclosure

The response must reproduce or attach the complete exclusions relevant to healthcare, trainees/observers, communicable disease, professional services, cyber/privacy, safeguarding, sanctions, war/terrorism and activities outside the insured's authorised scope. Summaries alone are insufficient.

## Documents available after broker confirmation

- programme concept and 12-month architecture;
- observership specification and Host Site Standard;
- participant eligibility and scope-of-practice declaration;
- draft participant and host-site agreements;
- country and host-site schedule;
- incident, safeguarding and escalation procedures;
- legal names and registration documents of the final contracting parties.
